About the Math and Science Center
By WCMSC at May 26 2009 - 15:46
The Western Colorado Math and Science Center is a non-profit group that runs a hands-on science museum or exploratorium. We are open to the public as well as a popular field trip location. In addition to our exploratorium we do tours of a nearby nature trail on the Colorado River. We offer camps for kids in the summer and often have classes for teachers.
