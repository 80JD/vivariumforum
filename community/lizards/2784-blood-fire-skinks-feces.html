Blood in Fire Skink's Feces?
I have two African fire skinks- a male and a female. I keep them in a 20 long with Jungle Bed substrate, a bark bend to hide in, and a shallow water bowl. They have a Repti-Sun UV-B bulb. I mist them daily and they have an undertank heater on one side of the tank. I feed them crickets every other day and supplement every other feeding. I've tried other foods, but they only ever go for the crickets.

I bought them from a local pet store two months ago. It's the place to go here if you're looking for a less common reptile. The owner said, "Skinks are easy. Crickets and water are all you need." The skinks had been at the store for a while. He kept them on what looked like orchid bark with a basking lamp.

My skinks have good appetites and leave the evidence for me to find every morning. This morning I found feces that looked normal in every way but one. The white part had some reddish pink on it. It wasn't mixed together- there was difinitely a white part and a reddish part but they were part of the same piece. (I hope that makes sense.) It wasn't runny at all. The whole thing appeared to be solid or at least semi-solid.

Is this sometimes normal? Should I make any changes to their set-up/feeding? Do I need to rush my babies to the vet?

i'd say if it continues and especially worsens, get him to a good vet. if it is only pink it may just pass. blood in stools can be bad for any animal. there are also many things that can cause it.

common causes in a viv are plants that can be irritating or toxic, diet especially if the feeders are too big and last but not least the wrong substrate can cut the insides or cause impaction in some reptiles.

i am not really familiar with skinks. i could look up a couple care sheets for you. you could also post pics and maybe get better answers that way.
