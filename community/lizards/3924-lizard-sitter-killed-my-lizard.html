Lizard-sitter killed my lizard!
Sorry, long post, i just need some advice!

I had a healthy pet water dragon, his name was Friedrich and i had him for 4 years with absolutely no health problems. ( not even snout rub!)

A very close friend of mine, a trustworthy friend offered to care for him while i was doing some home renovations. He had minimal experience with reptiles but i personally believe that theyre easy to care for. I showed him how to properly handle the animal but explained that he should just be left in peace for the week.

The tank, supplies, food, and lights were brought over to his place and put in his living room. Friedrich adapted well, i know this because he was actively eating fruit within 30 mins of being moved.

Within not even 24 hours, i got a call from my friend, asking what the lizard looked like when he was sleeping. After my explanation, he asked if i could drop by his place and take a look at Friedrich.

When i arrived at his place, i found out that my friend actually "had went to the store": and told me i could go inside.

I remember that horrible feeling when i looked into the lifeless eyes of my pet, whose personality ive grown to love over 4 years.

His back was shattered, his hips were severely bruised and had died in an agonized position, curled sideways.

I called my friend, in tears, to ask what happened, and after no clear answer, i took Friedrich home to be buried under my lilac bush.

Im getting choked up just writing this.

My friend is now ignoring me, ignoring my calls and text messages and this has been going on for 5 days.

What should i do? I want to be reimbursed because this is 4 years and hundreds of dollars down the drain! Any thoughts?

most lawyers will do a free consult, where you can learn what your rights are and what courses of action you can persue. you won't have to retain the lawyer, they aren't required in small claims court. verbal agreements are legal and binding in most states, and the fact that he had the animal at his house is evidence that there was some sort of agreement.

try to talk to your friend and see what comes of it. if he admits what happened, see if he is willing to pay you out of court, at least the value of a comparable dragon. if he is, make him sign a contract. if not, take him to court. in either case, after you talk to him, try to get him to email back and forth with you in case you take it to court, since emails will provide concrete evidence to a judge.

because this is an exotic pet and not a cat or dog, it is more likely that the animal will be considered persobal property in court. if someone borrowed, say, your plasma television, and it became broken while in their possession, they are liable, whether THEY broke it or not, to replace the item or reimburse you for the value of the item. if the dragon is considered personal property in your state, the example will apply. even cats and dogs are considered personal property in some states.

it may seem like a hassle, but you deserve some sort of compensation, both for what you've gone through and what you poor dragon went through. he may have been "just a lizard", but he had a life, and he should have been able to live it. not only that, he was also a valuable animal that you have spent alot of time and money on. don't let anyone convince you that you should just get over it because it was "only a lizard".
