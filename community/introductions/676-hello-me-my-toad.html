hello from me and my toad!
greetings,

just joined up and wanted to say hello, i have an american toad in a 10g vivarium, soil substrate, two ivy plants, and some moss, it isn't exotic, but i enjoy that, it's a unique backyard vivarium that even has grass, very simple and easy to take care of, no extra special humidity or lighting needs, i plan to move up to a 20g or more and came here for ideas and help on my next setup

glad to be here and thanks for looking!

thanks for the replies!

i actually live in upper michigan and that is where the toad is from (eastern american toad), i also grew up with these guys all around since my house was right on the shore of lake michigan, they were very bountiful and always had to be rescued from the evil lawnmower of doom their calls fill the air on nice spring evenings and i love it

toads sure are gluttons! i am very careful how much i feed since they will just keep eating until sickness or health problems occur, i don't want any impaction, i feed a variety of crickets, mealworms, butterworms, and even earthworms if i happen to find one outside on a rainy day

i think toads are underrated because they are plain/common, but are so easy to tame, and just a neat amphibian overall, mine will eat a worm right out of my hand, and let me gently spray her down in her pool on hot days
