terra... basically just for plants, although sometimes referred to as the cage itself or a just animal enclosure too.

viva... holds land animals and plants

paluda... viv with a fishy section

ripa... fish tank with a river bank theme where plants grow above the water out of the back

sola... contains plants and/or animals yet utilizes natural sunlight through an opening at the top (like a skylight) these usually hold plants regardless animals or not

aqua... well, usually has water and aquatic life (obvious, i know) also a type of cage or a glass box

rium... you'll need to add that to the end of each word to make it complete (obvious, i know, lol)

did i miss any?
