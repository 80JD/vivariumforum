i'd cover the dirt w/moss

when we say floro's don't make heat, it is merely an in comparison ordeal. noone has really figured out how to make light without at least minimal heat!

plants do not need uvb... herps do... mostly just diurnals (awake during the day)... it doesn't hurt though as long as you don't use cfl's for uvb!

if nothing will be directly exposed to (plants, animals, water touching) the back glass, a uth will provide great day and night heat and it should be hidden by your background.

if possible best setup is to have low watt ceramic heat (light socket style). any light that says daylight/sunlight/growlight or shows a color of 5000k-6500k will do fine (cfl or flourescent is best).

you do not need a night light as long as you can sustain optimal night temps! while they may be more comforting to some pets... ???... idk, they are really purely for aesthetics and night viewing!

as for fixtures... if the tank is a front opening... the manufacturer should make some that go pretty well with your tank. you can always build your own hood to fit your tank/needs/fancy or just go with clamp lamps or a desk/table fixture... it's really just a matter of what fits your location/needs.

i agree with, mh32 epiphytes are awesome and can really make a viv . also, you can just gs in/attach small pots to bg for more options.
