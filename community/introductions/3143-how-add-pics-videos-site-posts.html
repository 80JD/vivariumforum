how to add pics/videos to site/posts
1) log on

2) click on gallery




3) click upload




4) click browse

5) choose desired pic from your pc

6) click open

7) repeat steps 4-7 as necessary until all desired pics are listed

8) click upload/submit




9) enter titles/text and organize as you see fit

10) click proceed

your pics should show up on the new page

11) click on pic you would like to post




12) copy img code provided

13) paste it into your post

14) save




if you choose the top link it will post thumbnails... they are below for reference... the other link will appear like the pics above

to add pics directly to your post from your pc...
  1. click go advanced
  2. click the paper clip
  3. click choose file
  4. find the file on your pc and click open/save button
  5. repeat up to five pics total per post
  6. when you're done click upload
  7. click close this window
  8. place cursor where you'd like your pic on your page
  9. click the paperclip
  10. click on preferred pic or attach all
  11. done

EDIT: this will result in a click-able thumbnail like the first pic in my next post

to post videos directly into your post
  1. go to your video and check the address bar.
    blend crafters.jpg
  2. any LETTERS OR NUMBERS after "=" copy.
  3. go back to this site and type in [ YT] (for youtube of course ) without spaces.
  4. then paste (control and "V" works well).
  5. then end it (close it out) with [/YT ] without spaces.
  6. MAKE SURE THERE ARE NO SPACES ANYWHERE OR IT WILL NOT WORK.

ok, here's what i do:
-upload your pics from your computer into imageshack doing the resize option of 640x480.
-then copy the "direct link" url provided by imageshack (under "image details" button, then click "links to share this image" on the right).
-hit the "insert image" button on the toolbar in vivforums (the little button that looks like a mountain with a sun).
-paste the "direct link" url copied from imageshack into the pop-up box & click "ok".
-walah!
