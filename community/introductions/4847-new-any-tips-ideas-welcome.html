If the measurements in your drawing are correct, you've got a 20 gallon tank, which should afford you a bit more room to work with.

You can use anything to keep your substrate out of your drainage layer- it just has to hold up over time, keep the substrate from falling through and allow water to pass freely through it. A lot of smaller hardware stores do have this available by the foot- call around and I'm sure you can find something suitable.
