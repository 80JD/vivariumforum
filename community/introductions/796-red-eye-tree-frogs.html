To answer a few of your questions -

No, you don't need an under-tank heater, as this serves no purpose. Red-eyes are arboreal (tree-dwelling) frogs, so they won't be on the ground much anyway.

Yes, you ought to have a hygrometer. (the thing that measures humidity)
I use a Flukers brand thermometer/hygrometer. It measures temp and humidity, it's small, and can be purchased at most pet stores. (I got mine from PetSmart)

A spray bottle is fine. I'd suggest misting at least twice daily. If you won't be there frequently enough to keep the terrarium moist, then an automatic mister might be good.

As for plants, bromeliads are great for vivariums. They're extremely easy to care for and they look great. Frogs like to perch on them sometimes too.
