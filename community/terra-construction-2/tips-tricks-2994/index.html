Just make sure it is thoroughly washed first.

Lets see...

Moss collected from tree branches can be super glued to a branch in your vive to make a fast, natural looking branch.

Thermometer/hygrometer combos should be taped along the seams with electrical tape to avoid damage from misting. They can also be mounted to the front glass, facing out, with double sided foam tape. This allows for a better view of the screen, as well as a better humidity reading.

Soaking and then baking wood is a great way to sterilize it, soaking helps keep it from catching fire in the oven, and also "boils" to kill any bugs or germs. This is faster and easier than boiling or boiling then baking, and does both jobs at once.

Check the free section of Craigslist regularly. You NEVER know what great stuff someone is giving away.

Ok, I have decided to ignore mention of Satan herself and go ahead and stick this thread.

Now, I thought I had posted this already, but here goes:

You can use a credit card or shopping card or whatever with the corner cut off to smooth your silicone better. It's alot less messy, and you can actually save silicone because you can lay down a smaller bead and smooth it into the corner better than a larger bead you smooth with your finger. There is also a ready made tool you can buy(I've seen it on TV) that has lots of different shaped and sized edges. Maybe look it up and cut a few cards to the different shapes on the tool and keep them with your supplies for future use.

When using Great Stuff be sure to do a single layer and let cure completely. If you pile too much up you will notice it skins over trapping a liquid resin. this will find it's way out eventually in the form of foam and most likely ruin something. I think this "resin escape" can also be the cause of the foamy bubbles some people have encountered in water features.
Also, allow extra time for great stuff to cure because I'm pretty sure it's toxic if not fully cured.
