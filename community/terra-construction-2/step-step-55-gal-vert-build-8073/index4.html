I used 2 parts black silicone ( only use 100% silicone GE 2) 1 part mineral spirits ( or you can use toluene), and 1 part dried peat moss. Mix the silicone and mineral spirits first, then ad the peat. Then covered the nylon rope with it, and lade it on a pan of dried peat and dumped more peat on top to give it a good coating.

I just checked my vines, and there is no smell at all!!!
