sounds like a nutrient deficiency or wet/strangled/cold roots. yellow is a tough one without a good description/pics. however... transparent certainly sounds like magnesium deficiency to me. if the roots are strangled/wet then giving them a little more air should help alleviate the other symptoms, but i think unless they're actually drowning then nutrients would be the root of the issue.

are you noticing it more on newer/older/all leafs?

detailed pictorial and description, please.
