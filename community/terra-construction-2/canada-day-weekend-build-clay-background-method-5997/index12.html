I can draw something up, or find it online but it would be a small section of tubing with a larger diameter than the normal tubing, with some type of filtering media in it, which the water passes through.
The same concept as a water filter on a fridge or a fuel filter on a cars gas line.

Make the tube a few inches long, pack it with the foam media, put some barbed connectors on the end for your smaller tubing to slip over and done! When the media gets dirty, just unplug the tubing and rinse/replace the media in the tube.
