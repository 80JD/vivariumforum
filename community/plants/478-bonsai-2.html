Hey its a forum...the thread belongs to no one...the picture of your tank is kind of deceptive as to the size of your tank but I would assume its around a 30 gal...IMO regular sized plants would throw off the proportions to the tank so I would look for some minturized plants...Black Jungle has some I believe

Also be careful of leaving the ficus in their original pots for too long...over time the roots will grow into one massive ball and become root bound and acutally strangle itself...don't know how into bonsai's you are but when you train a tree/plant for a smaller pot you have to do periodic (sp?) root trimmings...just a heads up...good luck
