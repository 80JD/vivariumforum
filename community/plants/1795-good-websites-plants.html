It depends on what types of plants you're planning on getting & what kind of herps you have. Keep in mind that many herps and amphs will try to clamber over plants and even dig them up.

With larger herps, you plant selection is actually quite limited in many respects. Plants strong enough to have a large herp climb all over it or sit on it without getting flattened or broken are generally big plants -- often too big to be taken care of in a viv. Small to tiny herps and amphs like pdfs and day geckos provide a much wider range of options.

Btw, can't remember where at the moment, but I do remember chatting with some folks who were quite unhappy w/ the quality of the plants they received from Black Jungle. I can't speak from personal experience, however, as I have never ordered from them.

For durable vine plants like Pothos or perhaps Hoyas, cheapest place to go is your local big box stores or perhaps farmers market. (I would recommend staying away from ivies as, IME, they seem to be extrememly prone to mites. The mites would be harmless to your critters, but can spread to any other houseplants you have.) Occasionally you'll find folks, such as myself, who sell off or trade plant divisions -- at which time the biggest expense winds up being the postage not the plant(s).

I think I've only purchased one of my critters from a pet store -- an that was a local "mom & pop" operation. All the others I've picked up at herp shows. MUCH cheaper than buying them online or at a store. Plus you can look them over (impossible on line) and talk to the breeder before buying them.
