haha. I have no idea how i did. LOL. I think its the lighting. I have a Coralife Aqualight T5 Fluorescent Light Bulbs for Freshwater. Its a dual light fixture. One bulb says "Coralife Colormax F18-T5-BP", and the other is "18Watt-T5-MP 6700K". I like the really slim look of it.

All of the wood is siliconed in/down. I did boil it all for 15 minutes. And then I soaked it for over a week, changing the water as soon as it turned brown. Once the water stopped turning I boiled it again. I was gonna bake it too but thought it was going to be overkill. I have done a 10 gallon fresh water fish tank before where I just dropped the wood in. Took it about 3 hours to turn my water deep brown. lol
I think it may be just sand and substrate dust. Its been getting clearer, I think. If its the wood I may just have to do a bunch of 50% water changes over time to get it to stop.
Mossies are native to river beds so a little cloudy water isn't going to be bad. I would like it to be clear just for the sake of my water plants getting the adequate light to grow once I put them in.
