New to the site wanted to show off some work of mine.
So i have been building paludariums for about 2 yrs with great success. Here are some pics. i also built an entire top wardian case for my 40gal breeder. I will be starting a new palu soon out of a standard 30gal with a newly desgined top.

So here are some past and present pics of my 40gal breeder with a 40 gal top.
Here is a list of the inhabitants.
1 crested gecko
1 Skunk Gecko
1 Crocidile Skink
2 Grass Lizards
1 Big Eyed tree frog
1 Northern Tree Frog
2 African Clawed frogs
all inhabitants have lived in this setup for over a 1yr without incident.
I did have 2 redeyed tree frogs. 1 was eaten by the clawed frog and the other was found dead with no injury. so i have resisted adding any more of them at the moment.

The tank includes a monsoon system as well as zoo med 511 i had an eheim external pump but the zoo med works better with the rain bar for this setup. Also all the plants and background is real. the only foam i used in construction is at the top of the background. There is multiple plants as well as numerous air plants. let me know what you guess think.
