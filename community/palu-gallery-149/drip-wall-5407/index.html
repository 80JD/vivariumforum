just plants? would it need to be removable? what are we talking here? i'm assuming the wall would have to be waterproofed so the wooden slats that hold the plaster in place don't go crazy with mold behind the wall. i'm thinking you're talking an inside wall of an apartment?

you could take a small piece of pond liner and sew it to the ecoweb, then cut it to size leaving a 3-4" flap extra on the top. put a thin piece of trim board behind it and fold it over so the flap disappears over and behind it. put that up against the wall, lift the ecoweb sewn end and rest it on your head while you nail the trim and extra flap of rubber to the wall.

of course i'm assuming you want to save the wall. at that point you could easily silicone the rest to the wall and the silicone will hold it in place since it will have a propensity to lean towards the wall anyway and the weight will be suspended. in most cases silicone on a plaster wall won't do any damage a little paint won't cure. anything more and spackle should fix it...

assuming you'll have a reservoir underneath it you might not want it against the wall at that point anyway.

why not just make a simple shelf? you could make one with the acrylic. some mdf painted gloss black would look good too.

if you didn't want to do that then, yeah you could add a couple tabs to the thing. small ones just above the screw holes with all angles pointing forward would keep the water moving away from the wall. you wouldn't even have to cover the screw holes most likely... just up to them. if you can cut 1/8-1/4" slivers with a straight edge at one end and at least a 45* angle at the other end, connect them to the face and top piece and then set them into place it should work out well. i would make the face pieces at least 3/4" wide so water doesn't eventually flow in there.

look up invisible book shelf. i think you could easily do something like that. i don't know how you have the thing set up but a little silicone could be used as a suction cup to hold the top to the shelf with hidden hardware behind it. you'd just have to make sure the pattern you apply it in is one that overlaps and makes pockets, then press it into place.

basically you'd make like an aquarium... 4 sides and a bottom. this way your bottom would be your face/front. the front and back would be top and bottom leaving the open top to go against the wall and your "L" brackets will be upside down bolted flush on the top (where the bolt heads will be covered by the fountain) and the nuts and possibly washers inside. cut notches in the top where the brackets will come out so you can mount the whole thing flush to the wall. now that you have that whole assembly put together you'll screw it into the wall, apply the silicone in overlapping "S" patterns to make your suction cup pockets and put the fountain on top pressing firmly down on it and up on the shelf itself. putting a couple soft rags and some clamps on it overnight at least would be a good idea also.

i think it would look sharp, seamless and give you at least very close to the look you're going for.
