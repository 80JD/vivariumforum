I don't do either of those two things (diving or climbing) personally, but I would imagine that you could find people doing both here- southern Ontario's not really mountainous per se, but the Niagara Escarpment would probably provide a worthy face or two, and amidst a lovely bit of scenery to boot. I know that quite a few people dive the lakes here- some are deep, some shallow, but there are some interesting things to be seen; shipwrecks and the like.

Toronto's not too bad a city either, all told- there's something for everyone here, I guess. I suppose I'm involved with the art/music scene here, and there's certainly stuff like that happening all the time. One complaint might be that the rents are ridiculous here, but we manage to make it through somehow, even the poverty-ridden such as myself.

Temperature-wise, Toronto's at the top end of what's known as the Snow Belt, and I bet you can guess why. We get dumped on quite a bit in the winter, but it's not often too unbearably cold- I'd be lying if I said that it didn't go down to -5/-10 F from time to time. The weather's often quite humid, making the summers a bit less than comfortable (the plants love it, though), and it usually hovers around 90 at worst, though sometimes we'll get a heat wave past that. I guess I'm not selling the place too well, but there you have it.

So how do you plan to go about getting over here? You'd better not ask me for a date with my sister, ha ha.

As a proud Canadian I would say make the move. I live in Manitoba so I would have a tough time answering any Ontario specific questions. Though you have to realize that Ontario is huge, Northwestern Ontario is very very different from Southern Ontario. I assume that you would be looking at Southern Ontario near Toronto. As I understand it they have pretty mild winters compared with us (two months of -30 or colder). As for SCUBA I am sure you could dive in the lakes around there in the summer without a wetsuit, it wouldn't exactly be Carribean diving but still you could do it. Taxes are higher but you get more for them. Universal Health care is a beautiful thing. Good luck in your decision making.
