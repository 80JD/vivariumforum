Pachypodium is an awesome plant! but i think you would be better off buying one for $2 from home depot. they get huge! there is one at the Conservatory of Flowers in San Fransisco that is well over 12 feet tall. the large spines make it a fairly dangerous plant. i grow both P. greayi and P. lamerei, if you have the space they are a great addition. the seed does not store well, so i am surprised to see it sold in a kit. the entire plant is toxic, but the spines deture most would be snackers. many care sheets say they will handle full sun, ime they prefer afternoon shade and dont like to get too dry. they can handle extreme heat as long a they arent roasting in the sun. in more humid climates they can probably handle more sun, but im not sure. still, its a great plant to grow!
