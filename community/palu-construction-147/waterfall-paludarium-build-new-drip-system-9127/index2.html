So i was getting harassed by my friends that i have nothing in my palu so one of my buddies gave me some toy dinosaurs to put in it, so when people ask i can say i have dinosaurs. lol Since i originally made this thread i did get some critters to put in it. I have 2 orange galactonotus dart frogs (male and female), 4 cherry shrimp and a nerite snail. So your suggestion of small invertebrates was right on. They just rummage around the bottom eating my excess of alge.

Here is a pic of my male frog:

they usually stay on the back wall, under the canopy of plants


it has grown a lot since i have last posted i will get an updated pic up too


You do raise some good points about humidity. Heat and humidity have been my biggest struggle with this tank. I now have a vent fan on my hood that wicks away the heat from my lights I notice it does pull some air with it so the humidity will drop to about 80% when its on. When i seal the top vents the palu will fog right up, so it must go up through the top. Either way i do water changes once a week so it doesn't get out of hand.
