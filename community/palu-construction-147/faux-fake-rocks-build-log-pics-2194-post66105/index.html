from Curing concrete – Dendroboard | All About Indoor/Outdoor Speakers
Quote:
Yes unfortunately the grout should be cured as well. it is basically a concrete product and because the tank environment is very moist and humid, your grout / concrete will continue to hydrate or cure for a period of time, whether you have a water feature or not.

When cement / concrete is “curing” it is actually absorbing water molecules, ie. hydrating. After about 28 days in a moist environment it will be mostly finished hydrating or curing (cement actually keeps hydrating VERY slowly for an indefinite period). As concrete and grout hydrates they release calcium oxide and calcium hydroxide which are two forms of kalkwasser. The leaching out of these by products are the main cause of the high pH swings which will end up overdosing your tank which is likely to kill your plants and animals. The kalkwasser will leach out whether you have a water feature or not.

Sorry that is not what you want to hear, but knowing should prevent you from losing plants and animals. That is if you properly cure the grout.

All the vinegar does is lower the pH on the grout’s surface, it does not stop the curing, speed up the curing, or eliminate the need for curing.

I am new here and been doing a lot of reading on all subject's of building and making a cage. I see lot's of people asking what to use to seal there build's. a guy from Lizard Landscapes uses a product called Sheild's All for his rock features in a moist area and anything under water he rub's it all down with Silicone to seal it. Only thing it leave's the area shiny.... When he uses the Shield's All on the last coat he sprinkle's sand on area's to help dull it back and also for traction and grip for your animal's. Im building a case for a Chameleon but have not posted it yet. Im not sure if Im going to use a product called Dryloc to go over the foam to seal it then maybe paint with Acrylic then seal again with the Shield's All to seal again. My case is all wood so i have to be careful of leak's. Im wanting to use live plant's and water feature but am leaning towards just a mist system and just a clean painted surface on the floor for easy clean up. I have installed a drain and used a membrane in the floor using thinset ultra flex over the membrane. I will probably use a cool mist fogger to keep the humidity up. I see people going both way's on screened or glass for the Chameleon.I figure it will be tough to keep the temp and humidity up in the summer with the AC going so that is why Im not going with a whole screened case. I cut the top out and it will get screen and the door glass was broke from my nephew's playing. it's a shame the case is an old China cabinet with curved glass. The door glass a lone to replace is about 180 buck's.So it will get a piece of salvaged glass from the original piece that was broke which I got 14 inch of it that will go at the top and the rest will be The thin perforated ornamental metal you see in screened doors at the bottom to keep your pet from going through the door. It will bend easy to the shape I need. I will start a thread on the build soon. Sorry to hijack the thread ,but hope the info on sealing will help.
