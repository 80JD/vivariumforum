Quote:
Originally Posted by froggsong
You can plumb these straight from the tap if you have it available, which will allow you to use your water pressure to power the mist.
This is the trouble with trying to do this in a more closed system, I think. An 100GPH pump is probably not even enough to squeeze a drop from those nozzles (I've tried with a stronger submersible pump). I don't know if there's a conversion or relationship somehow between GPH and PSI, but you need a minimum of the latter to run any misting nozzle. With a submersible pump you'll also lose pressure through head height, etc. as well.

Misting systems are expensive because they have to be- there's no way around it or some enterprising hobbyist would already have thought of it. I think that what you get for your money is well worth it, personally. Try shopping around for the individual components to any popular vivarium misting system- you won't find a better price, I don't think.

i do not disagree. however, different strokes for different folks.

for example... mixing dart frogs (not just any frogs). there are some great arguments against it by some very knowledgeable, educated, informed, experienced folks. i have to agree with them. but if someone wants to individually pay for, treat, test, swab, do fecals, etc. and then have all that money wasted when they all die or one eats another... or even worse they mate and you are now overrun with worthless tads you may have to euth... it's their bad decision to make.

then again with testing, swabbing, experience, quarantining, careful consideration, etc., NOBODY is going to tell me i can't put MY animals together if i choose to do so.

not yelling... just expressing points nobody takes into consideration often.

not exactly the same thing though as i'm sure someone can and i know someone has constructed something cheaper that will and does work.

i just think folks should consider the down side first and decide if they can live with it. for example if the system is in a basement/laundry room with a floor drain and their enclosure will allow water to leak out (say in an exo terra) before it fills up or covers all available land and they want to plumb it into the house it's their decision.

now just adding a pump from a garden sprayer will have other issues. eventually the pump will stop holding it's pressure or blow out and not pump any pressure. one would obviously have to replace them periodically. although the idea seemed sound this will bring the cost over the long run up to more than what a misting system can be purchased for.

so there's an up and a down no matter how it's done...
