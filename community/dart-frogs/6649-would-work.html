Seems mixing morphs is a delicate process. Cross breeding is a major concern. 10 gal is small for housing multiple frogs of same morph, let alone mixed morphs.
If you must mix morphs, any eggs, tads, froglets in the future must be given away/sold as mixed morphs. Problem is, will the buyer be honest enough to give away/sell potential mixed morphs as such?
The Dart Frog Keeper hobbyists need to steal an "AKC" of dog world and impose it into some sort of Dart Frog Pure Breed registry. To my knowledge, it doesn't exist. It is very important given the fact that Dart Frog natural habitat is being decimated!
