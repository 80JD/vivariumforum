I need someone with experience to help me out to see if I’m in the right track.

So far my plan is the following:

1.Have the tank made out sort of like the following:

The box at the right is the one I’m keeping the frogs right now.

3.
__a. you'll need a mister pump... misting needs to be done under pressure regular pumps can't duplicate.
__b. 2 minutes a day will likely be a bit much
__c. how many tubes is that? you may need more... 100% silicone should work fine
__d. please elaborate on "cement based coating" it sounds fine... i'm using latex based cement patcher for my crabitat... i would still check the label, msds and possibly with the company if things don't sound right
__e. styrofoam is fine... just don't shape it outside on a windy day.

4
__a. from what i understand epiweb is just recycled plastic spun into a web like mat or block. for that you could just use a sump filter block for a fish sump filter... or several. there is a small mom and pop web based business near me that still sells and epiweb equivalent... eco web. there's a bunch of other things you could use for a substrate also. organic soil, peat, coco fiber, leca, etc. all come to mind.
__b. i would do round rocks... or leca
__c. why black foam?
__d. i would go with 2 t-8 or thinner bulbs full length of the viv for your plants.

don't forget access to your pumps. most cork will break down fairly quick if left wet. corkbark is an exception to this rule.

follow the threads... 3 of them... and the links i provide... should help with your walls... Blue/Pink foam board

this might help you, idk? ... good places to buy... please add to it!

also check out the builds on blackjungle.com, my favorite link in my signature and the rest of the stickies on this site.

welcome to the site btw.

2 tubes might be more than enough depending on your final design.
i think the cement will PROBABLY be fine too. i live in america though so i don't want to assume what's over there.
if you're doing insulation foam boards or styrofoam you should be able to cover and secure them quite easily using concrete and siliconing it to the glass in the back.
if you can find epiweb or get firstrays to ship you ecoweb without breaking the bank by all means follow Marty's build to the "T". i'm not so sure of your possibilities there though.
did i mention fiberglass replacement screen? that's a good divider between your soil and your drainage layer.
you could probably find coir rolls somewhere in your area too. they'd make for a decent background cover you could plant in.

a lot of people around here make false bottoms too. that's another option if you'd like. there's also the coco fiber background method. not my favorite but it looks great just like the others. gives it a natural woodsy feel and almost seems to add depth.

hth, beck

p.s. don't forget to look into those links... there's helpful info in there.
