The cloth pad on your bio balls would be considered mechanical filtration. Basically it catches the larger particles (solid impurities). It was probably just filter fiber, it's very inexpensive and can be found at any pet supply store.

Your chemical filtration would be something like charcoal or activated carbon. This is especially important with animals who spend a lot of time in the water more importantly animals that defecate in the water because it absorbs harmful chemicals (liquid impurities).

That being said, from what I know, not many PDF's spend a lot of time in the water so putting a lot of time and money into filtration could be overkill.
