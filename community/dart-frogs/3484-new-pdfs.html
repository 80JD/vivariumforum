If your going to go with azureus, you may want to go with a tank with a larger footprint. They are more terrestrial frogs. That 12x12x18 exo would be good for a pair or trio of thumbnails. Maybe a 20 long, 20 high, or maybe a 29 gal would work for a pair of azureus. The exo inly has a 12x12 footprint vs the 24x12 of a 20H.
