10 minutes is a suggested minimum soaking time to kill many things regardless what sterilization form is used. it's not necessary for all things, but for some. when diluted times can vary.

options for the stone:
topical treatment for sterilization such as bleach or a biocide or potassium permanganate
power wash
rinse and scrub
wash with mild soap
boil and cool down in the water
sechem excel can be used as a biocide treatment

options for leaf litter:
boil
soak and heat in oven (keep close attention, obviously)
excel and rinse
pp dip and rinse
co2

if you use chemicals at all use the old adage... rinse, rinse, rinse... and do it again.
if you use anything chlorine based definitely dose it 5x's recommended strength with a dechlorinator.
if you boil or bake the rock (baking would not be suggested) be sure to cool it down slowly to lower your chances of shattering.
if you heat the leafs in the oven... don't walk away and risk starting a fire.
