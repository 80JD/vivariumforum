Help!!! Am I freaking out for no reason???
I went to feed my dart froglet this morning like i always do every morning...I noticed his water dish was low on water...so i grabbed my hand mister and started spraying water into the dish which the froglet was resting on the side of...when he didn't move when i started spray i began to freak out...so i stopped and went to see if he would move when i put my finger near him...he didn't move, which made me freak out even more...so i tried to pick him up...got him on my finger...this is where i really started to lose it...finally he jumped off my finger and back into his container...but now he really isn't moving...he is still in the same place where he landed...i thought he would want to hunt the fruit flies but he hasn't...he is just sitting there...my question being, is my dart okay and i am freaking out for no reason...or is there something really wrong and i need to do something...please hurry and let me know something asap...I am freaking out and am on the edge of my seat...

UPDATE: I just checked on him and he is still the same...i took one of my terrarium lights and put on top of his sterlite container so i can get a better look...i noticed that a fruit fly was crawling on him and he didn't even seem to care...i don't know what is going on...it seems like he don't have an appetite...I am so worried...

predators predate... eating.

meters are important one way or another. even snake keepers keep them... even if it's just in the room as opposed to each setup. plus he's new to you...

what is he taking as far as food... how much?

the meters that come with the exo-terras cannot be relied upon solely.

he may be settling in to his new environment, startled... or just not hungry. it happens. take it easy and keep us as informed as you have the ability to.
