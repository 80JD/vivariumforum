Morphs are variations of the same species of frog. (they can and will breed)The reason there are morphs is because they are from separate isolated areas that contain/trap the type of morph from its cousins,usually geographically.

You have obviously seen how passionate some people can be against mixing . Your innocent question is the crux of what most of the dart frog community is afraid of. "ohh I like those yellow frogs and I like those blue aswell"... The end result is baby frogs that just don't exist in the wild. Unlike the hybridizing in reptile community we would like to keep the frog lines pure. I know that sounds a bit simplistic, but I hope that answers your question a little better than "Don't Do It"

No one is going to stop you if you want to go about mixing your animals. However it goes against pretty much everyones way of thinking and you will probably end up getting flammed for it haha

If you have no experience with either morphs seperately, then how are you to ever know there is a problem with them together? Typically when mixing is experimented with, the owner has a deep understanding on each specific animal's tendencies and daily patterns. Once housed together (in a LARGE enclosure), observations can be made to determine if either of the animals is suffering because of it.

Why do you want to mix anyways? Both morphs will look just as great in seperate tanks, plus instead of having 2 different ones, you can get a pair and start breeding them
