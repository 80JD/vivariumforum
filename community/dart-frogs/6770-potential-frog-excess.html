I have researched to the extreme!!! I bought frogs from Saurian.net. They are breeding like crazy! Of course, I would like to get the $$$$ I put into them but it is definitely not a money maker for me; not yet?!?!?. I truly believe there is no better "pet" than the dart frog. I don't have any frogs yet, just tadpoles and eggs (be sides the original sub adult juvenile frogs I bought). I'm sure there are many people with tads and eggs to sell (once they morph to frogs).
I think my goal is to bring about knowledge of these wonderful jewels of the natural world and bring their accomplishments to be better understood by people in the general population. Many people keep aquarium fish, terrestrial lizards and snakes, and many other reptiles and amphibians. The dart frog is a true gift of the natural world; excellent pet, possible medicinal value and valuable biological educational tool ( total life cycle from egg, tadpole, easy food supply (fruit fly cultivation), growth and breeding. The slice of the rain forest in a near complete ecosystem nearly closed system in a "fish tank".
I'm rambling but I have kept a few different exotic pets... none to which have captivated myself and my entire family as much as the dart frog.
If only we could all live with the realization of the simplicity of satisfaction from raising a tropical amphibian!
