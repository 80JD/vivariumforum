I'm pretty sure they're incompatible species. If I'm not mistaken, Leucomelas are larger than Azeurus and that in itself would cause a problem.

PDFs don't require that much space, given their size. It depends on how many you want to keep. Also a big factor is that the more you keep, the more space as PDFs can be territorial. I'd say a 44Gal hex could hold maybe 4-6 Leucomels, 6-8 Azeurus. The 72 bow could probably hold 6-10 Leucomels and 10-14 Azeurus.

If I'm way off someone tell me lol.

If you wanted both, I would keep the Azuerus in the 44 and the Leucs in the 72. But that's just me

Rollei is correct about the Azures. As youngsters they will be fine, but down the road you will probably run into wrestling. The luecs will be the most social of the two. If your are interested in a large number of frogs I would use the bow front. The luecs while primarily terrestrial will use the whole tank, they will aggressively climb every where when they are young, not so much as they get older. I would say 10 -15 frogs are acceptable for the 72 gal.

However, It sounds as if you are just starting out with PDF. From the looks of your signature I think you will take to Pdfs well, its really less complicated and less work than the aquariums.

My advice is to use the 44 hex, with 3 luecs, Start slow. Decide if the darts are going to be a good addition to your home. It will be better for all especially the frogs, if you learn about their needs and behaviors from a smaller tank and a smaller group. I don't suggest a large group of frogs just starting out , it can breed complications that may be missed by novice and experienced keepers alike.

Compatibility will be an issue, I'm afraid mixing will be a problem for all involved.
