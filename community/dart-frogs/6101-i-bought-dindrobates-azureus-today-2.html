Wow..

You will be making a new culture every week/week and a half. A culture takes about 2 week to mature and produce enough flies for feeding. As a culture gets older production slows down. You don't want to keep your cultures over a month because mites become a serious possibility. Mites are bad because the will out compete your flies and kill the culture. The mites spread like mad and will contaminate any and all cultures.

You will be using 50-100 flies to start a new culture
(about 2 weeks before you use this culture)

This way you stagger out your production so you don't run out.

I haven't seen any mention of supplements .. you are using them right ?

Also a note on feeding// I would feed a smaller amount twice a day I find it works better than just one feeding when they are little.
