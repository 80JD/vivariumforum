I have 3 leucs... got lucky 2 males 1 female (from surian.net). They are bold calling frogs. I've got them in 29 gal viv w/ 3 broms, prayer plant, philodendrom, java moss, wondering jew, some other plant (I think a form of pathos plant) drift wood, 2 coco huts with egg laying lid underneath, sprouting saphgnum moss and some sort of hijacker fern. Humidity is kept high ( daily hand misting, condensation on the glass so thick, have to squeege it to see in tank). Feed dusted fruit flies every other day ( try for daily, sometimes doesn't get done) Temps range from 70 degress to about 78 degrees ( wood stove in same room causes variation)
They are active however, they have been hiding more recently...
Female is laying eggs every 3 weeks (approximately).
I think once your frogs adapted to their environment, they will be more visisble. Give it time...

Going out on a limb here... definitely not the popular "spoken" way of housing... If you keep mixed morph frogs, be sure not to sell any frogs as pure breed. It really is important to keep "pure" blood lines. If you keep or bought from mixed morph suppliers, please advertise as such.
I keep one tank of Leucs and Auratus together. If I were to sell any possible mixed morph offspring, I would advertise as such.
After this statement, I'll expect some bashing on this forum. For all the "bashers", be sure I would not advertise any frogs as "pure" bloodlines if they are not.
