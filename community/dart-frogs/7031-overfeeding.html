Overfeeding??
I have recently purchased some green sip tincs and i just have a quick question about feeding them. I have had them for a few months now and they have always had a good appetite. I am just wondering if I am overfeeding them. I have read that they are opportunistic when it comes to feeding, but what is an average number of fruit flies that should be fed per frog. I have been told that about ten flies per frog and then if they still act hungry add more. My little guys will practically eat until you stop feeding them. When I first got them I would feed them about 15 flies per frog and then add a shake or two extra. I have since cut back because I do not want to get obese frogs and heard that this can be an issue. On the one hand I want my frogs to eat all they can so they will have no growth issues, but on the other I do not want to have unhealthy frogs. I would say I still feed between 12-15 flies per frog and they are always eaten with much gusto. After they finish they all line up at the front of the tank like they are begging for more. I do supplement with springtails on Sunday morning as a extra meal for that day and seed with springtails on Sunday night after lights out. So my question is am I overfeeding them? They all look healthy and not bloated by any means but I am just concerned and want some advice. Thanks

Keep in mind that these are Tincs and they're eating machines. Growing Tincs will eat more too. I've watched a 2 month old froglet eat 10 flies np. Also, if they are breeding the female will consume more food.

Body weight and left over flies are the best way to get a handle on whether you are overfeeding or not.

Fat frog - cut back

Thin frog - add more (not a lot more, a thinner frog is healthier than a fat frog).

Left over flies - way to much.

It took us a while to get it down. We've had Tincs for close to two years.
