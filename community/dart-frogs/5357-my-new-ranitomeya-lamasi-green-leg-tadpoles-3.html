Don't be afraid to mist more, darts like it wet. Your substrate shouldn't have "puddles" but plenty of moisture will serve you sell. Make sure you get some leaf litter in there as well. It will help hold moisture, give your frogs somewhere to hide, and help with maintaining good microfuana. Get yourself some springtails, if your tank is wet enough it will mold and they will eat it and help breakdown the frogs droppings when they get out of water. Also you might want to start learning to culture fruit fly's since you have plenty of time with the tads.

BTW: Where did you get the tads?
