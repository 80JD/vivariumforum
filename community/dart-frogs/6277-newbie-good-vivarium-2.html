That design is good, but better suited for thumbnails and pumilios, which like the vertical space. I agree with the others that the floor space is pushing the limits (on the small side) for a pair of the terrestrial species you mentioned. Petco is having a $1 per gallon sale for tanks from 10 gal to 55 gal starting this Sunday (August 29th). You could get a 29-gal tank and glass lid for around $50 and make an ideal vivarium for a breeding pair of any terrestrial species.

If you have a local Petco, you'd also save on shipping and have enough to buy a decent light fixture.

Just my opinion.

If you don't want to do a traditional aquarium, there is a guy from California who constructs all glass enclosures of a similar design with sliding glass front doors. I saw his stuff in Daytona (Protean tanks) and they are larger and much more affordable. He can make one in any dimensions you want and he carries several standard sizes that are better suited for terrestrial species (more floor space than height). I couldn't find his website, but here is a recent thread on another forum with some pictures: http://geckoforums.net/showthread.php?t=65136
