Darts don't swim well at all , and thats a lot of open water...
If this is to be a dart tank against my opinion, I would suggest a big drift wood piece in the water, broad leaf aquatic plants that cover the surface of the water feature. You will need to have escape areas for the frogs before you add any.

My 2nd question would be what kind of darts are you looking at getting?

I can't tell you what to do, but unless you can get rid of most of that water feature no matter what you do that is not a good PDF set-up and they will stress out. It's not just the fact that it's way too deep and large, but that makes the land area way too small for them, especially Auratus (the green and black ones you speak of) which are one of the larger PDF species. PDFs aern't cheap, so your best bet is making a whole new tank or you might be paying for some soon-to-be-dead frogs.

Also, I trust you're not mixing the FBTs with PDFs, correct??
