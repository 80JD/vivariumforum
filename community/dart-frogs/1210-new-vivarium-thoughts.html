Is the guy that told you that mentally handicapped? Because I cant see a professional giving advice like that. Keep darts with darts. I have a post history that shows I am on the proceed with caution side of the argument. to mix even different types of Darts you would need a larger tank. to mix different species even bigger and I dont know if I would mix Darts with anything.

1. They are smaller than most other animals and could be eaten.

2. They are not as poisonous as their wild counter parts but would still have toxins that could harm other frogs, remember all frogs are toxic and all frogs have different toxins frog A's toxin may be harmless to us but will kill frog B with a quickness.

3. Dartfrogs are kept in very humid generaly stale air environments. Most other animal even needing humidity will need more air flow.

RETF's eat crickets. crickets can get large enough to harm PDF's and could also eat their Fruit Flies depleating the food supply.

I an really surprised a person selling frogs for a living would say RETFs and PDFs are compatible tank mates.

Im not an expert but I see nothing but tragedy in that living situation, for all occupants.
