OKAY:
I may have found a way to make the guitar-viv work and be frog-suitable...!

Make a suitably-sized hole in the back of the guitar.
Make a same-sized hole in the front of a frog-suitable tank (custom-made or otherwise, but I'm still mostly thinking in the creative stages now).
Attach them together, covering hole and join in natural-looking clay (or whatever the background material is).

Then all I do is set it up, run it frogless until I think I can handle them, and then add darts!

This way, they get a nice home with an interesting alcove, I get to sometimes see frogs in a guitar, and ...you guys get the peace of mind knowing the frogs aren't cramped.



This all depending on nobody spotting some flaw or another in the plan. So what do you think?

So, keep in Mind It's a VERY rough photoshopping job, but i'll explain it as well...
First, coat the inside with an epoxy (aquarium epoxy is best, dunno where to get it)
So, basically, you'd use the top 3-4''for lighting, use the guitar face, but install a fan into the "side" (now top) of the guitar, to compensate for heat.

then layer the lowest point with peagravel (or sand, or anything you really want) to form a small pond like features, then using driftwood and GS (expanding polyurethane foam) create a bank and have some smaller growing things (cryptanthus, selaginella, pilea, peperomia, stuff like that) growing up the bank, and have a fast growing vine (maybe F.pumila?) growing up the back.

Oh and you could put some small aquatics (anubias nana and mini microsword comes to mind, even riccia) in the pond...



Again, this is just a suggestion, based on what I would do...
