Breeding Behavior or Fighting Behavior??
I've got a couple Auratus. One is definetly bigger than the other. I haven't heard either of them call. They are a little over a year old. The bigger of the two chases the other around the tank. The smaller eventually goes inside a film canister. The bigger follows the smaller into the film canister. The bigger attempts to get on the smallers back. The bigger pats him and "bobs" up and down in some sort of display. I don't have any eggs in the tank that I've found. I don't know if this is aggressive behavior or love makin behavior.

Any help would be great....

I bought a sexed pair of auratus from Patrick a few months ago and they've given me quite a few tads already. Sounds like breeding behavior to me as well. They have a pretty quiet call and I have never heard my male calling. I bet you'll have a cluster of eggs in the next few weeks. Once they get started, they'll lay anywhere from a half dozen to a dozen eggs about every 18 days to three weeks. Mine gave me about seven egg clutches and then they stopped. My guess is that they're taking a break and will likely start up again in a month or two.
