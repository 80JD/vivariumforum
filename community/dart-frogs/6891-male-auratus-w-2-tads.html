My male auratus calls most days, many times per day. The female will starting following him around the tank. After some circles around the tank, he eventually will lead her under a coco hut. She lays the eggs (usually about 7-9). He fertilizes them.
After about 2.5 weeks, the eggs hatch. The male goes inside the hut and sits on / near the tadpoles. The tadpoles climb onto his back. He carries them to a small pool of water (usually a bromeliad plant) and deposits the tadpoles.
My frogs are about 1.5 years old. They have been reproducing for about 3 months now.
