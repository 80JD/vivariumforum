frogs skin can be sticky to soil and grainy things being it's moist. then they drag that wherever they go. when they get in the water it comes off. hopping around on moss or leafs helps that. the screen keeps it from falling straight through.

i couldn't give you an estimate. i've seen people do complete 50 gal. tanks for $40 and i've seen people sink probably around $1200 into an exoterra (1 exo... plus the frogs).

what soil they use is neither here nor there if you plan on de-potting, rinsing and aging the plants in the tank.
