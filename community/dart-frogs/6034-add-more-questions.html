That's the spirit! I think that some people are afraid to ask questions... I check the forum several times a day, and unless I miss it because of a forum hiccup, I read every post. I don't respond to all of them anymore, but I respond to most of them. If I don't know the answer, I'll find out. If I see someone not getting help, I'll figure out a way to get it for them. And I am always a PM away if anyone wants to ask me anything directly.

I have come a LOOOOOOOOOOOONG way since joining the forum. I have learned more here than on any other forums I have been a part of. Not only do I learn when I ASK a question, I learn when someone ELSE asks them too, whether it be through the answers they receive, or the research I do personally to answer the question myself. I've learned so much since I joined here and come to love herps so much more, that I'm actually planning to go back to school to become a herpetologist. I doubt I would be doing that if not for what I've learned here.

Questing opens doors, expands your mind, and brings you joy. So quest away.
