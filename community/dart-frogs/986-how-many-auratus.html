I would say 4 or 5 but no more than 6. Good math is a pair for a 10 gallon and 5 to 10 gallons of space for each other frog. keep in mind the filled space of your viv. if you have a deep drainage layer and thick background you have less space frog frogs. also make sure you have plenty of cover and hiding spots regardless of # of frogs. this will decrease territoriality and stress, making your frogs feel safer and be more active.
