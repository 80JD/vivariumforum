A guitar for my frog...?
Or a frog for my guitar.

I've been casting my eye around my house looking for ideas for an interesting vivarium, and it caught my attention that my guitar needs replacing. So now, I'm wondering if I can turn my old guitar into a small vivarium for thumbnail dart frogs.

Aside from a little more research into caring for frogs, I'm also considering what I would need to make it suitable. My current plan is to replace the front panel with glass or acrylic, possibly making a 'sound hole' access hole or ventilation hole; research into treating the wood or solving the problem of moisture; then create a clay background inside the guitar and house a small bromeliad and (possibly - this is unresearched) some kind of orchid.

I'm fairly new to animal care, though I'm a very quick learner and very passionate about it, and so dedicated. So, have I missed anything important? Is this an impossible task, or a stroke of genius?
