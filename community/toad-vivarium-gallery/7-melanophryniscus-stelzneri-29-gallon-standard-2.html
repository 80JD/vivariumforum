So precious! And if you ever were considering a no-fuss background you could always get the plain black stuff people put on the backs of their fish tanks. The flat blackness makes it difficult to notice imperfections in the application and it makes the plants really pop. I use it in my fish tanks and have applied it to an existing set up using packing tape and an exacto knife. Piece of cake. Less fabulous than some of your other tanks' cork backgrounds, but it wouldn't require armageddon in there.
