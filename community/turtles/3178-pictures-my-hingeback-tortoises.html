Thanks everyone

K. homeana is a forest tortoise with considerable range over the continuous Guinea-Congo rainforest region. They inhabit forest floors and areas along river banks. Unlike most tortoise, they are insectivores and even swim a little. SO far mine prefer to eat worms, darkling beetles, and portabella mushrooms.

Keeping these guys is NOT for the faint of heart, lol. They are very shy, very easily stressed, and very picky eaters. Not to mention they are all imported wild caughts. That is my goal... To produce captive bred babies for the same price as wild caught adults and do my part to stop importation.

I have more pictures, I will post them shortly!
