Most dart frog tanks are not heated by heating pads. Unless the temps stay below the mid to low 60s constantly their is really no need for heat. Usually the heat from the lights is plenty, and a drop in temps at night is supposed to be beneficial.
Like you mentioned, the substrate and in your case, foam background really helps hold in that heat from the lights.
