The wood is the culprit. wood contains tanic acid and this leaches into the water it soaks in. It does change the water PH but should be fine. My aquarium has diferent wood in it and it gets that "Black Water" amazon look after a shor time(most of my fish are from the amazon though). You can boil the wood in in a deep pot if it will fit and this will remove allot of the tanins. if you do it afew times it should help allot, however there will be some left. if you dont like it you can do more frequent water changes. also carbon in the filter helps. It wont hurt most animals it just realy changes the PH. Mystery Snails wont survive in tanic water. they get holes in thier shells and die. You dont want to mix species. Especialy in a 10gallon tank. Look into Fire Bellied Toads. 2 or 3 of them would love that setup. Mini crabs will take bites of anything they can catch like most crustations. some one put a crawdad in with some RETFs and the frog hit the water and well no more frog. you could have guppys or tetras in the water with the toads as long as you didnt mind one disapearing once in a while. Also i if you dont have filtration pick up a whisper 10i filter anthat will hel the water. That is your best bet. And please please besides like I mentioned above with the fish/herp dont mix species especialy in a small tank. This should only be done by the most exsperienced keepers in large environments and even then its not reccomended. Fire bellied toads are the way for you to go I think. Or a small group of anoles but Im thinking to much water to land for the Anoles to realy be happy.
