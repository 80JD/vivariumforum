Crickets, Sowbugs (rollie pollies), springtails. Not only will they get rid of mold but they will also break down feces and decomposting plant matterial.

A good balanced system will regulate itself.
I believe that a 120 gallon is to small for a CWD.
Its recommended that a CWD needs at least a 8 ft long x 4 deep x 4 high Cage. As they get bigger they will a pond. as long as they are. 4ft long for adults.

The fans will help with mold in areas that the air circulates. between the substrate and the glass is not one of these areas...

as far as temp/humidity, the fans will only help keep it fluent top to bottom in a tank this size, it won't pose any problems if setup right. DON"T use the fans to push in fresh air.. circulate the air within the tank and you will be fine.

Quote:
Originally Posted by Fillygirl
I am still not sure if I should do anything to the mold at the front of the tank. Should I move the soil and scrape it off the glass and then put the pill bugs (sow bugs) in to keep it from coming back??
Yeah you can do that.

The mold is always there and will pretty much always be there. Usually it's just too small for us to see it. It just grows to the point where you can see it when conditions are right. It can't infect your frogs but I could understand that it doesn't really look very nice.

When I get mold in my setups and I want it to dissapear immediately (and I don't want to disturb the tank contents with my hands) I wet it. The thing is, the mold probably started growing because conditions are too humid. I'd suggest circulating the air inside the tank or drying it up a little. If you don't have anything in the tank except live plants you could open the top for a day or so? If not then you could put some kind of screen on the top to let the air circulate freely?
