No way springtails can mate with fruitflies. lol

I can't see woodlice (which is the proper name for these animals, by the way but they are also known as rollie pollies, isopods & pillbugs) being toxic. I think the worry in the other thread was that they could harm frogs because thier "shell" which is actually thier exoskeleton, was tough to digest.

I feed woodlice to all my frogs and I haven't seen them harmed. I have RETF, FBT, GTF, WTF as well as local species like Spring Peepers, Leopard frogs, Green frogs and Wood frogs. IMHO they aren't toxic. I wouldn't feed woodlice to froglets though.

As far as eating plants - I think potentially they could only harm the roots of a plant. I don't think they are animals that eat live plants. One of my tanks has them in there and they don't harm my plants as far as I could tell. I went to check some root systems underneath the substrate in that tank just now and I don't see any root damage.

All my tanks have springtails, they just got in there "on thier own" so to speak, because I never purposely put any in there. Anyone who has an established tank will have them in there I suspect and it's pretty natural to have them in there. I culture earthworms outside of my setups but haven't really thought of letting some loose in the tanks. Might be worth a try because I can't see them doing any harm even if they were to die in the soil.

Earthworms should be used in any planted tank in my opinion.
They keep the soil from getting compacted and areated. They also fertilize the soil. Plants will die if they get "root bound". Unless you flooding the tanks they will survie.

Pillbugs (woodlice, Rollie Pollies) are detritivores. They eat dead material. They actually live above ground not in the soil, if you really pay enough attention you will notice that they are found just under the decaying plant material and above the soil line.
