If you dont have a Heavy misting system get a hand held garden sprayer, home deopt has one that holds a gallon of water and can hand pump air into it for like 7 bucks, and blast the poop off the plants and into the soil to break it up. if you keep the water soft you can avoid spots on the glass. other then that Viv cleaning is no easy task if you let it get outta hand, i blast and polish the plants every day and check for new mold breakouts

Well let me say beforehand since you haven't kept either of these species on thier own individually I wouldn't recommend starting a mixed viv with them.

I'd have many many really logical reasons for saying that but I digress

My word to the wise is to keep them individually for a little while. Personally there is no way I would introduce a new frog into an established tank (meaning any frog including adding a new GTF to a GTF only tank for example) because from experience I know they need to be quarantined for at aleast 60 days. New frogs have to be dewormed and treated for chytrid.

I have no previous experience with clowns so I can't help you there. Tiger legs have similar care to RETF's. Again it's really important to know how each species behaves individually and on thier own before mixing them. You won't see the warning signs the frogs are sending unless you know what to look for.

If you want to be really wreckless and just throw them in together and see what happens then don't expect too much. Not really the way I would do it, though. That's my take on it...

You could try your best to match the recommended temps and humidity levels then see if they can survive together.
