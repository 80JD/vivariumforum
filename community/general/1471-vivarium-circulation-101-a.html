Vivarium Circulation 101?
Okay, I've been lurking on the posts and searching and I've got a few questions on vivarium air circulation that I'm hoping some more experienced folks will jump in and anwer.
I'm in the process of planning out a 120 gallon Oceanic Vivarium and I think I need a little more knowledge before I begin constructing.

Question 1: When hooking up a computer fan(s) for circulation, is it blowing in or out of the tank?

Question 2: How much is too much and how do you know? I read a few folks hooking their fans up to sensors to come on automatically??...

Question 3: How do I go about hooking a fan(s) up? (I read on one website folks just taking old cell phone chargers and matching them up to the fans as closely as possible??)

Question 4: Does the placement of these fans matter? (I read somewhere that good air circulation prevents fogging glass as well as mold?)


I'm sure at one point these questions were answered in other threads but I can't seem to find them so if someone could point me in the right directions that would be great. Any and all help, as well as opinions, are always welcome. Thanks!!

1. It is blowing within the tank. you want to circulate the humid air, not draw in tons of fresh air or humidity maintenance will be an issue.

2. it should not be windy, just a gentle motion of air. all you are doing is preventing the air from becoming stagnant, not forcing it through the trees.

3. homedepot or radio shack sell universal power supplies that have adjustable voltage switches. this switch can be used to raise or lower the speed of the fan.

4. I plan for mine to blow air right across the face of the glass to prevent fogging, but you want a good spread of them in a viv that large.

Fans are way down the line for you at this point. What are you planning to keep in this monster of a viv??

You want the fan blowing top to bottom, either at an angle against the glass(to prevent humidity buildup) or across the tank (still top to bottom). The goal is to have a nice calm steady breeze throughout the tank to keep air moving everywhere. This will help regulate temp and humidity throughout the tank.

placement as far as angles and how many fans will depend on the size and shape of your tank, internal obstacles, etc..
Idea...If you have a fogger, turn it on and play with the fan. it might show you where there is no air flow there will be fog.
