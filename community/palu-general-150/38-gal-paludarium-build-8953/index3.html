I personally love pea puffers and it looks like you would have enough water for one. One is all you would need to completely destroy your snail population. They will probably rid your tank of snails in about 2 weeks. From then, you would probably need to make sure you have some frozen blood worms on hand.

As far as keeping them with amphibians, I think you can do it with the right ones. Fire bellied toads might be ok with them, but one concern is the toxins they give off. I've heard that fire bellied toads give off toxins that are potentially harmful to other amphibians, but I'm not sure what it would do to fish if anything. Also, where they spend a lot of time in the water, there's also a chance of the toads eating the puffer or the puffer nipping the toes.

With all that said, one thing to keep in mind is every puffer has a different personality. I have 2 pea puffers in 2 separate tanks. One I keep with dwarf cat fish and red cherry shrimp and it has never even tried to nip at them, the other I have to keep alone because he is pretty vicious. I think it also depends on if the puffer is last in the tank or first. I think they tend to be more vicious if they were there first, but that's just a theory.

Best of luck!
