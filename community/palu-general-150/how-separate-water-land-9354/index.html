If you're going to make a "tall" waterfall, you are going to want to invert the tank in such a way that you are making it horizontal (so that when it is put back, it will be vertical) because stacking foam is just going to make it goop down flat. Also, you may not want to use JUST greatstuff foam. You could use rocks. You'll see what I mean about halfway down this page:
FrogFevers 55g Display Tank Build...
After all is said and done, you MUST seal it with 100% silicone caulk. Otherwise the foam will degrade, and possibly poison the inhabitants.

For future reference, you should search the forums FIRST before you ask a question as common as this, because there are TONS of topics on this already.
