Hello,

You have a spotted salamander, Ambystoma maculatum...they make fine pets and can live in excess of 20 years, but I would suggest a few changes in the care you provide.

Covering the top to increase humidity can lead to fungal infections and other problems in time (poor ventilation). Spraying moss and providing a water bowl is sufficient. Be sure to use an instant water de-chlorinator (drops, sold for use with tropical fish) in water bowl and spray bottle.

75-80 F is above the recommended temp for this species - they will live for a time, but immune system weakens and leaves them open to bacterial/fungal infections and disease. Move to a cool basement if available or chill moss with a small freezer pac and replace daily. 60-68 ideal, or even lower if possible.

You'll need to vary the diet quite a bit - earthworms are far superior to crickes as the base of the diet, supplements important if unless diet is very varied (please see article below). Cut down on food as the animal approaches full size and is kept cooler - 1 small earthworm or 6 1/2 inch crickets weekly suffice for adults at 65 F.

Be sure to rinse and replace moss regularly..ammonia builds up quickly but is colorless and odorless - animal will absorb through its skin and be poisoned over time.

Please check out my article on Spotted Salamander care at The Spotted Salamander, Ambystoma maculatum, ? Care in Captivity – Part 1 | That Reptile Blog for further info and feel free to write back.

Good luck and enjoy, Frank
