Mudpuppy Question-Seeking Breeders/Suppliers
Hello everyone,

I am interning at a learning museum in Ohio and am responsible for procuring two different amphibians for our new exhibit about watersheds. So far, I have been able to get an American Bullfrog, but have had no luck with finding mudpuppies (Necturus maculosus).

Does anyone know of a breeder or supplier that currently has some to sell?
I have been checking Wards, Nasco, and Carolina Biological supply and have had no luck (Mudpuppies are seasonally available).

We have all supplies needed to care for them, and I have even met with an owner to make sure it would be the right fit for our tanks, we just need that mudpuppy. We could try to go out and catch one ourselves (laws permitting), but that is certainly a last resort.

I appreciate any help you can provide.
