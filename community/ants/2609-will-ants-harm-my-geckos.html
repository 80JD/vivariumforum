Ants will be ants , and while some only consume other insects and honeydew,fruit juices, grains of sugar and other sweets substances, some ants will eat meats and may try to take small bites outta your gecko. the damp wood and humidity make a ant really comfortable. and with breeding crickets thay just found a 10 star hotel. IMHO you need to tear down the VIV replace the substrate and boil what you can is saltwater. if not Eventually your tank will be 20% viv and 80% ant farm. grab a subject and take a picture and hopefully somebody can ID the bugger and you can go from there. You can also try the Dry Ice route, flooding the tank with CO2 and choke them out. Take care of the ant problem in your house before rebuilding the viv if you dont this will be a rince and repeat mission.

The dry ice method is actually quite easy. Remove your geckos (or any animal you want to save). Gather: dry ice, styrofoam cups, aluminum foil, and tape.

Put chunks of dry ice in several cups and set them in the vive. Cover the top with aluminum foil and tape it all the way around to seal it. The aluminum foil with poof up, but ours did not blow up. Leave vive alone until all dry ice has dissapated--at least several hours. Then remove foil & allow air to circulate through the vive and substrate to return to room temp or normal temp. Then replace animals.
