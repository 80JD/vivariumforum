Even the descriptions online for the kyoto moss says that it is ground up. It looks to me like they gather the sporophytes and grind them into powder. I don't know if that affects the way it grows or not, but since people have been growing moss from this stuff for years and years, I guess it works fine.

My experience with spores is actually gathering the sporophytes, that is why I suggested exposing them to a more acidic environment. If they have already been release (or in this case removed) from the capsule, then it shouldn't be necessary.

Peat moss is just milled sphagnum. It should be fine to use. I know that your picture of your growing medium might not work as well as straight peat, because you have larger chunks in there and the moss will have difficulty spreading across it. Since you're growing from spores, though, it might be ok.
