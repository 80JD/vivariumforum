Forgot to mention, spray watering will probably not be a sufficient way to water the plants so you should water the grow medium first, spraying may be beneficial between regular waterings in dry climates (like mine) to increase the humidity. Plants should be given enough light and air circulation to dry in no longer than 4 hours after watering. Also, you mentioned epiphytes in your message, do not let tillandsias or epiphytes in general sit in puddles or medium that retains a lot of moisture for long periods of time. A little moss under each plant is fine.
