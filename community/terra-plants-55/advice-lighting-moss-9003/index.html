All lighting produces some heat, that is unavoidable.

LED lighting emits the least amount but the lights are expensive and the only ones I know of that are really good for plants emit purple light (combo of red and blue). Not terribly attractive.

Cfls are probably your best bet as they don't emit a huge amount of heat (though far more than LEDs) and are relatively in expensive. I use daylight specturm cfls in my terrs.

Even with dried out moss, it isn't uncommon to see new moss appearing. The bagged moss often still has moss spores on it -- not to mentioin fern spores as well -- and moss spores are air borne so mosses can even show up that way. How familiar are you with moss's appearance? I only ask as people frequently mistake the presence of algae growing on dead/brown moss as the moss "coming back to life." It is difficult for me to easily explain though new moss does have (to me at least) a very different appearance. If it is just algae, the color tends to be a dark, slick/shiny green. New moss growth is often, but not always, a lighter shade of green than the older living moss and you can see the individual moss plants/growths.
