Well, after dooing some reading, I would recommend at least a 20G long with a topper. While they are small, they should still have more space than a small tank by itself will offer. The length of the 20G long tank is good, but a little more height (at least that of a 20G high) is going to be better. I suggest building a topper of eggcrate (or something similar) covered in GS to offer climbing space and to trap more humidity, with an open top and perhaps a plexi or glass front door. I think something similar to my CWD topper but on a smaller scale would be perfect. It will allow you to have areas of higher and lower humidity within the tank as well as offering plenty of space for the animals to fee secure.

You should be able to sanitize that tank just fine. The tank I have my japaluras in right now was a nasty fish tank when I brought it home. Just take a razor and a magic eraser to it, then wash down with a 10-15% bleach solution and rinse well.

I still think a topper for a little more height is a good idea. Sure, they are found mostly on the ground, but you can make a topper add so much more room to the tank. YOu can build shelves into it to add extra ground space, you can plant hanging plants up high so that the vines hang down, etc. They are super easy to build and not that expensive (less than 20 bucks for a tank that size, I'd think). Check out that link and see if it's something you'd like to consider. I'd say just another 10-12" would be just the right amount of added height.

I realize this thread is old but just for Pygmies you do NOT need to extend the height of your enclosure. Unlike other chameleons, pygmy chameleons are not arboreal but are actually terrestrial.

A 10g should be perfectly fine for just a pair, but if you plan on getting more I would go with the 20g (or maybe pre-emptively go with the 20g, cause you will probably fall in love with them and get a few more! they are the only chameleons that are social and can be housed together).

Also, I would highly, highly recommend a drip-water source or a *VERY* shallow pool of water. Pygmies WILL drink from standing water (mine did at least) but they don't like it and ...well just make sure your bowl is very, very shallow. These are NOT agile or careful creatures by any means...they are sort of like lemmings. You really have to completely "baby-proof" their cage so to speak. Unfortunately, I say that part from experience..
