Chameleons are not an animal to be handled they are more like aquarium fish, look but don't touch. Chams tend to stress out pretty easily and once they stress they go own hill pretty quick. Even their own reflection will stress them.
I don't have any because there is no place in my house that is peaceful enough not to stress one out.

As a general rule, I do not handle my herps. Even my tortoises. Especially my aquatics. The only time any handling is done is when it is necessary, for baths for the torts, or cage cleanings, or tear downs.

I recognize that they are reptiles, not puppies and kittens I wish more people realized that They really do not like to be handled, although they may tolerate it.
