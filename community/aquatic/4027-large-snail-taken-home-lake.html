That's a trapdoor snail (Viviparus malleatus or V. japonicus). They're originally from Asia, and are used over here as pond snails for outdoor koi ponds. It doesn't take them long to wind up in local water bodies, as well, and they get much larger than the indigenous species, so they do quite well. I found you a basic caresheet: How trapdoor snails benefit ponds and aquariums with pictures . They do get quite massive, though. A pond near me has them, and I thought about keeping/raising them, but as you said, they'd need quite a few gallons per snail.
