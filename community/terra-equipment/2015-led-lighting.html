I guess things are different in America because where I live LED is a very common & available light source.
At one end of the scale Christmas LED string lights are quite often used as a moon light effect to the other extreme of ultra bright systems lighting up reef tanks.
I love the effect of LED & use them a lot however the really bright systems are expensive ( although dropping in price every month) so I stick to the replacement 12 volt bulbs GU10 LED - Various & LED — (UK) Light bulbs, halogen lamps, spot lights and tubes by mail order in the UK. I dont know about American house hold lighting but there must be an equivalent replacement bulb?
Anyhow I use them as a cool low running cost light source to wake the tank up with clean sunrise lighting & shut the tank down with cool blue moon light.
