Hmmm... ok, just a speculative idea here... You could have the crickets contained in something that you could install a light in. Have a separate compartment that would stay totally dark, with a door that operates on a timer/counter. You could have the door between the two compartments open when the light comes on, and have some sort of counter (maybe operated by a laser beam or something?) count the crickets as they move into the dark compartment, and close the door between the two when there are as many as you want in the dark chamber. When the door closes, the light turns off. At this point, you could have the dark compartment dump into the vive. Maybe the whole floor could open and dump them in from the top or something. Of course, this would likely be very expensive to construct, but it would be cool as hell to see in operation, lol...
