Thank you for explaining. That makes perfect sense. Very good idea! Definitely a good way to do a sump for a smaller tank for someone that can't drill for a sump for whatever reason, or just doesn't want to. I have thought of something similar in the past, but my reasoning for it was to make a rain system. Do you have any plans to do anything like that with this?

You might take a look at the power of the pump for a hang on back type filter for a fish tank, as they pull water up in the way you are describing. That should help you determine how powerful you need your pump to be. If you haven't already looked at fountain pump, make sure you do, they are generally less expensive, and most of them allow you to adjust the flow (invaluable in my opinion). I think the pump I was looking at from Home Depot has a 10' head with adjustable flow and was less than 50 bucks (that one may have been $60, actually... it could have been the 7' that was less than $50).
