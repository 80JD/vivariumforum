me take
Preface: I'm an electrical engineer with a background in automation controls

So me being the geek I am, I want to fully automate my habitats through the use of a PLC(Programable Logic Circuit) based control panel. This would monitor temperature/humidity and turn lighting on/off etc to control the climate of the tank - a way over elaborate air conditioning system. Anyways, the guys in the office like to make fun of "the frog kid" as my wall paper is always some red eye tree frog or whatever the flavor of the week may be.

Anyways where this pertains to crickets:
Jokingly, the idea of a automatic cricket feeder got thrown out. My idea is to have some type of hopper with a tube running into the enclosure. The tube would have a door on each end. At a given time both doors will open allowing crickets to pass in. To sense as crickets go through, a break beam sensor is the best I could come up with. Basically, you'd need a sensor at each end - see how many crickets go in, how many go out. Once the desired number of crickets have entered the staging tube, the first door closes...after all have past into the enclosure, the second door closes.

Now this isn't a sound idea seeing as it goes off the assumption the crickets will walk linearlly into the enclosure from the hopper.

Hope this gives a good laugh because the schematics that were being passed around and modified at my office were hillarious.
