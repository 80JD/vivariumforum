I do own this... It is hard(impossible) to fire up fluorescents at dim light. they need a "POP" of power to fire up the phosphors in the bulb, so the dawn simulation leaves a little to be desired. the Dusk phasing off is pretty cool and
much easier on the bulbs as the phosphors are already lit.
The automatic setting timer is great, turn it on with the switch at 10/12/14 hours and it automatically sets to turn on again at that same time every day. it has a test feature, and I believe it has a safety that won't allow the ballast to fire if the bulbs are burnt out. this keeps it from frying your ballast if the bulbs are dead. I know the hagen T5's have this feature so i think this does too..
I would have liked longer cords both for the power, and for the bulbs.

I give it 4 out of 5 stars.
