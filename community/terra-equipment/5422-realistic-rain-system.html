if you want realistic rain you'll be designing something yourself essentially. sure we could help, shoot you ideas, share our collective knowledge/experience/etc. but realistic rain systems all seem to be unique to the builder.

one downfall to rain in a viv for watering is that rain will quickly saturate a setup. you may be better off going with something proven like a misting system or a fogger. i'd say a rain system would be something you'd want localized in a corner or very seldom used. otherwise you'll be best off building an overflow drain into your system.
