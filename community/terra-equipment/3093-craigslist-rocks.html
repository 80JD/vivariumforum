You can do a search using any terms you think someone may use in reference to a tank (tank, aquarium, reptarium, terrarium, vivarium). Some of them list in the pets section (it's not a for sale section), some in general for sale, it really just depends on where they think they should list it, since there isn't a category for it. Doing a search really narrows it down. Also, if you are handy at all, keep an eye out for windows or glass that people are giving away/selling and make a tank yourself. Also keep an eye out on in the free section. If you are quick, sometimes you can get great stuff. Someone was giving away a 150G tank with EVERYTHING (fish, filter, plants, stand, lighting) for free. I wasn't fast enough for that one, unfortunately.
