It is also called "light diffusing panels" or something like that... The label on the ones I just bought (from Home Depot, on the isle where they sell fluorescent fixtures) says:

White Eggcrate Louver 2GT 2x4
Dim: L=45.84" (116.4 cm) W=21.88" (55.6 cm) H=.375" (1.0 cm)

(I thought the dimensions might be helpful)

It is made by Lithonia Lighting, which it says is an ACUITY Brands Compnay (lol at THIER typo). When you ask for eggcrates, they are probably thinking you want actual egg crates, that they keep eggs in... At least that is the response I got. You can call and ask again if you don't want to waste the gas, but I bet if you have the actual brand name and what it says on the label they'll be able to find it for you, in the computer if nothing else. If they DON'T have it, do a search with the brand name and what they call it on the label and see what you can find.
