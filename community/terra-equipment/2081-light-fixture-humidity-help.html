You would use a barrier on the screen to keep in some humidity. Suggest something inexpensive like sarran wrap. You might want to get something to create a gap between the light and the top as the light will put off heat and it might increase the temp for your RETF's to an uncomforatble amount. The types of plants you use will definitely be an indicator of what bulb you'd want to get. Also, post some pics, let us see what your working on.
