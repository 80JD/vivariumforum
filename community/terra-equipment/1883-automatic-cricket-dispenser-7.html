Here is my idea for the dispenser

You need a long tube for the cricket to fall in but not be able to jump out. 12 to 20 inch is my guess i didn't work with cricket that much. This work with door operate by electric solenoid/piston. The top door has an optic sensor to count the cricket when they fall in. Lets say 5. They fall in being attract by light, food, heat, something. After five the top door close and keep them lock inside (this work on a 24hrs cycle). At 1pm or the time you like the bottom door open 1min or 2 to let all the cricket get out or fall into the viv you can add a little cellphone vibrator unit or something similar to force the cricket to fall. When the bottom door close after the time program is past it reset the top one to open and start the hole cycle again.

So that is my idea if you like it let me know i might need help to build it. specially for programmation.
