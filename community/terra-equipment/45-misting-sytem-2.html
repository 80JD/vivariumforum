Hey Guys, (for those that don't know I run MistKing.com)

here's the story of the pumps. The kits were originally designed for froggers. Typically the demand wasn't anything higher then misting for 1 minute several times per day. Every now and then a timer would fail and somones pump would burn out. I since then implemented a thermal fuse so in case a timer does fail, the fuse will burn out before the pump will. Pumps are self priming most of the time, sometimes you might have to prime it when the diff b/w the reservoir and the pump is big. Some of the chameleon and lizard people like to use misting systems for 15-20 minutes at a time. That's not really advisable on that pump as you might be running a risk of burning out the fuse. The small pumps should not run for more then 5 min at a time, then they need to cool down. It's not a perfect pump, but for frog tanks it works like a charm - especially if you are running it on a repeat cycle timer. I am working on getting a pump that will be a bit more expensive and will be able to run dry, be self priming, all that good stuff... I just need to find a bit more time to test things out. Right now, I'm running on borrowed time, hhee

If you guys have any more questions, let me know
