honestly i think this idea will require either:
a robotic arm with a mass of checks and balances... optical sensors... real time 3d mapping... a drop tube in/on a scale w/2 trap doors... a timer to set a time to start... a trigger to tell it when to stop w/fail safes (example: numeric algorithm that counts crickets and scale that measures weight, stops the arm, closes top door and opens bottom)...spring loaded pressure sensitive pincers covered in a poly rubber... very difficult w/out the resources/money for a prototype.

or a very simple bug trap rigged up as a breeding pen inside the tank... like a minnow trap w/smaller holes and some gs around it... you could easily learn what you need to do to keep the right flow once the initial design is constructed... they will eventually learn to sneak out 1 @ a time... the trick will be figuring out how many to keep in there to keep the flow going... it will take trial and error just like anything else... but should work... also should buy at least 2 weeks for a vaca b4 you must reduce population again... gut loading can also be done inside!
