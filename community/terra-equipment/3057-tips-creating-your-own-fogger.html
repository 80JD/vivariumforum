Why exactly are you having trouble keeping the humidity up in a 10G? The smaller the enclosure, the easier it is to keep it humid, IME. Do you have a screen top? If so, that's the problem and you should cover it with something (plexi works well). You can also buy an air pump, a small bubble stone, and get a glass bottle (or even a plastic soda bottle), then put the bubble stone in the bottle, fill it with water, plug in the air pump, put the bottle into the vive, and viola! instant humidifier.

Ok, so this is the starting product, the KAZ personal ultrasonic humidifier


Oops, i exhaled to soon.


Just kidding, anyways this shows the reducing coupling from the 2" hole, to a 1/2" thread. You'll need one of those... I had to grind away half of the octagon shape, to have half circle, half octagon. This was because the KAS top has a slight angle, It wouldn't have set level in the hole if i didn't remove some of the sides of the reducing coupling, It sits flush with the ledge inside the hole after.
I also had to silicone the reducing coupling onto the KAZ for a good snug fit. Just make sure that it is a solid band around it, and you can get by with an extremely small amount of silicone.


This shows the 1" OD (Outside Diameter) 3/4" ID (Inside Diameter) vinyl hose. It also shows 3/4" Elbows and a 3/4" Tee. These are some of the parts that im going to use for inside my tank.. I am making it run up through the bottom of my paludarium, and running under the substrate to where i want it to exit. Its hopefully going to be completely hidden, but we'll see how well it goes.


Now, you can go with any size of vinyl hose you want to, but i would suggest to keep it over a half inch ID because it seems to not push through to much fogg unless its a wide hose.. The wider the better...
I haven't purchased these parts yet, but i plan on getting some reducing barbs and smaller hose for another place for fog to exit, on the base of the waterfall... I plan on making my waterfall about 3 inches of actual fall, so im not sure if the fogg will trace the water down it or not... Either way, it will give a better effect if i have it near the top and bottom of the falls.
