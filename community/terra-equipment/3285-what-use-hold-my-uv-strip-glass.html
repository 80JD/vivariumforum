radio shack sells a great alternative to velcro... can't remember what it is called, but it's a clear plastic thingy with little hemorrhoids everywhere for lack of better description... it sticks to anything with the glue it has, and it locks in so it wont EVER come apart by accident like velcro... btw the glue sticks better than double sided tape and it is easier to remove after it has bonded. be careful not to use too much... you may never get it off!

for the other end... use a small nut and bolt with washers.... poke the bolt through the screen, add the washer to hold it... add the nut to tighten it... get it close to tight, pop the bolt head in the screw holes of your fixture and tughten... or you can silicone it in the fixture itself... a lock nut or some lock tight will keep this contraption from ever unscrewing if you think that's necessary.

clean the glass with water and a razor... then wet it again and use a green pad and squeegee... this should get it clean enough to get a better bond... if not do it again w/any window cleaner or finger nail polish remover/acetone... i'd do the whole process twice anyway!
