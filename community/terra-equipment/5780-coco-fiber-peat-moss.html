Coco Fiber or Peat Moss
What is better for covering a great stuff background with? I've used peat moss before, and it seemed to work okay. But a lot of people seem to use coco fiber. Does it hold better or is it just more asthetically pleasing? What do you guys use when you are creating backgrounds?

I usually use peat, if I'm using that sort of technique, because it's cheaper and I don't have to wet it to expand it.

When I'm using a technique like the concrete binder/titebond method, I use peat or coco or a mix of both, it just depends what I have on hand.

Generally, peat is dustier than coco, it's ground up smaller. Coco comes in a larger variety of ... grinds?, like chunks, croutons, fibers, coarsely ground and finely ground, so you can customize what you'd like a little more than with peat.
