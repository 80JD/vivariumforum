We've been using the Zilla Power Center, digital version, which I think is easier to use. You set it once and it takes care of things for you. You'd only need to be concerned if there's a power outage.

jWe're very pleased with the digital version.

I googled the product name and checked prices until I found a deal I was happy with. Remember to check shipping costs, too, at each place--some places offer free shipping if you order over $50 worth of equipment/supplies, which can make things less expensive in the long run. If one place charges $28 plus $10 shipping, that's really $38; if another place charges $33 with free shipping, that's $5 less than the place that has the "cheaper" price.

You can also make your own, and some people (like Beckdg) can tell you how, but my DH & I didn't have the time, so buying was best for us.

I use that one..It has
2: Daytime (Yellow)
2:Nighttime (Blue)
4: Constant on (white)

The Daytime/Nighttime can be set to alternate every 15 minutes

I usually run mine 14 Hours daytime/10 hour nights because of my light

I have (2) 6 Plugs plugged into each daytime/nighttime for 12 each. As long as the total wattage does not exceed the maximum for each..I run about 96 watts out of 2000 I think at on during the day and right now 1 13 watt at night.
