lol, your right, sorry! I just got caught up in the wonderment, heh... Ok, here's some of my advice, I don't have all that much time to give any right now! I tink you should go ahead a do a BG and put in live plants, BEFORE you get your little lady in there. It will be better to do it NOW, than have to find somewhere to put her to do it later. If you put in a BG, you can make ledges, planters, all kinds of stuff, and because the enclosure is so large, you can totally go all out!!!! I'm getting all excited imagining the stuff you can do with it! Of course, a CWD will do just fine in an enclosure that is not natural vivaria, but hey, it looks better to have it totally planted! If you want it to still be easy to clean, You can plant ALL the plants in planters. I would recommend some sort of tree-ish type plant in the bottom left, just make sure it is one that will not grow too tall, and will hold your dragon's weight. When you are looking for one, don't forget the to add the height of the planter to the height of the tree. Of course, you CAN use one that will potentially grow taller than the enclosure, you'll just have to prune or bonsai it. You can grow just about any tree in miniature form! For the BG, you don't have to just use GS, you can use the extruded polystyrene insulation foam. The kind I got is pink, and it is more dense so it will be easier to carve. This will be PERFECT for ledges! You can make all sorts of shapes and rock formations with it, and it looks soooo awesome! A good thread to look at for the tools you'll need to carve it and whatnot is: https://www.vivariumforum.com/communi...n-rebuild.html This guy knows alot about GS AND the insulating foam, he makes some awesome halloween decorations with them. If you decide to do the BG, you can add SO much dimension to the tank, and I think your dragon will love it. They love to climb and bask, so the more they have to climb on the better. Ledges, vines (artificial, natural dried, and if you can find any - live), plants (make sure they can stand up to a trampling and having the leaves punctured! If your dragon doesn't nibble plants, you can use some dieffenbachia, if she does, stay away from it, because it can potentially cause paralysis if eaten. My guy hasn't eaten any yet, but I'm keeping an eye on the leaves for any bite marks), branches, driftwood, even ladders will be a good addition to the enclosure. I went to a beach this past Saturday that has tons of driftwood (alot of it whole trunks! too big for me, lol), and picked up some great pieces. I got more than I need, but most of it is smaller pieces, I couldn't carry any big ones back over the drifted trees to get it back to the car. If you use found branches (you can even cut them off live, which I prefer unless I can find good driftwood pieces), just sterilize them by baking for a little bit, or freezing. I wouldn't do bleach for driftwood, unless you are able to soak it in a chlorine remover to get all the bleach out, I ruined a great piece by bleaching it, it sucked all the bleach up, and I haven't been able to get it out yet. Alright, that's all I have time for right now, but I'll try to post some more later when my hubby isn't looking, heh...
