I'm liking these tetra in-tank filters. they sit in the tank and take the regular old tetra filter pads. you can use it easily to make a waterfall, can cram the pad full of carbon, and can fit a small heater in there too if need be.
the tank I'm working on now (44hex) is getting 2 water features. 1 with just a pump, and one with the whisper 10i. check it out.. they make a pretty big one too... I think the 40i flows about 170gph.
