the fans are on the same time as the light right now ( i have timers i'm just too lazy to program them), they are on a speed controller (PC 4 channel) - which I will be switching out for another controller w/ 24vdc supply all in one that I found at MicroCenter. the dryer duct would have been huge and gaudy and I couldn't find an easy solution to making a fan box, but come to find out that startech.com 40mm thin fans fits perfect inside a 3in to 2 in reducer, throw a 2in to 1.5in reducer bushing on the other side and some pvc and then some 90 degree elbows and viola! pulls and pushes air from inside the tank all while being hung discretely on the back of the tank (think of a fish tank filter).

So far it's been working , but I"m thinking for heavy CFM applications, I can do a double fan on each side.

this is the all in one control/power supply I mentioned: http://microcenter.com/single_produc...uct_id=0251807
