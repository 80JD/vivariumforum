New build 210g turtle tank with pictures (work in progress)
Hello all,

I am working on a 210g paludarium to house my turtles. I got the tank for a steal, and I am about 3/4 of the way through the background construction.

I made the background on light defuser with a basking shelf, which has 2 three inch capped plumbing pipe caves for my convicts to hide.

I do not have pictures yet.. But I zipped wood to the defuse added a couple of rocks, and cut in a large ziplock food container into the basking shelf that will be filled with substrait should the turtles lay eggs in the future. I also have 2 5 inch hydroponic net pots and 6 3 inch pots foamed in as well as the piping for the water feature.

I used t-rex "Jungle Bed" and black silicon to cover the background.

Right now I have to vacuum the excess substrait off, and touch up some bare spots, and then it will be sitting until we move to the new house in about a month which should give it a nice amount of time for the silicon to fully cure up before seeing any water.

I am kind of at a loss for plants and would love if anyone has any ideas as to what I should get.. right now I only have a scottish moss, and irish moss that I saw when I was on the road this week. I do not thing I will be using them in any of the planters though.

I would like 2 creeper style plants... Something that will give me nice wall cover.. I'm sure I am going to get an assortment of air plants as time goes on as well.. I have found a couple of sites that have some really amazing air plants.

I assume that broms are a sure bet for this type of setup.. any other ideas?

Also, I was wondering if anyone knows of any kind of spray clear coat that is fish safe.. I am worried about the areas of the background that will be submerged in water deteriorating.. It's already covered in the mix I mentioned above which looks to be mostly cork bark and coconut fiber. I basically would like to put a clear layer of something on top of it to seal it in.

Thanks for your time, and I will have pictures up as time progresses.

some pictures from the water feature test. I decided to go with silicone to coat the parts that are under water I just applied it in thin layers.. let one dry add another I think I stopped at 5 and it looks as if it worked.. at least so far.

I drained it today and will let it completely dry out before finishing up some touch ups, adding moss to the top of the background to hide some silicone, and glass that is visible, and some patch ups. The black foam at the bottom should be covered once the substrate goes in, so that will stay as is. I also have some river rocks and driftwood that will fill in some spots as well (like covering the pump) and then it needs to be moved to it's final location and get some turtles swimming around.

I am going to build a new cabinet, and canopy in the future to hide the misting tubes, and lighting. One step at a time I guess

On a one to ten scale I would give this a 7.. I learned a bunch of things for future builds, and I think it is pretty good for a first attempt.

As it gets set up, and starts growing in I will keep this thread updated as much as I can.

I am looking into air plants, and trying to figure out what bromes, and orchids might work in my setup that I can mount to the background or on treefern / cork and attach that to the background.

Any advice on that topic is more then welcome!
