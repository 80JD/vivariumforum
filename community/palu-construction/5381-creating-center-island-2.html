Here is a view of the tank we plan to use as our Paludarium. The Canopy hinge opens to 9.5" so we can squeeze a 5.5GAL tank in there for sure along with another tank as long as it is higher than 10" (we plan to keep the water level at least 9.5"


The style of tank is really really unique and has many options for modifying and retrofitting. As you can see it would make a wonderful Paludarium...


Giving you an idea of the depth of the tank. Its nice and wide and not too short (29.5" X 17.75")

Top of the canopy (which is a part of the Aquarium and can not be removed without damaging) has vents, slats and holes for all sorts of add on equipment. Its a European Style tank and I believe we are the only ones in the area with this type of tank.

It came standard with 2 X 13W T5 lighting and tray filtration unit. We plan to remove the filtration unit and add extra lights for yet even more light along with a moonlight for night viewing.
