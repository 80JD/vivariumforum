Do be aware that limestone will GREATLY alter the pH of your water. Most likely to the point of burning the amphibians delicate skin. Be sure to get a pH test kit to monitor the water for a few weeks if you are dead set on trying this product. You'd have to cure it and leach it similarly to the way concrete must be sealed and leached (please note that cure and leach time for concrete is different than that for grout, which most of us use).
