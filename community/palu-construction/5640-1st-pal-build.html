1st Pal build
Hey guys this is my 1st post, so hoping it's in the right section.

Just completed my 1st Pal build and thought i'd post my pics and get your feed back on what you think and any advice you may have.

I started with custom built tank 3ft high x 2ft x 2ft, with sliding front doors and full mesh top. I had 2 overflows drilled into the base, one for the land area and one for the water area. I had the return drilled in the top left corner and a cord port drilled into the top right corner.

I siliconed in a glass petition to separate the land and water areas. I built the rear rock wall by stacking up styrofoam pieces, leaving a channel in the back wall for the return hose that would force water out of spray bars behind the rocks in the water area. And built a reservoir in the top right corner that flows down as a waterfall.
Once i'd shaped the styrofoam i gave it 3 coats of a mix of cement, silasec (concrete water proofer) and bondcrete.
While the last coat of cement mix was still wet i threw a mix of different coloured sands at it and then let it cure for about 2 weeks. I then hosed off any loose sand, then once it was dry applied 2 coats of clear pondtite.

For the land area i placed eggrate on pvc stilts topped with a volcanic rock for drainage, then on top of that fly screen topped with sphagnum moss. I siliconed pieces of malle root to the edges of the glass petition to make for a smoother transition to the water and to hide the glass.

Plumbed up both overflows into the sump beneath, which holds 2 pumps, one to run the return spray bars and water fall and one to run the rain system which is made from garden irrigation spray nozzels.

I've planted it with broms, native Australian rock orchids, ferns and baby's tears.

Anyway hope you like it. Any feed back would be great.
