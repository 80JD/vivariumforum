"Okinawa" Project
Here is some of the preliminaries of this large project designed for Cynops ensicauda popei

I'll post a few pics each day to show you folks the construction process.





This is an oblique view of the Upper Cabinet. While not drawn to scale the dimensions are as follows:

48 inches long
17 inches high (back dimension)
19 inches wide
6 long inches (front dimension and top)
17 1/8 inches X 48 inches for the angled Polycarbonate cover
6 X 48 inches (front and upper Polycarbonate panels)

And here it is with the fans for temperature and humidity control installed:




The case includes (not pictured here) a programmable thermostat/hygrometer that controls the fans, Timer for the lighting, Lighting and a water pump. I'll show these in detail in the construction photos. The main window opens forward and has a retractable mesh suspension system to minimize escapes while doing maintenance. Water testing and changes can be done without opening the main case. Also not pictured is the "feeding" port for getting live foods into what will hopefully be a nearly escape proof design for both critters and live foods.

My goal here is to build the entire project, including landscaping for 300 USD or less. So far I am exactly 128.95 USD into it, most of the cabinet materials have been purchased.

Correct. These newts go through a relatively long terrestrial phase once morphed, so the water feature will be about 5 inches in depth at most in the pond end. The bulk of it will be the waterfall and stream running through it.

The retractable mesh sides for the "door" are merely plastic window screening rolled around a spring loaded shaft, the "business" edge of which is affixed to the edges of the main window panel. The panel is hinged at the bottom, so when opened outward, the screening is kept taught. When closed, the screen rolls back up, where it is hidden in the frame. I am not sure if I can truly explain it in text very well, but the pictures will be up soon...
