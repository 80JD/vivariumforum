By DF's do you mean dart frogs? Palus with a major water feature are not suitable for darts. A relatively small feature, with multiple points of exit would be fine. However, if you are planning the typical palu with 6-8" of water and a smaller land area, then you will need to reconsider the inhabitant chosen.

Added difficulty is definitely keeping the water clear, and having the correct levels to have healthy plants and fish (Ammonia, Nitrite, Nitrate, pH, Hardness, Chlorine) A palu is just like an aquarium, with the additional maintenance of a viv
