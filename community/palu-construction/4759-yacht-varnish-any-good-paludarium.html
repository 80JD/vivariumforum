I think it's doubtful that the company will condone using this product for this purpose, as it could be considered a bit of a liability for them, and certainly wasn't designed with this use in mind. The only company I know of who claims their products can be used in this way is West Systems, who make marine epoxies and have stated that their products are safe for aquarium construction.

See what they say, though- they might surprise you. Also, a lot of people here, myself included, use products for other than their intended purpose to no ill effect.
