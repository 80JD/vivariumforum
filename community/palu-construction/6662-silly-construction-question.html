Yes making a false back chamber is a method to be able to get at your pump. It is still a hastle however, since you will need to get behind the tank, or turn it, and remove the top, and shove your arm down the chamber. Next time I do a complicated water feature I will be using a sump for the pump and filter. Its more expensive, but its ay easier that way.
