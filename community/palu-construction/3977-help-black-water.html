Yeah, that's basically all you can do. Woods are the the major producers of tannins which cause the brown/black water. I'm not exactly sure which woods produce the most tannins, but I just recently got a piece or mopani wood and it seems to be leaching quite a bit. I never really new that LECA/coco fibers have tannins, though they are dusty and can cloud your water tas well.

Ideally you could soak the wood in a separate container, changing the water everyday, until the wood does not tint the water anymore. But in reality you could probably be soaking the wood for a month, so I just stuck my wood in a after a day of soaking. Having a good filter with a lot of carbon (chemical meadia) will help reduce the pace at which the water becomes tinted, but eventually your just going to have to do water changes when it gets bad. Hopefully after a while the wood will stop leaching. Good luck!

Get as much of the heavy particulate out as you can by doing water changes, then if you still have issues you can put SeaChem Purigen into your filter. It absorbs tannins very quickly. It works great. Turned my first Paludariums water from looking like a cup of strong brewed tea, into crystal clear water in just a few hours.

Now I use it in all my tanks, whether they house fish, frogs, or a combination of both. It also absorbs toxins, and can be used as a carbon replacement. But, unlike carbon it wont strip out micro-nutrients for the plants, so it's safe for planted tanks.
