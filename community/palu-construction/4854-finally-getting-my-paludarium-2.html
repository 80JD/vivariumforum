My waterfall posed a problem since I'm still feeling really unsteady about the ability of water to penetrate my terrestrial area :[

Granted, I REALLY dont want to do this so my plants will have more soil, but what can ya do.
If any of you have a firm belief that if I cover my GS land border with UF2, then silicon it, and it will not be penetrable by water.. I may still try to do that. This was my initial plan anyways, but the first terrarium I ever made had the issue of flooding :[

So I've been working quite a bit lately and havent had much time to work on my paludarium! :[
But, I have done quite a bit of work since the last time I posted! I decided to allow water under the land area as a false bottom rather than praying that it would stay waterproof! For my tank, I really feel like this is a plus. I am going to have an air stone under the false bottom so I can have increased oxygen and water circulation. I am in hopes that this will improve my ability to culture small crustaceans ( Freshwater SCUDS and Brine Shrimp ).

Also, I went plant hunting out in the woods where i grew up.. because of a spring fed creek that I remembered! Sure enough, I get there and right as I step foot in the woods all I see is moss. It reminded me of Fern Gully! haha. So I made a killin with the moss/fern game!
I'll post a few pictures of all of these things in a second, but I do have one question.

The Aquarium I have my clams in is outside on the pool deck in the sunlight. The water is pretty clear, where it does not get direct insane amounts of sunlight, but the parts that do get quite a bit of sunlight are going ape with algae! I'm pretty sure it is hair algae, which is perfect for my clams anyways, so I've been letting it roll! I'll post pictures of this as well after I mortar some rocks while it's still daylight! Let me know what ya think!
