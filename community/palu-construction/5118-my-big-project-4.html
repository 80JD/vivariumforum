hmmm... anything alive still currently in the tank?

if not soak up what you can by pressing dry portions of a towel folded up into the wet area. then lay the tank on its side and blow a fan into one end and let the air flow out the other end. you'll need to adjust the angle and placement of the fan as best as you can. what you want to do is keep a leg or something in front of the opposite end of the tank from the fan, point the fan straight in the other end and feel for the most airflow across your legs while you adjust the angle it blows in. once you have the most flow coming out you have the most blowing across the surface and you let it sit for a day or 3. take it easy during that time checking it no more than twice daily. it'll dry, no need to stress over it.
