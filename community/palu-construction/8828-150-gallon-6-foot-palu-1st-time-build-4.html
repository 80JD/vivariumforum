Im not sure about the two different frogs (not really a frog guy, more reptiles) but the anoles and frogs should live fine together considering the amount of space...they put them together at the pet stores and though many die-hard animal lovers would say that they are stressed and too crowded, the animals survive and even have better lives than they would in the wild despite the "harsh" conditions.

As for the cichlids, since they are pretty aggressive fish and the water area so large and deep they may eat the frogs and lizards that fall in.....probably wont happen, but if it does they larger animals stand a better chance at not drowning when not being nibbled on haha. More passive fish might be more friendly, but I'm sure the cichlids will work just fine if you're determined to keep them.
-Jeremy

I currently have green tree frogs and green anoles together, and there have been know problems. With a tank your size you have lots of room, and with lots of visual barriers it shouldn't be a problem. I think that a large group of fire belly toads would love it in there. I wouldnt mix green tree frogs with red eyes, the retf stress very easily.
