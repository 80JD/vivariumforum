to start off welcome to the site and good luck on your venture.

now to offer insight. a few good reasons people don't often create vivs for iguanas. 1 they are plant he!!. they are very much into eating plant matter and with their size can trash most house plants fairly easily just slouching around let alone tail whips. the double whammy! one reason people don't often mix iguanas is not because of aggression. it's because they are delicate when it comes to disease. for this reason it is commonplace to feed babies and/or juvies a small portion of feces preferably from a wild caught adult to boost it's immune system (inoculation). this could prove to be he!! on the fish.

if you understand the risks and take the precautions i see no reason why it won't work. a baby will be much easier to accommodate, especially plant wise.

I got this pink styrofoam that is compacted for perimeter insulation on building foundations. Its more dense then the great stuff and even the normal white packing type. This is i deal when using a cement like coating because flexing makes the cement crack. Im probly redoing the stream bed in the pink stuff cause it came out so much better.

I used a hammer claw on the edges and a stiff wire brush to shape and scuff up the foam so the cement grabs the pores of the styrofoam.

You can see the background foam also. I used a product that is waterproof when cured i cant remember the name because i got it for free but its similar to the grout or mortar process others have used. I just used a latex glove to spread it out and fill any cracks then blotted it to give it more texture. I used silicone and screws to hold the ledges on. I used about 3 screws on each, one straight in on the middle and the other 2 are angled towards the center so they are fighting each other as they try to pull away from the wall.

Let me know if anyone sees any problems with my filter design.I assume just rearranging the filters orientation wont change its filtering capabilities. Any insight is helpful seeing this is my first build. The entire height is less then 4 inches and will be fully submerged in the water and not sticking out and 1, taking up more space from my Iguana both laterally and vertically. and 2 i dont have to try to blend it into the landscape like before. I just need to get a hose to feed the water from the outlet on the filter to the water bowl at the top of the stream. alright thats it for now... - Dallas
