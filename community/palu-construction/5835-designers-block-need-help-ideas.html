That's definitely a good point regarding adding extra water into the system.

I bet if you picked your plants right you could get by with a fogger. The trouble is that they're not terribly reliable, especially when they're installed inside the vivarium. Another thing is that it's pretty tough to control which way the fog is going to go, especially with any kind of air movement in the tank. Play around with one beforehand and you'll see how finicky they are. There's also some chance of a risk to your animals, from what I've read, but I haven't had any experience there.

misting systems are easy to set up believe it or not. i don't know about other brands but mist king systems seem like there's pretty good chances of having it leak proof once your finished. if you order the timer with it too you probably won't have to worry about over watering as it's a seconds timer. the premium nozzles and the zip drip modules really help in the event you have nowhere to put your reservoir but above your pump since it really does a great job keeping the system from constantly dripping.

don't count a misting system out if you're planning on a planted b/g. it'd be nice to be able to aim your watering where you need it and it is really cool to see your tank rain as opposed to constantly spritzing it.

if you're trying to stay on a budget, i understand though.

clay balls work fine. the names are usually brand names. however we do use leca as a generic term. as long as you don't have too much fun playing with them we don't really care what you call them.

i would go with a 3" pipe. it should give plenty of room for access with a shop vac as well as minimize the amount of space used for its purpose.

plexiglass and silicone is definitely a short term solution. they don't play well together over the long run. i would suggest either going with an acrylic tank and using an acrylic/plexi adhesive composed of methylene chloride or sticking with only glass for your divider. one thing i would suggest if you choose to go with glass is to knurl the edges. in the event that the glass gets exposed somehow it will be much safer as it will eliminate the possibility of your pets cutting themselves wide open on sharp edges.
