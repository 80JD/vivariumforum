paludarium build log
this is my first attempt at constructing any kind of terrarium. im using an exo terra 18 - 18 - 24 with the intention of using the area below the door line as an aquatic zone in a paludarium type setup. over the coming weeks of the build il try and add pics and update as often as possible.

the plan is to keep things simple. i want it to be easy to access and maintain. iv kept marines and reef aquariums before and know how complex some systems can get when you need 3 seperate extension plugs for all the equipment, so here im going to try and keep it minimalistic as i can,.

im going for a terraced design, sloping up to the rear corner of the tank. the bottom level will be a roof for an open cave in which fish can swim about and aklso will be where water drains through from the planted zones above, (kind of a bio filter)

water will be pumped from under the cave up to a waterfall/stream (not really sure yet). an additional pump or maybe the same one depending on available pressure will supply a spray nozzle system at the top for watering.

not sure what livestock will be included yet so im building with an open mind...

So, heres a couple of pics of how the terrarium looks out of the box and some of the equipment i brought to kick off the construction.

I abandoned my search for conventional egg crate and opted for this plastic patio tile type material. as i want to have a viewing window into the cave, the green colour will be less conspicuous too.

The plan is to keep the generic rock BG siliconed to the back wall, and create a surface on the adjacant wall from polystyrene sheet. the expanding foam will cover both of these when the time comes, but at least with the exo terra BG i dont need to put expanding foam right to the edges and it will stil look fairly natural. plus i want to try leaving some areas where the rock BG is exposed to see if it gives a nice effect or not.

heres how the palu looks in its current progress.

as you can see the generic BG has been chopped up to make supporting legs for the terraces.

some PVC tubing is also propping it up. i intend to have another PVC pipe running up the back corner to feed the hose from the pump through to the top of the tank

polystyrene covers about 80% of the left side which will be covered in expanding foam

I know it looks kind of boxy at the moment but this shape will be broken up when i giv it all a generous coating of expanding foam. and the gaps are intentional. here pices of malaysian bogwood will be mounted to break up the shape a bit too. the hole in the bottom right will remain an entrance to the cave for the fish and for me to hide the pumps and heaters
