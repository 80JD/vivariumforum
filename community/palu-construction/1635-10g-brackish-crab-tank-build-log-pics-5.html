One way I've set up tanks of 20gal ( high) and up to a 45 gal is to forget the whole eggcrate thing, simply divide the tank part way on a diagonal into land and about 1/3 water and use a ReptoFilter (Hagen) as a short waterfall hideable w/ driftwood and a cork back wall w/ a bit of Great Stuff filler.
That's an oversimplified description but it has many variations & can be cheap, fast, and beautiful. Also easy to maintain.
