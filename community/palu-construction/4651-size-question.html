Are you planning to turn the tank on end and make it a vertical space, or were you planning to use it the way it was intended? If you don't turn it on end, it isn't tall enough for any tree frog, except tiny tiny ones, or babies. Tree frogs need lots of vertical space.

Now, can a RETF live in a tank that size as long as it is oriented properly? It would most likely be fine, but is at the minimum that I would use for a larger tree frog such as a RETF.
