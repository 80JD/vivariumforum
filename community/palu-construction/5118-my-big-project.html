Im assuming since you have egg crate on your list you are planning a false bottom???.... If so it should be easy to hide both a pump and a filter underneath it. Or you could hide a pump under a group of rocks, in a small cave, or behind a bunch of submerged plants.

As for the fogger....A humidifier such as the one Demon is using will be better then a ultrasonic mister, as you can attach tubing and run/branch it where ever you want.

For a drain some people just syphon out water when they notice the level raising to high. Others drill a hole in the side of the tank at the correct level and use a valve to drain it into a bucket...And others will even attach this drain to their home plumbing lines...It all depends how complex and self maintaining you want your tank to be.

Not sure what is the best way to keep the soil from becoming oversaturated, but the tank I am building now has a false bottom with a planting area. I have siliconed window screening (double layed) to the egg crate so that the soil can drain with out the substrate getting washed away into the false bottom. I have blocked off the planting area with greatstuff foam and sillicone to make sure that very little if any soil can get through the cracks.

People sometimes use a lecca + layer of moss + then the soil technique to keep their substrate out of the water. Although I havn't tried this technique before, it seems like a mess waiting to happen.



My mental observation- I think that it would look better if you install the fog tubing near the top of the viv background, maybe in a small, concealed pit or cave. . That way the fog will drift down, looking like the fog came from further up the "cliff/mountain" instead of mysteriously emerging from a hole in the middle of the background.
It would also look good coming from the source of the waterfall.

Wow, those branches are pretty neat. I bet I could make something similar to that with some of the thin open cell foam sheets I have. I'd be willing to make them and sell you some, hehehe... Seriously though, I bet I could make that easily, and moss will grow better on foam if that's what you're looking to do.

One benefit to having a FB is that you will have a larger volume of water, which will give you better water quality and stability. Yes, siphoning water out will lower the water level in your pond, but you'd generally be doing that when it's time to clean the pond anyway, just like with a fish tank. If you keep misting and misting and never removing water, your pond is going to turn your land into a swamp, and then a lake!

Your design looks pretty good. One suggestion I have is that instead of having a straight line where your land meets the water, you should make it more dynamic. Add some curves, make it more natural. You're not likely to see a square edge between the land and a pond in nature.

I agree with Dazzle about the fog, you should have one spot coming out of the waterfall. It will cascade down the water, which looks totally cool. Also, put the outputs higher than in your design. the higher they are, the more natural it will look, and the more life you can have growing on your BG.

If you're interested, I'll be listing some tree ferns in the Classifieds here shortly. I'll be selling plain unproven roots, and proven roots (the proven roots have ferns growing out of them already) each root section will be between 2 and 4 inches long. They are easy to grow, you just staple it to the BG and cover with moss. The roots will put out ferns that will grow through the moss. They stay relatively small and look really great. They would be nice for your current dart setup as well. They can take a decent amount of heat as long as they are kept hydrated, so they are good to use in just about any setup.
