I have decided to simplify my intentions for the fans(for the 3rd time now) I am simply going to wrap them in mosquito netting, and silicone one side to the glass top pointing at the 2 seams of the front panes of glass, at a 30 degree angles(30 deg to the front glass). I think this will make the best circulation without blowing a breeze through my trees, keep my glass clear, and keep them out of the frogs reach. He has been ALL over my10 gallon, but I've never seen him walk upside down on the glass top, so I think this is the safest bet.
