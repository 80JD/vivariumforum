Thanks! It's really nice to get feedback!
The tank is 18" x 18" x 24" with GS, silicone, peat, and ghost wood

I didn't take that many pic.'s during construction because I was just kind of making it up as I went along (and I know this sounds silly, but I didn't want to jinx). I wanted to make a kind of grotto effect with plenty of private places for the darts to feel comfortable retreating to if they wanted.
The water is about 3" deep, but is graded by 3 little platform/steps so that anyone who finds themselves in the water could make an easy exit. There was supposed to be a waterfall down the central log, but the wood had cracks that I couldn't seal, so it leaked way too much water into the substrate. I tried to tweak it several times, but it never worked, so I nixed the water fall.
Everyone here was so kind and helpful with all of my questions, I really wanted to show them what I was working on. Thanks everyone!
