5 1/2 gallon build
So I originally made this tank for my brown mantellas but they have proven to be to curious... They found their way to get back into the pump area, get stuck between the lid and back ground and they just jump out at me when i get into the tank so it was a complete fail. But I used this tank more as experience and I think it came out pretty good. Here are the pics.

Now if any one has any suggestions as to what I could put in here throw them at me because Ive got nothing.

Now the making of the tank..
I used great stuff pond and stone for the back ground and pool area. I sealed the potters foam in first though, which is the water fall area. And for cement I used commercial grade quikcrete resurfacer. I should also add that I did four layers. And my thanks go out to beck for the youtube video.
