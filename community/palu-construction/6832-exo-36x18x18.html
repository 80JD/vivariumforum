Sorry, are you trying to use the beckett pump on the misting system? That will never work, there is not enough pressure (different from the flow rate). I use a beckett for my waterfall, but its just not built to do that job. The impeller is a magnetic shaft and its meant to move a lot of water through an unrestricted hose. Any significant pressure build up and that impeller will just stop spinning.

You can get pumps similar to what mistking offers in local hardware stores, but they are still costly ($90-100) and then you still have to plumb them to fit 1/4" hoses. By the time you get done, its easier to just order it from mistking, IMO.

You would be looking for a small well pump/diaphragm pump which is usually in the plumbing section and my guess is you will need to know how much PSI you will need. Too much and you could pop a leak.

If you wanted to get fancy, I think you could tap into your plumbing, run an RO filter and use a sprinkler system solenoid.

Hope this helps.
