Hey the rocks and progress look great.
For your background, it is waaaaay easier to put the GS on something rather than applying directly to the glass. REmovable, eaisier to clean and so on. You can cut egg crate to be the right size or use styrofoam sheets found at home depot in the insulation section. You can use either silicon or titebond to attach the substrate. If you use silicon, brown looks much better than black or clear because it matches the color of the substrate. Titebond is wood glue that can be found at home depot and is a yellowish color, but dries much harder and more solid than silicone.
Coco fiber and peat moss will both work for the background. Honestly, it doesn't matter what you attach as long as you get the look you're trying for. Im currently using titebond 3 (it doesnt matter what number u get, they're just improvements to the product) and jungle bed from petsmart. I wouldnt order coco fiber when u can stop by any pet store and get bedding that looks great.
THe only thing you wanna look out for is that a lot of stuff like peat moss or orchid bedding and stuff like that sold at home depot and lowes and places has fertilizer that is not good for animals in a contained environment. You're better off just going to the local pet shop and spending a little more to get something safe and saving the hassle of shipping substrate.
