10G Brackish Crab Tank: Build Log with Pics.
Here was my start a while back



I have done alot sense then just dont have pictures uploaded.

The water leaked though so i have a different plan to fill it up past the little "dam" that you see there in the front. the only problem is now the water on the left side of the "dam" doesnt move very much at all.

Any ideas on how t get it flowing? What is the smallest power head/water pump that i can get?

You will see in updated pictuers that all the greatstuff was coated with white/black silicone mixed to look grey its highlighted and shadowd and it all looks like rock, i love how it turned out and im very satisfied.

Also, where the filter is. I wrapped it in saran wrap and coated it in crisco i then surounded it with more foam, after it dried the filter poped out with some cutting of foam and then i carved it too look like rock, i made a little cave at the bottom so that the water could get to the filter. It has a peice of net to keep the crab from getting in there.

Inside the filter will be filter sponge on layer one the carbon in a bag then some nylon mesh then some window screen with some black gravle on top the crab will be able to get into it and should be like a little shallow pool.

The tank will be brackish water and i do plan on keeping bb gobies. I will be using a small pad heater rated for up to 5 gallons i will slip this into the fliter and push it up to the back of it.

Light will not be aquarium it will come from an allspectrum reading lamp.

ill get some update pictures later tonight.

Thanks for reading leave feedback and sugjestions please
