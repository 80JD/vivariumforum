expanding foam update
ok, so the expanding foam is done. it worked out pretty well i think considering it was my first experience with the stuff.

i started from the bottom and worked my way up. the bogwood was mounted in the foam as i went along. i also bought a nice piece of knotted root wood. not really sure where it came from but it looks great. hopefully i can attatch some little orchids to the branched roots.

i had to use wire to hold the pots in place before i foamed them in. only had room for 2 pots with all the wood. but its ok because there is plenty of room for planting on the terraces.

hope you enjoy the pics :-)
