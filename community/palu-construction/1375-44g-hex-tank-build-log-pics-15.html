Quote:
Originally Posted by Waterkeeper
plants breathe CO2. its like us having too much oxygen.
It's interesting you used that example because you can have too much Oxygen.

Too much Oxygen can damage lung cells and prolonged exposure can be fatal to humans. The air we breath is only 20% Oxygen. Some have a misconception that pure Oxygen is OK because they give it to patients with respiratory problems, but those patients are also breathing in trace amounts of the air as well.

Too much Carbon Dioxide is now believed by scientists to increase starch levels and cause plants to sweat less. Neither of which has been proved detrimental, but it's important we realize that any change in an environment has an effect on the life depending upon it.

Now science geekiness aside, I doubt the high levels in this example were sustained long enough to have any effect. I'd also like to add I've thoroughly enjoyed reading about your build process as I prepare for my first build.
