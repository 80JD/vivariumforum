Baking should be enough to get rid of the mold. Also, some air circulation would help. Mold generally grows in a new vive anyway, but having the air circulating (even if it is just the air in the tank, rather than fresh air from outside) will generally get rid of the problem. If you don't want to install PC fans, you can just set a fan to blow into the tank for a few days, and it should help (that should be fine since there are no pets in there yet, plus you wont have to remove everything. It won't hurt to give it a try for a few days, and if that doesn't work try something else).

I did NOT know you had a PLANT planted in your filter! That is the coolest thing! And it makes SO much sense, the plant will aide in the filtration. I just have a sponge in the top of mine, growing a healthy crop of algae, since the water is under one of the heat lights. I LOVE the idea of using gravel and putting a plant in it. That is the best thing about your whole tank, lol... So many people can use that idea! There are so many great things about the way you did everything, but that's my favorite.
