Thanks momerath!
Quick update (and I appologize for my lack of pictures... *sigh* will get them loaded when I have the time to mess with it):

Yesterday morning I re-smothered the right side of the cave where I found the leak that led to the log. Also filled the leak from the top waterfall, did some major moss attachment to the background (want more though! lol), and filled in the leak from the bottom creek/waterfall area.

Just finished a water test about 15minutes ago. No water under the log *YIPPEEE!!!!*
The other two leaks from the creeks are not leaking anymore. The mosses are able to stay moist from the two creeks, and even have a nice dripping effect going on.

A couple more things to fix though... the main waterfall can't hold all the water that is flowing into it, and is steadily dripping water into the planting area again. So, I'll attend to that once everything is dry again. The top creek is slowly dripping water into one of the planting cups, but a bit on silicone will fix that.

There are two patches of moss that I think will have a hard time soaking up enough water for them to do well, so I am going to install cotton wicks underneath the moss. That way I don't have to worry about the sheets drying out.

The water is rather murky, and I'm going to dump water over everything when it's all done so as to get rid of extra dust/dirt.
