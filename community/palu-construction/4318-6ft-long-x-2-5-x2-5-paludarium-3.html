I can't imagine why the green tree frog would be so expensive... Could you post a picture of the frog you are talking about so I can see if it's the same one I'm thinking of? I know you aren't in the states, but that's an incredibly high price to pay for something that's only around 6 dollars here.

The P. leucomystax can swim, yes. They stay on land more than they go in the water, but they do go in the water, especially if frightened. I doubt they'll eat fish. They like great big crickets and other similar bugs. If you DO catch a couple for your palu, you can just catch bugs for their supper from your yard.

There's a type of frog here in WA that would be perfect for your setup. It is a land frog that also likes the water, so it swims and hops around on land. They get about the same size as the P. leucomystax. I caught one once, and I was going to keep it, but I decided to let it go because i raised some tadpoles and was just SURE! that I had a baby one... Nope... So, I'll have to catch another this spring, maybe... It's too bad there's no way to export any of the native critters here in WA...
