It would help if you posted a picture of your progress so far to give me an idea of your layout.

But if the glass isnt covered in anything just yet, I would use expanding foam on each side to give it a gradual slope. Before you do this however, add silicone to the glass so that it will hide the foam added. Then you can cut away the excess silicone to the exact perimeter of foam used touching the glass.

Check out my build thread "Grimms vivarium Build" as I have a water/land separator similar to this. The only difference is that I didnt use glass first, and it ended up with a slow leak to the land protion.

The silicone on glass that is viewable, I did 1 thin coat to ensure no bubbles, then 1 thick coat after it had cured.

Why dont you like the look of it, I dont get it? It creates a natural looking slope, then you can cover it with silicone and pebbles like you mentioned on your other thread. Much better then a straight unnatural piece of glass.

If you arent willing to change the design, then I would go get some 2000-3000 grit sandpaper and smooth the edges of the glass....Or just cover it with substrate and moss....Or silicone.
