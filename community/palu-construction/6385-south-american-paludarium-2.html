I've had saltwater reefs for years. The larger hole was for the durso, which is a quiet way to get water to over flow into a sump, the 1.5 inch hole was for the return line. They pumped water from the sump, into a pvc return line there.

The holes in the back were for closed loops, used for water circulation. Basically, one hole would feed an external pump, the other hole was the return line. It's important for a lot of water circulation for reef tanks.
