You could use something to stabilize the slope, like eggcrate or screen... For example, in my newt tank, there is a rather steep slope from the water portion to the land portion. I have used gravel as the substrate and it is what makes the slope. I have a piece of screen on the slope to minimize erosion, and moss has grown down the screen and into the water, further stabilizing the slope.

That's a gorgeous tank you used as an example. Thanks for that one, I'll have to dream up something similar sometime soon.

Its too bad the link doesnt show any pics during the build. The substrate is said to be aquarium soil. Im sure the soil sticks to itself better then gravel would. Also, the large rocks will help keep the slope in place.

If you want some unexperienced advice then maybe try this out.....Maybe place 1-3 pieces of plexi, plastic, or glass vertically at different heights. Drill holes in each piece to allow water flow, and silicone them in place.

Or you could run mesh the length of the tank, and silicone the ends to the glass. This would be the best method to ensure water flow, yet still act as a retaining wall.

Im just throwing ideas out there so they may work, or may not.
