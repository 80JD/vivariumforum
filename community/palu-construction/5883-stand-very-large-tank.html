Stand for very large tank
Hi there, I am going to be getting a large tank soon but have no stand for it. It will be turned into a paludarium so wouldn't be as heavy as a normal tank. Can anyone give some advice for building a cheap functional stand (doesn't have to be pretty) to support the weight? I have been browsing furniture shop websites but no hall table or anything reaches more than 2/3 of the length. The tank size is 2 metres by 1.2 metres by 60 centimetres (for non-metric people I think that's about 6 1/2 feet by 4 feet by 2 feet). Thanks a lot!

Also, there don't seem to be any posts on this, but has anyone simulated a complete contained ecosystem in a paludarium and not had to add anything to it at all? I wonder if it's possible to get nitrogen/iron/phosphate/CO2/oxygen monitors so you can tell that everything's going smoothly, and whether you need to intervene or not (like if the CO2 drops, you might add some more fish)?

the bullet case? that's only because you'd need it completely custom. most tanks are not custom and have several fabricated stands that would cost a lot less than building one yourself to choose from.

almost every aquarium shop is building their RACKS purely as a functional piece of equipment. if you want to make yourself a beautiful viv to compliment or as a show piece in your living room and then have it sitting on 2x4's that's one thing. if, however you want it too look like appropriate furnishing for the room then 2x4's are just added cost since you'll at least want to wrap it in some veneer most likely... even if it is wood grain. there is a difference between a functional rack system and a tank stand to furnish your living room with.

2x4's will also add to the total weight of the stand making it a bear to move when it comes time. my stand was made with 2x4's and i regret not looking further into it beforehand. mostly because i can park my car on top of my tank and the stand itself weighs way more than i want to move in the event i had to. i've done demolition for a living before and i'll definitely say, i'm not looking forward to breaking the thing down at all. i've had six friends suspend they're weight on it right after we filled the tank (so added to the total weight) and i tried to knock it over and it wouldn't budge. sounds great, but not practical at all. i will be peeling carpet out of the concrete with a spade with a 6' handle when it's time to move the tank.

once your done waterproofing, sanding, sealing, building, trimming, leveling, making shelves, adding door hardware and then you add up the total price... with most tanks... check out a couple online retailers (big al's, that pet place, drs. foster and smith, etc.). i'll bet they have something in stock they could ship to you for at least $100 less than you payed in materials in most cases. with bigger tanks you likely would be better off shopping local due to shipping costs. at any rate, it's no big concern for somebody with money to blow that wants something specific and has the tools and know-how to pull it off, but for majority of people they have no idea what they're getting themselves into until the cost and time invested is overwhelming.
