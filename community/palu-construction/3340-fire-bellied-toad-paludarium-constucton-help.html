i need to know what you want... you can use various types of foams to make structures... you can use eggcrate to form them... you can use silicone and many materials to make a facade... you can use many types of cretes, grouts, sealers, paints depending on where each piece will be... you can use hooks to hang planters and such... there's a lot of possibilities... just remember to leave wiggle room and check fit relentlessly and repeatedly if you decide to make structures outside of the tank and then put them in... i'd say an easy styrofoam and crete bg with a small (purchased) waterfall... use aquarium gravel or leca as a bottom layer and slope it over the bottom of the waterfall... fill the tank with water high enough for the waterfall to utilize it... add a bunch of wood and a few plants like anubias and bamboo that can have their roots directly in water... hang a couple potters in the back corners for some hanging plants like pothos... just make sure you slope the land area high enough that you can put some screen and coco fiber/moss/leaf litter and add a couple of hides... idk.. just a quick idea... if you want everything removable you will be giving up a lot of possibilities and it might prove worth it to spring for a slightly bigger tank... a more specific question would help... we're full of ideas, but it's really yours that needs to come together for you.

What are the dimensions of the tank (length, sides, front to back in the middle of the bow, and height)? A ten gallon sounds too small for two FBTs to me. I've never seen a ten gallon bowfront though, so I'm not sure.

If you are set on the ten gallon, I'd do a drop in BG if I were you, if you want one. They are easy, and you don't have to glue it in.

Pothos is good for a FBT paludarium, it can grow in water and on land, so it's quite versatile. They also like to sit on the leaves, which are strong enough to hold them.
