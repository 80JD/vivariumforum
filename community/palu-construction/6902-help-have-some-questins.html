Iheard you should use HCL to cure concrete. I tried using the concrete and am not a fan of it personally. I don't like having to mix more a little bit at a time every 5 minutes so I made up my own paint/sealant which is completely neutral in PH.
Here is the recipe:

Drylok paint (the kind for your basement) 4 parts
1.5 parts non toxic acrylic clear sealant (x-clear is the brand)
Concrete color or the colored acrylic paint of your choice (make sure paint is AP non-toxic)

Obviously, let it dry for about 2-3 days before adding water or putting under water

Note that if you use colored acrylic paint instead of concrete color you should add a little more of the sealant to the mix.

This mix has been tested for a few months now without any issues to test fish, snails, etc. Tested PH and water parameters and they are absolutely fine. Saw that you could use Drylok on the Caudata.org forum, and caudates are very sensitive so I am sure it is fine, but rarely does anyone in the paludarium hobby mention using it.
