rinsing it and letting it dry would not be a bad idea in case there's any residual dust from manufacturing or sitting around that would interfere with it adhering to surfaces. it should not be absolutely necessary though since it's a very porous material.

yes, for a b/g you'll want to silicone it in place. some people do cut slits into it while others will cut pieces and sew or tack them onto the piece they've hung to form a pocket. either way should work out fine. broms shouldn't have to be planted into it but i'm sure that'll be fine. i think mounting them might be a better idea. it's best to ask one of the plant guys with each specific brom species imo rather than making a blanket statement about broms though.

it should do fine in contact with water. misting and fogging should not water log it. it should drain quite well. if you can get moss to cover it than a cover will not be necessary, but you'll have to be sure the moss will do that and stay healthy under your care. not everyone can keep moss healthy. in the case that you're one of those people who can't or you're not sure it's probably best to put something of a substrate over it.

great stuff should seep right into it and shouldn't be a problem imo. just make sure you want it how you put it because the great stuff might prove impossible to remove completely.

did you order from first rays? he's right over in doylestown and it's likely you might have had a chance to meet up with him and look over what he has face to face. that might have helped you out a lot with a handful of questions. i'm not so sure he wants to meet with his customers but if i had the chance i'd probably go see things personally myself.
