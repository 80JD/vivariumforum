South American Paludarium
Okay so here is the deal I managed to score about a 300 gallon all acrylic tank for 50 bucks. No there is absolutely nothing wrong with it, the guy just had no idea what he had... gotta love craigslist. The dimensions are 8'W 2-1/2'D and 2' Tall. When I was younger I had a caiman crocodilus for about 5 years, when I had to move for school I ended up driving him to a snake farm in Texas and hoping he would have a better life there then if I would have sold him to some idiot. Now that I am a little more settled down and have even more room then before I really want another caiman, but this time just a paleosuchus palpebrosus. I'm looking for you fine peoples advice on setting up a nice paludarium for him. I'm looking for help on filtration, background, plants, and a small fish species that I can keep stocked in there for if he just happens to not be full of mazuri but won't kill my bank account if he takes a liking to them haha. I've never really had a nice tank that had filtration in it. My last caiman I just had to do water changes every couple weeks because he was a mess, which was very annoying. There are two holes in the bottom of the tank, I'm assuming for some kind of filtration, but one is 2" and the other is an 1-1/2". It also has 4 holes drilled in the back 2 with PVC L's and other 2 are just straight PVC.
I'm not an aquarium expert so I have no idea of how to connect all these into a waterfall integrated into the background. Should I just patch the holes in the bottom and use one of the tubes on the back for pumping the water back into a filter and up to the waterfall? Most of the questions I have are general palu kind of questions and I'm sure if any of you take the time to read all of this you will know what to say.



Sorry for the terrible picture by the way my girlfriend has the good camera ha. Uni is to show scale. If any of you want to see pictures of the arrangement of the holes just let me know. Thanks in advance and I hope to keep you all up to date with this.

Hi.
I know nothing about caiman. but as far as the filtration, you are very lucky that the tank is already drill so I would take advantage of the holes, you one of the bottom holes as the outlet hole that goes into a external filter and one of the holes in the back for the return (This could be a water fall)

Will you separate the water from the land by a piece of glass? if so you could use the other hole (assuming that it is on the opposite end of the tank) as a drainage hole so your land area doesnt get to wet.

Good luck and dont forget to post the pics

you're going to need bulkheads and fittings accordingly for those holes. a wet/dry filter is probably going to be your best bet on this tank. for noise reduction you can use a couple things like a durso standpipe (sp.). you might want to look into a fish forum with a saltwater section or a reef forum to straighten out your filtration. they'll know all the parts and be able to describe application and setting up your filtration pretty well.
