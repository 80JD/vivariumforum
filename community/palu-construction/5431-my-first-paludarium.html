Size shouldn't matter- there are lots of small plants available. How moist are you keeping the part of the background that doesn't have running water on it? That might determine what you're able to keep there. Keeping it evenly moist will give you a lot of choices as far as common vivarium epiphytes.

You could grow Anubias on the stone, too, if you wanted some foliage there- pick a small cultivar of A. barteri like 'nana' or 'petite'.

As far as lighting, I'm sure one of the round dome fixtures would be enough light. You could put a 40-something watt lamp in there and I bet everything would do really well. If you don't like the look of that there are always the Exo-Terra single-lamp fixtures, or an aquarium fixture for a nano tank. You could do something custom, too, of course.

I'd definitely go with a compact fluorescent- as many watts as you can safely fit. Incandescent lamps don't really provide a good amount of light for plants.

If the back wall stays somewhat moist all the time and you have a high general humidity within the tank, there are a lot of plants to choose from. How about a smaller orchid?
