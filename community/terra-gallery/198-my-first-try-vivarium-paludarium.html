some aquatics will realy set the tank off, i recomend anubias, cryptocornes and a nice patch of lilaeopsis. also some salvinia and riccia would be nice, maybe even a few java ferns. the cool thing about all of theese plants is that they will all do well out of water in wet substrate as well, so you could have some marshy areas if you wanted. also they are all very hardy and tolorate a wide range of conditions.

be sure and post some pics when you get the aquatics planted
