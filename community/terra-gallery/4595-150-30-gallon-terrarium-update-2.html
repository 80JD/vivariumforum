Originally Posted by phoenixflames44
would it be alright to replace the screen with about an inch of sphagnum moss???
Definitely go with the screen! If you don't all of your soil will work it's way down into your LECA or pea gravel. You can buy it at Lowes or Home Depot- it's a bendy synthetic sort of material that can be cut easily with scissors, and costs about $8.00 for more than you'll ever need. I would definitely go with the sphagnum moss too though. I put a really thick layer of it on top of the screen, and then threw my soil on top~
