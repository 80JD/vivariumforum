i am familiar with the grow bulbs that come with them. i would toss it in the trash now that i know better. i find i get better growth and overall plant health from sunlight bulbs from ge (5500 kelvin). even better is the daylight bulbs from ge (6500 kelvin). even better than both of them are the daylight spiral bulbs from ge commonly found at walmart. also my animals (fish and snake) as well as i seem not to like the grow bulbs that come with them. that light stresses my eyes and gives me a headache after a while. i could not look into my fish tanks for a long period of time and my plants were all dying until i switched from that very same bulb. just a heads up.

for your container issue, have you considered using storage bins? if you do it right you can hide them quite well. you can make the whole thing like a lawn ornament type spillway with false ground cover. then all you need to do is figure out how you want to disguise it to look natural. sounds like you may be looking for under bed storage bins to me.
