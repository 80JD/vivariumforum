looks pretty good for a first attempt.

i'd like some pics with better detail though. maybe cut the lights in the room and turn on the lights in the viv.

i'm not sure of your herp experience but for the most part on a first attempt a mixed tank per say is out of the question. it can be done but there are factors involved that suggest you should have a decent amount of experience with each particular animal (not just species) prior to introduction.

how we doin for heat... what's the humidity like... is the top closed in... give us some info to work with and suggestions should be better suited for your setup.
