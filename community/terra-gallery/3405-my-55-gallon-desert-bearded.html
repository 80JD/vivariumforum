Wow, this thread just came to my attention, and I must say I LOVE your BG. It's one of the best I've seen. I may work off of your design a bit when I do mine. I like that you used larger "rocks" in the design, rather than lots of small ones. It looks like a natural sandstone formation.

As for the comments against sand and for tile, I like using tile, and I took my guy off of sand (he wasn't ready yet and decided to eat alot of it, silly fellow), but I have noticed that with the tiles, his nails get worn down TOO much (as in to the bleeding point) due to the fine rough texture of the tiles. So, I'll be going back to sand very soon (a finer particle sand than I had before). Tiles are great for the clean up and are virtually no risk, but they do have drawbacks as well.
