Arkansas Walking Sticks
Wow... reading the only other thread in this forum it sounds like walking sticks are crazy rare... that's not the case in Arkansas.

Granted, they are VERY well camoflaged, but I usually find 10 to 20 walking sticks each year without looking (though, I've been told I'm an observant person... and I'm definitely a naturalist).

Hell, I live in the middle of town these days and found four or five while mowing and trimming the bushes this past year... when I go camping it's worse : )

But, as far as I know (to reflect on the other post), walking sticks are far from endangered in Arkansas (again, I can only speak for Arkansas regulations, to the best of my knowledge). But, walking sticks have always freaked me out a bit anyway, so I've never tried to take one home. That, in and of itself, is kind of weird... since I'm cool with snakes and spiders, but walking sticks make my skin crawl for some reason... I'll hold them, but it's a force of will, and god forbid one fall out of a tree onto my neck (happened last year while Dove hunting)... you haven't seen dancing until you've seen that : D
