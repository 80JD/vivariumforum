I'm still researching the specifics on concrete stain but this is what I have found so far.

Concrete stain can turn it dark - often it is just one or more types of iron oxide and/or carbon black/powdered charcoal.

Iron oxide can be had in yellow, red, or black forms, and by varying the amount of each of them you can get different shades of red, tan, brown, dark gray, or even almost black. Iron oxide can be bought at pottery supply stores or even on-line ( although shipping can get expensive if you have to get it shipped ).

You will have to make some trial pieces to see how much of what type of coloring agent will give you the color you want - don't forget it will appear darker when wet.
