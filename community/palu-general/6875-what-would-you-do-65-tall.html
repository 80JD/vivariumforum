I have seen many many pictures and I would like to do more of a land coming out of the back with a wood backdrop using my external canister filter to power a water feature. Honestly, I dunno which method of making a backdrop/land area you're talking about, but the process I'm talking about is where you carve and heat styrofoam/expanding foam into a rocky shape, cover it with cement or the such for a natural look, paint it to your likings, and then seal it with epoxy.
The only problem is that it can be a booger to install it in tanks with a brace like yours. In that case, you could build it from the inside.

simple crete background...

(you can carve/shape more detail if you wish... just remember to silicone it in place well so it doesn't float off)

i'd be inclined to do a tightbond slurry on what you're describing, personally.

you can also get some ideas on lizardlandscapes.com and the stickies on this site.

the bentonite clay would probably not be the best idea for running water unless you were going to put a few layers of epoxy clear coat over anything that will have running water. that could get shiny and unnatural looking.

great stuff and cork bark panels are also very flexible options.

You can always just use Great Stuff triple expanding foam, carve out what natural features you want, cover it in black silicone, and cover that with coco bedding. Ive used this a few times and it lasts for years and holds up great! If you do this though, make sure you work in small areas with the black silicone and coco bedding! it will dry out quickly if you try to cover the whole background at one time.
