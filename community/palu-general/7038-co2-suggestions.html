CO2 Suggestions
Hey guys, I just got done registering here. I've looked up several forums, and this seems to be the best one I've seen. You guys do a great job!! My question is about the CO2 levels for my paludarium. I am currently doing research to construct a 125 gallon paludarium with a 30 gal aquarium, and a 95 gallon vivarium. My plan is to have a 30 gallon sump to support the filtration system, pumps, heaters, etc..... I would like to put a CO2 system in the sump in order to give the plants in the paludarium the proper level of CO2. This is where i hit a snag. the water will flow from the sump to a double waterfall that flows into the main body of water. Will there be too much water agitation to maintain proper CO2 levels? If so, should i try to hide a CO2 system in the paludarium instead of putting it in the sump? Any help on this would be awesome. Thanks and GET TANKED!

yeah, between the sump and the waterfalls all co2 will do is constantly fluctuate, creating a BBA issue the likes of which have never been seen this side of the mississippi.

i would suggest maricide 14 day, gluteraldehyde, flourish excel, florin axis, api co2 booster, etc. ... a liquid substitute that's added once daily. most of these not only break down into co2 as an end product slowly to provide a 24 hour supply to the plants, but also are effective algaecides and/or biostats depending on application and concentration of each aqueous solution.

between planted tank, plant geek, barr report, ukaps, APC, aquaria central, etc. ... you should be able to figure out how to use most of these products for several purposes. however... the most straight-forward way is just to get the original (seachem's excel) and the least likely to be dangerous AFAIK is florin-axis.

HTH

as for specifics, i won't go into that right now. IMO, that would just narrow your options.

for ventilation... i'd say keep it easily removable for maintenance purposes. then you could cut up some thin filter pads, floor buffing pads, window screening, etc. and insert/attach into your vents and be able to get back to them to replace your filters or clean and/or replace your screen whenever you feel necessary. there are also inserts sold just for terrariums that already have a nice, tight grid on them that should work.

I would buy some type of diffuser for the CO2 and put it in the tank near the sump's outcurrent. A bell diffuser or the likes could work, and they're pretty simple to make. If that doesn't work, you can always bump it up a notch and go for a different type of diffuser. I myself made a bell diffuser out of the bottom of a soda bottle, and my plants are loving it.
