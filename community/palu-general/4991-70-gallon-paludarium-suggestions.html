Since you only have about 12" of hight above water that wont accomodate any sort of tree frog. But you do have quite a large amount of area so darts frogs may be a possibility. It all depends on what the finished tanks climate and plant life will consist of, what YOU want as a pet, and what amount of time and work you are willing to give to the inhabitants ...Usually when building a large tank like this you have a faily good idea of what will be living in the tank...That way you can build to accomodate the animals natural habitat.

Since you arent 100% complete building your viv I would suggest making a decision on what animals you will be keeping, and then finish the tank to the best of your ability for the selected animal.

Also the land plants you will add will need to be able to grow in the selected climat for the animal. You cant be putting low water/humidity plants in a tank with Dart Frogs. As for submerged plants I cant imagine it mattering to much, it really depends on your light intensity.

i would like to know the parameters of the tank and tap water and what level of care your dad's willing to do. the thing is that's close to 30 gallons of water... plenty for a wide variety of creatures. you could possibly house a small cichlid like a ram or some otos, habrosus or other dwarf cories (if your substrate's soft enough), a b/n pleco (if he's willing to feed fresh veggies and there's wood to rasp on), a couple peacock gudgeons (if he's willing to feed live or frozen foods), several shrimp species (if it'll be planted well with places to hide), long fin mountain minnows (i forget their common name) if it's cooler water, possibly a hillstream loach (with some consideration to it's needs). guppies are a great idea and i bet there's someone here that could just send you some babies... weather permitting. tetras... obvious choice... black neons are nice imo. harlequin rasboras, cherry barbs, synodontis negriventis (if you provide the hides for them... densely planted will help), malaysian trumpet snails if your ph is >7, ramshorn snails, a couple nerites (clithon corona, zebra, tiger... ) if you have enough algae to feed them, bridgessi, assasins, tylo's, thai micro crabs if it's planted with many nooks and crannies, maybe one crossocheilus langei (real siamese algae eater... has a black blotch between the belly and the anus), zebra danios, there's plenty crayfish that would do well... a dwarf gourami or betta (more likely females will do better with other inhabitants than males)... and i'm not real good with fish species...

if i had it to do it... ~30 black neon tetras, male longfin bristlenose pleco and ~12-30 red cherry shrimp... once i fishless cycled it and packed it with plants and wood.

lighting is pretty straight forward except intensity would depend on plants and placement and whether or not and how strong for uvb would depend on the animal. ~6500k-6700k is usually worth the purchase. co2 injection would help for your aquatic plants if you go too bright (>~60-90 watts). if you go that far you'll definitely need ferts for them too though.

land animal would depend on how the tank is set up. that's pretty deep water for some animals. many that could live in there and are good swimmers are likely to feed from time to time on your fish so you'd have to choose very wisely or put a lot of wood, plants, ect for them to get out of the water on.

what type of lid is on the tank and would changing it be an option? that could change things when it comes to your options also.

then there's plants... humidity, lighting, temp, wet or dry soil, mounted, planted, submerged, emergent, ferts, etc. leaves a long list even in a 70 gal tank. best to know what species is going on top animal wise to determine many of these variables.

how far have you got on the thing... is it just basically what you've said so far?

if i know any better a venus fly trap will need very moist, decaying soil and a cold weather dormancy period.

i would love to see pics and here more of your plans also. what types of animals have you guys considered and what do you guys know you don't want?
