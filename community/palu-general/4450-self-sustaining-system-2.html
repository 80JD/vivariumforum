I think you would need a much bigger tank to try and setup this kind of system. It needs to be big enough that the apex predator can roam around and encounter pray in different areas else the prey will just die out too fast.
Problem with doing terrestrial versus aquatic in a closed setup is that terrestrial usually need more surface area (I.e. Larger foot print) than an aquatic habbitat since they can use the full space filled with water.
Also waste from a land based animals are generally harder to filter than from fish, need a lot more bugs to break it down.

So yes it would be possible but need a lot of space per chain you want in your eco system.
