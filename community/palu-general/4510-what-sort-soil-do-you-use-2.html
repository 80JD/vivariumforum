here is my method

clay pellets from a local pets store, they have to be rinsed off to remove the dust and soaked in water to saturate them. it is designed for these applications so no worries.
I then spread them out over the bottom of the tank. They do soak up alot of moisture so to battle that I pour a couple cups of water down a pipe I set for this reason about every 2 weeks.
I then place a layer of landscapers cloth that is rinsed off over the pellets to seperate the rest of the layers from the pellets.

On top of that I put a mixture of 1 part repti bark to 3 parts unfinished compost, followed by finished compost .

Just to clarify unfinished and finished compost. I make my own from lawn clippings, ash from my fire pit, leaves , and the soils from my tank. I never use fertilizers, weedkillers, insect killers or lime so that isn't an issue. I only burn Oak or ash in the pit so that isn't a problem. I also don't drop in food....well unless you count the redworms that are used for composing and your a frog or lizard that eats them.
Unfinished compost still has large pieces of leaves, moss, and charcoal. thats why I don't use as much repti bark. The finished compost every thing has become soil except the charcoal which I pick out if I find any.
