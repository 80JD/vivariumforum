if it works by electricity harder water will be to your advantage as it has the minerals in it to support the electrical current. that's why you have to put salt in vaporizers if the water is too soft.

peat, wood, soil (without added minerals), etc. will soak up the salts/minerals and soften the water causing a lower tds (total dissolved solvents). when it does water becomes an insulator and keeps electricity from running current through it. i suspect this may be your issue.
