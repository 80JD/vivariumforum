20 Gallon Stocking
Hello, I am making a 20 gallon paludarium. I know it's small but we're building on the outside of the tank with screens to make it bigger. But right now I have black skirt tetra that I am buying more of once we get the bigger tank and start building, and I also have two guppies, which we will also be buying more. This tank will have about 15 gallons of water after we build up and out, thought it may go as high as 17 gallons. We are building this is a water sealed styrofoam with lots of different textures on it. Are the regulations to do with the size, or are you not allowed to have an aquarium or terrarium at all? If the latter is the case, it seems odd that you would be allowed to have a paludarium, since it is a combo of the two.

Whether you use screen, plexi, or foam will depend on the animal you'll be housing. Frogs need high humidity, so screen isn't going to work. If you want/need a background, plexi isn't going to work because the foam won't stick to it for long if at all). Your best bet will likely be either expanding foam sprayed on a frame of eggcrate, or foam insulation (white styrofoam type is very inexpensive and comes in a variety of sizes). The foam insulation will be the easiest.
