killifish would be good, also a betta would work, I put 6 platys in mine and they are doing well, they are cheap and colorful, and almost guaranteed to breed, if you have something that eats feeder fish that could be a benefit to them as well...I'd stay away from tetras, rams or any other cichlids for that matter without a filter, cichlids are messy, and tetras can sometimes be finicky about water conditions depending on the type, basically look for something that inhabits crappy water in the wild and you'll have success, I lived in Orlando for school and the drainage ditches besides having a lot of trash and turtles in them were full of small live bearers, almost looked like dull platys or mosquito fish...if they can live in that crap they can live almost anywhere
