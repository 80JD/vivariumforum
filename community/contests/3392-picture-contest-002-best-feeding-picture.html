Picture Contest 002 - Best Feeding Picture
We had a pretty decent turnout for picture contest 001, I hope to see an even better turn out for this one!

The subject is: Best Feeding Picture

Here are the rules:
1. Picture must be of a herp.
2. Today is Wednesday, April 1st. Entries will be accepted from today until Tuesday, April 7th at 11:59 PM.
3. Voting will begin at some point on Wednesday, April 8th and run until Tuesday, April 14th. The winner will be announced on April 15th and a new contest will begin.
4. One picture per member.
5. Picture must be uploaded to the gallery here, then posted to this thread using the "BB Code" link.
6. Picture must belong to you.
7. NO PHOTOSHOPPING!!! (cropping, fixing blur, contrast, brightness, etc are acceptable, but no abnormal enhancement of the photo like changing colors, adding anything to the photo, etc will be accepted).
8. The previous winner cannot enter.
9. Prize is 50 rep points and a custom User Title of your choice to last one month, your photo will also be stickied in the gallery.

Have FUN!!!

Herpetology (from Greek: ἑρπετόν, herpeton, "creeping animal" and λόγος, logos, "knowledge") is the branch of zoology concerned with the study of Amphibians_and_Reptiles.
Herpetology is concerned with poikilothermic, or ectothermic, tetrapods. "Herps" (or sometimes "herptiles" or "herpetofauna") include reptiles and amphibians, but exclude fish. However, it is not uncommon for herpetological and ichthyological scientific societies to "team up", publishing joint journals and holding conferences in order to foster the exchange of ideas between the fields. One of the most prestigious organizations, the American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists, is an example of this.
