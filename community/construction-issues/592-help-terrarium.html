click on forums and under Terrerium there is one called construction issues. the same area this thread is filed under. There are 46 threads in that area. I know it seems daunting but scan throught them, at the least you will get ideas on specificaly what to ask and on what direction you would like to go in.

Ill see if I can help by getting some more info.

1. will you buy a premade tank or can you/do you want to build your own?

2. what type of tree frogs? How many? how large do you want thier home to be?

keep in mind the region your frogs come from and thier specific needs. also be honest with yourself on money Vs Maintenance, will you be installing drainage systems, timers, misters and digital gages? or will you be sucking water with a turkey baister afew times a week, tuning the lights on and off, and misting by hand 2 or 3 times a day? Digital Gages often have a memory feature (if limited) and can help keep track of environmental conditions. I use cheap gages and keep records by hand. on the exspensive end you can have a mostly automated environment, even as was recently posted an uplink to the internet.

I might be able to give you ideas or direct you to the proper threads with this info.
