Great Stuff/Silicon/Dirt Question

You really have to press the coco fiber into the wet silicon. I would work in a very small section and then put a small pile of the coco fiber on the patch of wet silicon then press it in. if you are worried about getting it on you hands use something flat, place it over the patch and press the fiber in this should get it in there good.

I would also reccomend using WAY more coco fiber than you could ever get to stick. This way when you press it down you dont get the wet silicon on your hands (or gloves if your smart ) and you get better coverage. If its a small tank I do it all at once, but a square foot at a time is a good goal to shoot for.
