DAP Dynaflex 230 Mistake?
I read and read and read. I read I was supposed to use 100% Silicone in the Vivarium. I went to Menards and bought Dap Dynaflex 230. I don't know why the 100% Silicon requirement didn't jump into my head before I bought it. I took it home and used it in my 29 gallon aquarium to coat the glass then used it as adhesive for the coco fiber to the great stuff background. I waited about 20 hours and turned on the small water fall. The Dap has now turned a mushy gray and appears to be disolving in the water.
Am I completely screwed ( yes, I realize I'm probably screwed) or is there anyway, maybe more time to completely dry? I really don't want to start over. Of course, I haven't bought any frogs for the tank yet, but it will eventually house a couple frogs.

As long as you have the receipt and the tubes you should be able to return it.
