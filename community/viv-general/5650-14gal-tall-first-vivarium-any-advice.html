Definitely has potential... More water is definitely important. FBTs like to swim, and they will spend most of their time in the water if allowed to. They like broad leafed plants, like pothos (on land AND in the water), and they enjoy sitting on rocks that either JUST break the surface of the water, or are just BELOW the surface.

Definitely consider viva's ideas, they are good ones!

How big is this newt? Is it very slow on land like most newts? I think you should house it separately. FBTs are indiscriminate hunters, they will attack anything that moves, and they will try to eat it, even if it's too big, especially if they are going after the same food source. Now, I keep mixed tanks, and I've mixed with FBTs, but I don't think a slow, ground dwelling amphibian is the right mix there (regardless of how many people try it... A newt should live for around 6 years, you show me a person that has successfully housed the two species together for that long and I'll eat my hat...). Not trying to be critical or anything, but I must say I've never understood why people automatically think they can mix them, maybe because of the similar name? I'm not saying that's what you did or anything, I'm just rambling because I should have been in bed long ago, lol... Long story short, please evaluate whether you want to keep them housed together, I feel that it is risky.
