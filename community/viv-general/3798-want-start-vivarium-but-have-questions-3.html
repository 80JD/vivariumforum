Ahh . . . I understand. I'm afraid I pay for LOTS of books! Partly I must for my academic work, and partly . . . I'm a major bookaholic! My DH & I have cut down some, though, by going to the library more.

Does it have to be an e-book? Or would it be OK to get it through your local library? They might be able to ILL it, or if they can't get it as a loan, you could submit a request that they purchase it. The latter's pretty slow, but it may still be faster than waiting for the e-book!

I've seen de Vosjoli's snake book (since I purchased it) used; I've also seen several of his other books used. The Gecko book you mentioned earlier I'm getting used for $4.00 including S & H--the latter was $3.99 and the book was $0.01. Pretty silly, but we decided the info could be useful, and we haven't found too many credible online sources with a lot of detail. Anyway, it fills my need for more sources of info for more perspectives!

BTW, I printed out the info from that Phelsuma link you posted & read it more thoroughly--the guy's info about species' origins is extremely inaccurate in the sense that he's out of date & woefully understating how many species come from places like Africa. I have been astonished to learn how many desert species there are and the very wide variety of ecological niches these little guys occupy!
