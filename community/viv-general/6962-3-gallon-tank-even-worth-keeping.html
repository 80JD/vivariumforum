Is a 3 gallon tank even worth keeping?
To cut a long story short, it was my first fish tank. After a very, very short time on the internet I discovered it was unsuitable for any fish.

And so now I'm wondering - is there ANY animal that could live in it?

It is 12" long, 7" wide, and 9" deep. It's made of acrylic, not glass.

My only thoughts are:
Are there any insects or arthropods that would be comfortable?
and
Can I convert it upwards (change the 9" into 24" or more) to a suitable size and use it to house a very arboreal species? And if so which?

I'm asking sooner rather than later so that I can both start work on it (if possible) or throw it away (which seems more likely).

I'd keep it personally -- for all the reasons Beck mentioned.

Btw, there are terrestrial T's that would be fine in there -- the dimensions you quoted are just barely smaller than the KritterKeeper my Chilean Rosehair calls home. I've had her for about 15 or more years now.

Other arthropod options: Madagascar Hissing Cockroaches would be fine. Millipedes (including the giant), centipedes, scorpions, and many species of true spiders also come to mind as options.

As far as fish go, I have never done fish, but that is a far bigger tank than most folks keep betas in. Would it work for such? Or for a few guppies?

With that in mind, could it be used for the water section of a paludarium?

Additional uses:

an area to raise mosses or other moisture loving plants you wish to grow out a bit perhaps, before subjecting same to the wear and tear an animal might commit

a small terrarium for carnivorous plants

a soaking pool for a medium sized snake (useful around molting time).
