a piece of lexan (polycarbonate) could be used and should be easy enough to drill or cut around whatever's necessary. you could get everything where you want it and make a template out of paper and bring it to a glazier and have them make you a piece to fit your top with all your equipment. either way you should still be able to use a living hinge so the front can be opened without fiddling with your equipment in the back.

silicone, screen, etc. could be used to fill in the small gaps once it's how you need it.

If necessary to use plexi, you can reduce warping by making a frame for it and screwing it down to it.It doesn't have to be much, just enough wood to hold screws and not break when the plexi begins to warp. If you do the frame on top of the plexi, you can use it as a handle to open the lid.
