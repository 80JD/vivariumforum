Ferns are great for temperate vives, and there are MANY MANY mosses that require lower temps, so you'll be able to have lots of fun with those (moss makes a tank, imo). When researching plants, you just want to be sure that they don't have poisons that rub off of the leaves and that they don't have thorns, etc. You could try looking up plants native to the area that your frogs are from, then go with some of those. If these frogs are native to your area, I recommend taking a nice hike through the woods to collect some plants. If not, then you'll have to buy them, you'll find a bigger selection online, most likely, but you should find a good selection locally as well. Heck, if you live in most of the United States, you can use plants from your area with no problems. Even anything that will grow outside in your area should do well, even most house plants will do fine in 70ish degrees. It's actually far easier to stock a temperate vive than it is to stock a tropical one.
