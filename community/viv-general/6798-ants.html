Ants!!
...I has them! I was watering and doing some other maintenance I had been neglecting. I drenched the bromeliad, and noticed the little buildup of 'ant-hill' on the base. Then they went crazy. They're jet-black, and the smallest ants i've ever seen, think less than half the head of a pin. They seem to be completely arboreal (that bromeliad is over 2 ft above the 'ground'), and take no interest in my herps - in that picture there are probably 30 of em swarming around, and you can see a tree frog just chilling on the top leaves. As i watched em, they started taking tiny white pupa out of the plant, and stashing them between the bark on the log, so there's a full colony. They must have moved in recently, i've gotten shipments from both tropiflora.com and blackjungle.com in the last 6 months.

So right now, I'm taking this as a good thing. Even noticed a few appropriately tiny spiders too! I've been unable to find what they might be, does anybody have any info or idea what species they are?

Bit of an update -

I've let the ants alone, let 'nature' take it's course, and so far haven't seen any negative effects; they still leave my animals alone. I have seen them cleaning up dead insects and the like, and I believe they also feed on nectar from the various small flowers that bloom in there. I think they might help with the overall hygiene by removing dead or rotting stuff...

Also, figuring that a local species moving inside was more likely than getting an exotic type of ants via plant shipments, I started looking closely outside. Sure enough, I saw what looks like the same species crawling on a tree in my backyard (central Texas). I guess i'd never noticed them before because i just had never looked close enough.
