Quote:
Originally Posted by geckolord1
im thinking of adding the ficus i pruned a while back he fits he just needs his leaves back, and where do i put the ficus, when i put it in?
Is that why you did not add it before? It dropped its leaves? Just FYI, ficus are famous for dropping most/all of their leaves when you change their location/lighting etc. even if its for the better. But they do grow back.

The reason I mention this is, if you have it by a window or something for the leaves to grow back, when you put it in the viv they will likely drop off again. So you might just be better off adding it now and let the leaves fill in under the viv's light conditions.
