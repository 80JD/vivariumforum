Crested Gecko Exo Terra 24" x 18" x 24" vivarium build
Well, I picked up a used Exo Terra 24"l x 18"d x 24"h terrarium last week for a super price! Had some water spots on it as the previous owner kept a panther chameleon with a misting system. He only had the tank set up for 2 months and then switched to a screened unit so his loss was my gain!

With a good razor blade and water I was able to get all the water spots off the back, sides and doors. The hard water spots actually came off real easy!

So I'm in the planning, hunting, gathering stage of my viv build. At this moment in time, I simply have the terrarium. As the title states, I'm planning to keep a crested gecko, maybe a mated pair. My build thread will be modeled after grimm's vivs and the these initial pics which have a manzanita (ghostwood) root with the branches coming down into the water portion. I'm currently on the hunt for that special Manzi root piece. As soon as I find it, I'll post pics.

Current Plans:

Background constructed out of GS, titebond III wood glue with coco fiber and saragrassum moss mixed in for the top third and the titebond with a brown sand mixed into the bottom (submersed area).

I plan to drill the back with 2 x 1" holes for the incoming zoom med repti fogger line and the canister filter that I will use to run the waterfall and circulate the azuarium portion.

Lighting: Exo Terra 3 x 26watt hood with 3 x Walmart 26w T2s.

Meter: Exo Terra Combo Temp/Hyd meter

Right now my plans are to place the root in the middle and free fall the water and the fog down the root into the water. Will use red eco soil in the aquarium part and plan to plant mini anubias, with java fern. Fish: probably some small neons and a couple shrimps. The root branches will be facing and dangeling down into the water. I will of course use some gravel to provide a second area fro the gecko to get out the water if he falls in.

I think I'm convenced as to separate my land and water area using a false bottom for the planting in the land area with a buildup with egg crate similar to how Grimm did his.

Hope to have progress pics up soon, but not in a big hurry at this point.

All ideas, insight and info welcome!

i just wouldn't buy a temp/rh gauge made for pets at all. most likely a bad investment no matter which one you get ime.

a controller is up to you. if you like it and want it, why not?

fogger supply sounds like a plan.

lighting sounds good. might not be needed to that extent, but more is usually better for your plants.

spunjin is selling some manzanita on aquariacentral as we speak. might want to check out his packages. single branches too which is easier to put together "that specific look".

while you're at home depot might want to consider your gauges and a timer also. should be better quality and/or price than those sold for critters on both parts.

for above ground plants check out some of these places... good places to buy... please add to it!

for aquatic check out the plant finder at aquaticplantcentral, plantgeek.net and tropicas website. also... keep in mind many aquatic plants will do well anywhere it's moist and humid, so can be used out of water. riccia for instance makes for a great ground cover once it acclimates.
