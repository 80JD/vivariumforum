Build Log 55 Gal. Tall
Well, this has a been a project in the making for a while now. Decided to go with a pair of White Lined Geckos, and therefor I am going to convert my 55g. long into a 55g. tall.


Im using great stuff and quicrete for the background, and plan to incorporate a trickling water fall that will feed into a small pool at the bottom.

It will be planted with real and fake plants once I get to that step.

I still need to get some egg crate to build the false bottom underneath the planted area. The excess water from the planted area will feed into the pool, and waterfall reservoir.

I think i will use basic acrylic paint to paint the cement areas a more natural look.
