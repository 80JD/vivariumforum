Need help choosing inhabitants! Suggestions?
Hi folks! It's been a while since I've kept anything that would be at home in a viv (it's been mostly marine aquaria for the past several years) but I have a little 5.5 gallon going with just some plants that I've been thinking about stocking with some sort of vertebrate. The plants are pretty simple - bromeliads, jewel orchids, java moss - and it's been going for about a year. Here are a couple pics:





So, the big question: if I decide to add something, what should it be? I know it's a pretty small tank and there's probably not much that would fit, but do you guys have any suggestions? I love dart frogs, especially thumbnails, and I think this would be a great tank except for the size, but do you think they would be a possibility? Geckos also hold a special place in my heart (I bred Phelsuma laticauda for years before I got into aquaria), but I can't think of any small enough, except *maybe* a Lygodactylus sp., but I think even that would feel cramped. So yeah, I'm stuck, do you guys have any ideas? This seemed like a good place to ask.

Oh, I forgot to mention -- typically getting critters at herp shows is a LOT cheaper than what you will find them for in a store. (For example, when I got my pueblan milksnake at a show, she cost me $35. The similar size at Petco -- $70.) And if you ever get into snakes and need feeder mice ..... Even including gas and show admitance fee ($5 at the show I attend), it still wound up costing me only about 1/2 what the same purchase of f/t mice would have been at a pet store.
