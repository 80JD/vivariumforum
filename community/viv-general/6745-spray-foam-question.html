Spray foam question
I've never built a naturalistic viv, but I plan to build three very soon. I'm a contractor, and use spray foam to seal around doors, windows, and all kinds of other stuff too. I'm not a fan of Great Stuff for my day to day work, but will use it on a viv if it's the best product for the job. My question is, is there any reason why Great Stuff would be better than this- Todol Products

Here's the way they compare, IMO so far:

TODOL foam

Gun controlled, polyurethane closed cell foam (air and water proof), minimally expanding.

Great Stuff

plastic tube nozzled, don't know what it's made from, expands quite a bit.

Any wisdom?
