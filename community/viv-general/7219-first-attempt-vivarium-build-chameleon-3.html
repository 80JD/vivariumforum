The topper is simple egg crate painted black and it took over a month to build, mainly because of the various curing times/multiple applications of the various construction materials I used. It has been running for almost a year now with the cham doing great...she has plenty of upward space to move about, the plants are all thriving and the humidity levels remain high with the use of the lower glass aquarium. Our cham sheds completely, has a huge appetite and remains quite active. We recently purchased a baby male red bar amibole and will soon start construction on a larger enclosure for him, although keeping it a bity simpler in terms of construction. I expect he will grow considerably larger than our adult female veiled....his colors at 5 months are already incredible...he is going to be a real screamer when he fully matures!

I removed the turtle after a week....they are dirty, grow fast and it is probably not a good idea to mix the two. We have three baby turtles in a 20 long for now and just have a few small bottom feeders (dojo loach, red tail black shark, clown loach)

Foam is doing great after almost a year....I covered the surfaces in epoxy and then a layer of clear silicone with sand mixed in and let it all cure a full week before adding any water. Nothing is deteriorating to date and the evaporation is pretty quick which requires me to add fresh spring water every week. The fish are all healthy and I haven't lost any in the life of the tank. The water always remains clear.
