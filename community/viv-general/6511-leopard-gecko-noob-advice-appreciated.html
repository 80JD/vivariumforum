your set up sounds fine but there are something that need to be addressed, my answers are in red.

Quote:
Originally Posted by Shreddertheleo

Now for my questions.
1.) What kind of routine should I have for the lighting? Should I use the day time light at all since the room brightens up during the day? Can I leave the night/heat light on during the night or can I turn it off? It would still have the under tank heater on the warm side.

I wouldn't bother with the day time light to be honest as long as you say the room does get light. for the heat light you can turn it off or keep it on just personal preference, the geckos wont mind a cooler temp though for the night.

2.) Would peat moss be a better substrate in the moist, humid hidey?

what are you using right now? you can use peat moss, cocofiber, sphagnum moss, sheet moss or wet paper towels it really preference. i personally use cocofiber because i have it and it acts as a place for my leos to lay there eggs in.

3.) Is tap water fine or should I use the bottled spring water? I there anything better?

yes tap water is fine

4.)How much Calcium powder should I provide? It's Exo Terra brand with no D3.

ok your going to want to have a bowl or cap full of the Calcium available at all times in there cage you can use the the stuff you have right now without D3. but you are going to want to pick up calcium with D3 for dusting there food with, the D3 is important and vital for them.

5.) Where is the best place to place the probe? Currently I have it kind of tucked away.

i would keep it on the hot side to be honest so you know what the max temp is.

6.) I am using reptile carpet because I heard it is safer but I've also been told it's a pain in the butt. So down the road could I switch to sand or walnut shell?

there is also the fact that it will absorb the ura (part of the poop) and provide a breeding ground for bacteria. it also can catch on there nails so it really not good choice. i also wouldn't use wallnut shell of the sand thay use sell at set stores it's ether not that good for them of overly expensive. instead you can use slate or ceramic tiles like you get at home depo it conducts heat well looks nice and is easy to clean. you can also use plane play sand from home depo as long as you sieve it.

7.) Are these temperatures okay? Humidity too high or too low?

ya temps sound good, not much you can really do about the humidity so don't worry about it it's not that high.



I also have feeding questions.
1.) Crickets or mealworms? Or both? Could I alternate it weekly or bi-weekly or something like that?

crickets are the way to go meal worms have no nutritional value. this artical explains it "the pro's and con's of mealworms" when thay are full sized you can feed them supperworms which are better than meal worms still shouldn't be there main diet.

2.) How many crickets/mealworms at a time?

as many as thay will eat really every gecko is different.

3.) How many times a week?

when thay are still babies thay need to be feed every day but once thay are older every other day will be fine.

4.) I heard potatoes, dog food, carrots and cereal are okay for food for crickets and mealworms... is this okay?

for keeping them alive yes, the gecko no, you need to feed the crickets a good quality gutload for at least 30 minuets (ideally 24-12 hours before) you feed the crickets to the gecko.

5.) Will the exo terra calcium powder be okay for dusting crickets/mealworms?

i answered before this but you should use a calcium with D3 in it.

6.) How often should I dust them? Every second feeding?

i would do it every feeding.

one thing that wasnt adressed,.... he asked about misting the tank/geckos. DO NOT MIST REGULARLY. these leos are from an arid part of the world and as such should not have regular misting. The best thing to do is simply provide a moist hide. I take a ziploc food container (not the bag) and cut a hole about 1.5" in diameter. I place moistened (not soaked) paper towels in the container. they should be wet, but should not drip when you hold them up. Place the top back on and put the container where you want it. I place mine on the warm side of the tank. it turns into a little suana for them and they love it.

The result of regular misting for these geckos not can, but WILL result in respiratory infections and can lead to death of not addressed. Plus they just dont like it.
