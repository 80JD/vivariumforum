New 75 Gal Vive Build Log
tank5 017.jpgI am rebuilding my 75 gallon vive ..... housing tree frogs

egg crate false bottom with 4.5 inches of water.
2 water features: a stream and a waterfall

Plumbing: 1 medium fluval and 1 duetto 100 filter pump. poly filter lining and purigen powder under gravel substrate.

Step 1:

Black silicone on left side of tank to hide the egg crate.

Cork bark background siliconed to wall.

1.5 Inch PVC pipe to support the egg crate siliconed to bottom of tank.

All silicone= 100% silicone GE II

ultra thinset 2 is a thin set with a high proportion of polymer bonders within it.
Your choice was likely even better then mine, but I was being yelled at to get out of HD so only spent a little while and went with the tried and true.

A week is generally considered the minimum with soaking, some reef people soak for up to a month. I shoot for about a week and a half. My advice during the soak is have something moving the water around, i used a powerhead from an old reef tank.
