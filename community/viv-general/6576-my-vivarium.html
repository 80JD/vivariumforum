Very cool and looks like you're off to a good start. 62 gal is a nice size for a single gecko, you'll get to see their jumping behavior if you keep some clear areas between branches. Its always cool to see the jump across 1-2ft areas.

Sounds like you picked some hardy plants too, what kind of ficus were you thinking of? Ficus benjamina is a good tree that works well as a bonsai if you have a bit of vertical room. Ficus Pumila is great ground cover, similar to ivy, and also popular.
