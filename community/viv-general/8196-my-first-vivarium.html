My First Vivarium
So this is my first vivarium. In it I have an orchid, some ivy, a few different types of crotons, an AF, a bromeliad and some fern I found out in my back yard. I also have two FBT in the enclosure. This setup has actually been up and running for about a month and a half now. I'm already trying to think of ways to improve it. Basically just wanted to post this up see what everyone thinks. Again this is nothing fancy just something I kinda through together. I got the tank for free, I paid less that $5 for all the plants and $10 for the toads. Please let me know what I can do to better myself in this new hobbie of mine. Thanks!
