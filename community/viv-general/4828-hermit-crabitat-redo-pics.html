hermit crabitat redo... with pics
i found all my crabs above ground so i took the chance to fix their substrate issue.

first i put the cocofiber into a homer bucket with 5 pints of water (1 more than a gallon). i made me and the kids lunch... swelling takes a couple minutes.

i broke up the dry pieces and mixed them in a little better. then i started removing everything from the tank including the crabs...
100_2062.jpg

as you can see as i was pulling things out i was still discovering what messes they made...
100_2063.jpg

including ripping straight through the screen to turn over the soil (and leca) a little better lol...
100_2064.jpg

once i had everything out i started replacing the coco at what was to be the lower end under the water bowl. notice the uth pads and the foil tape...
100_2065.jpg

water bowl placed neatly...
100_2066.jpg

at that point everything is standard procedure so i just kept moving along with(out) pics...
