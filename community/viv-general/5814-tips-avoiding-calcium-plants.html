Do any of you have any tips? Other than to be more careful when dropping dusted crickets into the tank?

What do you shake the bugs in? a bag? a jar?

I have a system, lol (calcium will kill moss, and I have lots of it in my setups). I have a bowl, a jar (old el paso salsa jars work very well for this because of their shape), and a net (or other sifting apparatus). I add the calcium (and vitamins when needed) to the jar, then the bugs, then shake well. Next I put the net over the top of the jar and hold it tight (The tighter the better), then turn the jar upside down and shake the excess into the bowl. Then I tap the net to knock off the bugs, then shake them into the tank. Works every time.

Pretty much all of the loose stuff comes off as you shake them against the net. You can reuse the calcium for a while, but there comes a point when its not powdery enough to stick while shaking. If you find that you loose too much calcium powder to shaking, yours may not be ground finely enough (I've noticed this with some pet store calciums). A few pulses in a magic bullet or equivalent helps to smooth things out.

Check out the thread on moss propagation in the plants forum for more info on mosses. We never got to finish the discussion, but we'll pick it up again someday!
