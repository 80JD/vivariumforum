I saw this site weeks ago and use some of his techniques to build a waterfall. I use Quickcrete instead of grout. After applying 3 layers of Quickcrete I rinse the waterfall in water 4 times. Not to worried about the PH balance because I painted it with acylic (3 layers) paint then use Deco Art Seal All to seal the acylic paint- 2 layers ( it is non-toxic). Then I rinse the whole finsh waterfall again a couple of times. I'll install the waterfall and run water in the vivarium for a week before introducing the RETF to the vivarium. Tell you the truth...Im more worried about the GEII silicon than the acylic paint and sealant, causing health issues with my RETF's.
