T5's are more energy efficient than T8's or T10's.

T5 = 5/8" in diameter

T8 = 1" in diameter

T10 = 1 1/8" in diameter

It doesn't have anything to do with the kelvin rating at all. Just the size and efficiency is all.

Your plants will grow with one light buy may become leggy looking reaching for the light.

If you're on a budget and don't have any colourfull broms you could probably get away with another single light.

We have some nice broms and orchids in our tanks so we use 2 T5 HO and it does them well.

Nice tank btw.
