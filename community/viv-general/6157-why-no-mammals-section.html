Sort of, but you have to remember that the habitat in a wire cage is substantially different - it's not built to be a vivarium with living plants that need time to regrow after the animal tore and trampled it down, just by crawling across it, and don't forget that often such animals are taken out to be handled - in a way it increases it's immediate living area that the animal has access to.

You could put such an animal in a 50 gal tank as long as you didn't care what happened to the decor, and cleaned it out every day - it's not so much the size, but the impact the animal has on it's immediate surroundings, and such a tank would leave it's occupant with very little to do, and that is when they start getting destructive - they are just trying to keep occupied.

if we get enough interest and threads pertaining to mammals/rodents surely we'll put in a request for such a section. it's just not common and we're a somewhat small community as-is.

all constraints in consideration, a viv for a mammal is surely possible. if one can be done for a bp or a gtp then destruction obviously can be overcome. if one can be done for a full size cham then open air is not the end of the world. if people can convert closets to vivs and house 4'-5' tall trees (bushes imo) then space is not a limiting factor. all things considered, a viv for a mammal would be more like an aviary in many ways most likely considering you'd need deeply rooted tough plants and most likely plenty of space to accomplish such a task.
