I went SHOPPING, lol
Right after I dropped my husband off at work this morning, I ran over to Walmart to return the poor deceased froggie. I suddenly decided that I wanted to go to Petsmart. So, I hopped in my car and drove the 50+ miles it takes to get there.

I didn't get anything too special. I got six Rosy Reds for the Axie tank, last I looked there were two, lol. I also got three more ghost shrimp, all of which are prego females. I have one big female in there now that has been there for weeks... Maybe because of the lack of powerful filtration, some of the eggs might survive as larvae.

I bought two more mystery snails, one ivory and one blue. I already have a gold in my guppy tank, and a bunch of baby blues. I added the two new ones to the gup tank too.

I got the lady to pick a couple of trumpet snails for me too, and added those to my others in the guppy tank. Once I have enough of these snails I want to start offering them to you guys as barter for plant clippings, etc, lol. I think we could get a fun little trade system going. I have pond snails, mystery snails, ramshorns, and trumpets, and of course plenty of guppies.

I bought another nice big cabomba plant, for the Axie tank. I really like cabomba, I know a lot of people don't but I love it because it is so nice and lush.

I also found this little gem floating among the plants and had it thrown in for me... I have pondweed in my gup tank, but this is different from what I have or have seen. The leaves are almost fuzzy looking, and it is all tied together by it's roots. I thought it might be a nice addition for my palu eventually.



I also got another ADF, lol. I wasn't going to, but it turned out I could not resist. This one is much healthier looking than the last. I made her search through the tank until she found a nice fat one for me. Once this little guy is acclimated I will go back and get another. I also bought some frozen bloodworms.



The tank is kind of bare right now, I just took some clippings from my other tanks and planted them along with a little pond weed. I put 6 baby guppies and 2 baby blue mystery snails in it too.



My kids got lucky, after a lot of promises they got to have this tank in their room. They are thrilled. I might get them a couple of goldfish to go in it too, I really like black moores and pearlscales.

Free snails, a free plant... What more could I ask for?

AND I hit the store again today...

Mostly I am happy because I got the lady at Meijer to pick some more trumpets for me! They are butt-heads and usually tell me no there. I was able to add four to the Axie tank, four to the new frog/guppy tank, and dump about 15 more into my established tank.



My dang guppies are at it again... I netted about 20 fry earlier, and I looked in there awhile ago, and there are probably 20 more new fry. My older babies are getting BIG, lol. I am probably going to net some and put them in the Axie tank, and I already put some fry in the new tank.

I was watching the ghost shrimp in the Axie tank last night when I saw a tiny shrimp jet off one of the females! I took her out and put her in a seperate container, and this morning I had ten microscopic babies. I used a straw and removed them to a seperate container with a pinch of sea salt and an airstone. I put some flake food in the airline, hopefully it will break down enough for them to feed off it. One thing I found odd... They don't look "larval" to me. They look like perfectly formed miniatures of the adults. I thought ghost shrimp young-uns had a free swimming stage? Well, we will see what happens.

My daughter (the little one) decided she wanted to spend her Easter money on a fish for the tank that they now have in their room. She chose WELL.





Mommy approves We had to wait in line and we were standing next to the tank, another person came over with an employee and was looking at the guppies in that tank and she started to get upset. Not throwing herself, just, "My fishy...." The employee whispered, "Don't worry, I won't let her take your fish..." Kate was all smiles
