Are your measurements length (left to right) x width (front to back) x height? I only ask because s many people just toss the measurements up willy nilly and expect us to figure out which is which, lol...

Taller is better. They are tree dragons, after all. You want a BG that they can climb, and lots of branches up high, in the middle, and down low (dipping into the water if you put in a pond). I recommend at deep enough pond that you can have fish (guppies work well, they breed fast and are small enough to be eaten, except adult females), with a large enough surface area that he can swim if he wants, fish if he wants, and drop into the water if startled (if he wants!). My female always jumps into the water to get away from me when I try to catch her, lol...

If that's L x W x H, that sounds like a 90g tank. I'm so jealous! If it's one of the ends that's broken, that's perfect to turn it vertical. I would personally replace with eggcrate covered in screen (to maintain rigidity), and cover with plexi to retain humidity IF necessary. If you do the eggcrate covered in screen, the screen should be on the underside of the eggcrate to keep the lizard off the eggcrate (he'll hang from it, they like to, lol). You might see if you can get a light fixture to mount a UVB bulb inside the enclosure up at the top (probably have to be along the 24" side, I don't think they come in lengths less than 18") before you start constructing the BG. This will ensure that none of the UV is being blocked by the eggcrate (it's MEANT to block UV, and does a great job of it, if you put it outside, he won't get any at all, really), and give him a better area to choose how much UV exposure he wants.

For the front, you want a piece of glass or plexi, and depending on which you choose, silicone or that glue stuff that's for bonding acrylic. You want to put the piece of glass/plexi BEHIND the tank rim, so that the pressure of the substrate/water is pressing it into the seal, rather than away from the seal like it would if attached to the outside of the rim.

I've not mounted a door to a vertical conversion before, so I'm not really the one to give pointers on it. You should see how ghetto rigged my door to my big palu is, rofl...

Sweet!

I'd check HD and Lowe's (and other HW stores you might have) for pond/fountain pumps. They are MUCH less expensive than fish tank pumps, and alot of times will last longer. It will tell you right on the package how high the fountain pumps can pump to whatever height. The Beckett ones are really good, so I've heard, I know that HD carries that brand.

As for moss, if you'd like, you can look at my album in the gallery titled mosses and ferns and see if there's anything there you'd like. I have many more varieties available, I just haven't taken pics of all of them yet, lol... I sell by the sq". Of course, you might have some available locally as well, but I don't know how much moss grows in Texas. Also, if you like ferns, some Licorice fern would do WONDERFULLY in a tank that size, and it grows on tree trunks, so it does great mounted to the BG. I have a ton of it growing in my big palu with my Japaluras.
