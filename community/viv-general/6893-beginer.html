My first project is a 30 gallon long and I added 2 more feet of plexi to the top. I don't really think there is a specific size for beginners or professionals. I think it just matters on what you want to do with it or what you plan on putting into it. It also depends on how much time and money you'd like to put into your project. I'd definitely take advantage of the free tank though. Now its up to your imagination to come up with the right environment and right critters for it. Most of all, have fun with it.
