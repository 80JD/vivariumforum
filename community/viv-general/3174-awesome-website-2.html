The only sealent that I'm of that would work underwater is silicone, though the epoxy's might work as well. Also, I'm not sure if you even need to bother sealing it. Reef aquariums use cement for reef structures all the time without using sealent, they want all the little pores in the cement to be homes for bacteria. All you have to do is soak you structure in pure RO water for a couple weeks until the grout/cement stops releasing elements that would effect your water.
