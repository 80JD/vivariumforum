40 gallon viv with slate waterfall/pic
After several weeks of research and tons of great tips from this forum and the Orchid board, construction is under way!
Step # 1: 40 gallon tall tank :20"X20"X24" tall.Driftwood and GS foam backgound.Made outside of tank on fibre board.
Step # 2 :designed a false bottom with eggcrate and covered with mosquito netting.
Step # 3:making a free form waterfall out of natural slate,will power with a Hagen 302 powerhead under the false bottom.
Thats all I have done so far, but I just started today so I am pretty happy with the progress so far.Will be starting the coco husk application to the GS foam tomorrow. So glad I was paying attention reading these posts,and will make sure I sand the foam before putting the silicone on.Thanks in advance for all the expert advice so far.

update
So it's now day three and I see now why this hobby becomes somewhat obsessive!.I was up til after midnight trying to see how I was going to place the slate for the waterfall. Since the entire structure weighs about 32 lbs, I just siliconed together the bottom 1/3 of the whole thing. That way I have some flexibility if I decide to move it to another location.I really lucked out today when I went shopping for some coconut coir. I found a hydroponic retailer about 2 blocks from where I live. I got a block or 11 litres for just $10.00! It is really good quality with lots of bits and pieces in it.I had no idea how much it expands,one tiny corner of the block,soaked in a bucket is enough to do about half of the background.Sadly I ran out of black silicone. I estimate the way I am slapping it on, I will need 3 of the large GE tubes.Off to Home Depot for more.............

If you have a shop vac, I recommend that you gently vacuum the BG once it's dry and before you stand it up. If you do it with a clean shop vac, you can even reuse the coco fiber you suck off. I do this when I do a BG, except I don't wait for it to dry, lol... I do it as I go so that I can remove excess without dislodging what's stuck to the silicone so that I don't have to use as much to begin with... I vacuum it off and then take it right back out of the vacuum and start the next section. I can't wait to see more pics. It's looking good so far.
