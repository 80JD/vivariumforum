There are TONS of ways, I think you are confused because they are getting mixed up:

#1: GS Foam -> Mixture of silicone/coco dirt (make sure the dirt is DRY, i bake mine just incase)

#2: Foam -> Grout -> (optional) Non toxic Acrylic paints ->Wait 5-6 weeks until its FULLY cured (otherwise it will affect your water pH)

#3: Foam -> Grout -> (optional) non toxic acrylic paints -> Epoxy/Sealant

#4: Clay -> done. (Some ppl love clay, I HATE it...if you do not keep it just the right moisture level it will either dry up and crack or get too wet and crumble....fastest to set up, hardest to maintain and lowest life expectancy imo)
