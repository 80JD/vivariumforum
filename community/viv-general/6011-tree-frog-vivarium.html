Personally I would steer clear of soil, especially if your concerned about drainage. Using soil as a substrate can cause humidity issues and bad bacteria to grow. I would be careful with your design if you planning on using soil. Lots of people seem to use false bottoms to allow for better drainage design. I guess it depends on what types of frogs your planning on having.

I wouldnt mix species. If there was a big size difference, they may eat each other. However, you might be able to add some type of lizard. Best to decide the frog you want first, then figure out what animals can be housed with them.

soil is fine... it's the kind you use you have to be aware of.

you're looking for natural items and those made for such environments mostly. for example:

pond soil
laterite
sphag moss
peat
organic potting soil
sand
riverbed clay
etc., etc.
abg mix is popular and simple. as well plenty of sources carry it.

big willie... your "viv" is more of a palu... also, the stone substrate is good for anchoring some aquatic plants but not great for the roots/rooting of others and would likely be horrible for most terrestrial plants in most vivs. to make it work above water you'd need to follow methods more like hydro/hygro/aqua farming; ebb and flow, soil-less growing, etc. ...

now for the original Q's:
1. somewhat covered above. there are others also and most folks have their way/favorite way of doing things.
2. pea gravel is fine but leca is better for several reasons. it's not overly expensive either like one would think...
3. you could make a topper, canopy, cover it with glass, polycarbonate, etc. or just purchase the versa-top made for that particular tank. surely the versa-top would be much easier and more economical than other methods.
4. how many would depend on species. some can mix but as mentioned, without proper experience, qt, precautions, an extra tank for emergencies... i wouldn't suggest it. also in a normal size tank it's probably best not to mix similar species at all.
5. backgrounds are unique in construction depending on the method and final outcome. you'd have to choose a type and ask specifically about the methods for that type to keep on track. if you see one you like don't be afraid to ask about it specifically.
6. water feature... pretty much the same as backgrounds

check out the builds on black jungle... surely they'll help with some water feature and/or b/g questions.
