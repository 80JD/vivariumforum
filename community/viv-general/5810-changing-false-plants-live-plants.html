1. would be a good idea. the 5 gallon should be fine while you transplant.
2. substrate, yes... replace it, no
3. you can add the soil on the bottom without drainage so long as you don't over water. if you might over water it's best to have a drainage layer and something to siphon out the extra water.
4. looks like you've got some regular house plants there. some organic potting soil mixed with some peat moss and or another additive might be best for them without adding too much fertilizer for the frogs. i don't know what's best for the brom personally. maybe one of our plant gurus will chime in on soil.

If you do soil straight into the tank, I recommend adding something to keep it from compacting, like a little gravel and a bit of sand. That worked really well for me when I had my tanks without FBs, and I never had to change out the substrate.

You shouldn't have to clean too often, just make sure you get some critters for your cleanup crew (springtails, isopods, red wigglers), they'll help turn that nasty poo into plant food, lol...

How big is that tank? I'm just curious how you'll fit all that in there! Great leaf sizes for the frogs though, you're on the right track for sure.

I did not, no. I would imagine there would have to be some sort of shaking or sifting going on to have that happen. That might happen under water, or with completely dry substrates (such as trying to layer sand and gravel without either being wet), but I really don't see that happening with soil in a vive.
