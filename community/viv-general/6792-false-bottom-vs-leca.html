Ensure that your water feature is not very big. The tank you are using is fairly small for 3 darts (larger species), so the more land area, the better.

On to the questions....

1. Leca will provide more surface area for microfauna to flourish, whereas a false bottom will not. Its personal preference. I personally hate the look of Leca, so I would try and hide it somehow.

2-3. It depends how deep you want the water feature. I would leave at least 1 inch from the substrate to the water. If you are using leca, you may want an even larger gap to accomodate for the water's capilary action up the leca.

4. Tannins are completely fine. They are even recommended for tadpole deposite sites. You are looking for a clear brown water, not cloudy! If it is cloudy its time to do some water changes.
