You guys have more composure than me, lol... I read that and almost started rolling.

Rodents even like to chew through those brightly colored cages you don't like, rudukai. They'll even chew holes big enough to escape through.

I'm sure it can be done, but not with anything that you expect to stay nice. Rodents produce ALOT of urine though, and at high enough concentrations to burn plants. I know that you said that plants weren't the focus of the question, but I just wanted to put that out there.

Even with a more natural substrate, it would have to be changed almost constantly. The urine would build up very quickly, which is the reason that people use stuff like aspen. I can definitely see something like coco husk working pretty well though. It's more absorbent, and so wouldn't have to be replaced QUITE as frequently, and they could chew on the chunks, which might encourage them to chew on wooden cage decor a little less.
