Foam background questions
Hi, I'm new to this site and to creating a natural background for my vivarium. I'm doing some research on the different options available and I was wondering if anyone could shed some light on my questions to make sure I'm off to a good start.

I've browsed many sites to see examples and I think I like the look of the expanding foam backgrounds covered in coco fiber/moss the best. I am leaning towards using the Great Stuff pond foam since it is black and waterproof even though it's a bit more expensive. I notice that most people are using black silicone to cover the foam for waterproofing and to stick the moss to.. is there a reason you can't bypass using the silicone and just stick the moss and coco fiber directly to the foam before it dries (especially since the pond foam is waterproof as it is)? Has anyone tried this method? It seems like it would be a lot cleaner/easier/cheaper this way but I'm not sure if there are any drawbacks or risks associated with this.

Also, it seems like people are split between putting the foam directly on the glass and putting a layer of silicone on the glass prior to the foam. Has anyone had any bad experiences putting it directly on the glass? I've heard the concern are with the glass breaking but that seems pretty unlikely to me. Just curious what you all thought about that.

Thanks for any help and I'll probably be posting some more questions as they come to me so I get this right the first time!

Yeah the coco will not stick evenly to the expanding foam. It ends up looking terrible.

Wait until there are no more fumes and everything is dry before you add plants and animals. As far as lighting is concerned, a bulb with a Kelvin rating between 6500 and 6700 should work marvelously. But really, it depends on what plants you'll be growing. Make sure that your light fixture/ fixtures are large enough to shine light all over every plant in the tank, if they aren't you will get horrible growth. On the bulb's package it will say 6500k or 6700k, and T-8, T-10, T-12, etc. along with it's length. Choose whichever suits your needs. Have fun!
