Quote:
Originally Posted by FroggieLove
is there a brand or certain type of sealant that would make this project safe for my tree frogs?
Any product that is an waterproof sealant without Mold Inhibitor will be fine..


Now, when saying that it is "safe", is not neccessarily fullproof. No one on this site has completely proved that any sealant is safe around herps, but there have been no cases of anything bad happening (As far as I know).


Also, if you've never done anything like what the website describes, I would not try it with methods and herps not listed on the site. Practice makes perfect, but always think of your herp's health before making a "pretty" terrarium.
