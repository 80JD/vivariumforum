Very true! While it is still wet, you can sprinkle some sand (sanitize first) into it. This cuts down on the shine/plastic look it will have. Cover it with some darker neutral toned acrylic paint (I suggest mixing your own from the primary colors to match and blend it in) and you will have a great looking piece.
