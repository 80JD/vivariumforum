Plans
I work at a gas station as a fuel attendant because that is the only job I have due to losing my other one. Well today we received our new zippo lighter display and I immediatly whote a note to the manager stating that if zippo don't want it that I do. It does have a light (the circular florecent ones) and can easily be modded to make a vivarium. I figured the plastic bottom and top sections that have zippo all over them can be removed or at least covered with wood and be made to look very nice. the center part where the lighters set is powered by a motor which will be removed along with the center part and I was thinking in its place I will use those holes to screw in a tree branch 3" -4" in diameter with some smaller branches coming off of it here is a list of things I have already to make this possible


wood- oak boards from old pallets, the GOOD pallets

branches- various types of trees in the area broken by storms from the hard winter and spring we had, mainly Oak and Maple

foam Insulation sheet scraps

tubing from the aquarium

water filter/pump from the aquarium

live moss growing in the woods. There is Ivy,ferns and other plants growing in the woods as well that I can use.

clay pellets for water absorbtion/substrate

compost without kitchen scraps, fertilizers ect that would be harfull that will be used on top of the landscaping cloth that the plants will be planted in

landscaping cloth to seperate compost and clay pellets

Horticulture, Woodworking, fabrication and many other types of experiance

tools, and lots of them. I was an auto tech which means I have alot of usable tools from that, I also have a miter saw, skil saw, belt sander, finishing sander, dremel, drill and drill bits ect.

screws and finishing nails.

felt tape - the kind used for draft protection on home doors .the door needs to be modded because it has spaces and loose fitting I will probably have to make something for the door to set against to keep the animals and live food in as well as the humidity.

most likely I have or can obtain other needed items as well as the project goes on.


There are some questions that I have

does anyone make a circular florecent light that has the needed requirements for the amphibian/reptile health

Due to the moisture that will be in the Vivarium what can I use to seal the branch to keep it from cracking ,getting mildew/molded ect that will not harm the animals

what kind of paint can be used on the foam insulation that is not harmful and won't melt it.

what can be don to keep algae, mold and mildew from growing that isn't harmful.

any other ideas or advice will be greatly appreciated
