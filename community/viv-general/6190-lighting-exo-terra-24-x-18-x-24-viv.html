I plan to keep a mated pair of crested geckos. Lighting is a concern, especially for the front water portion of the viv to grow submersed plants (anubias, java ferns, mosses. Am thinking about buying the exo terro 24" long hood with 3 x 26 watt cfls for the back and a 4 x 24w T5 fixture at the front. Thoughts?

For a simple, and cost effective solution, I would go with two 24" T8 or T5 Fluorescent fixtures. They typically have 2 bulbs per fixture so it should be adequate lighting considering this will only be roughly 40-45 galons. I bought both of my 48" T8 fixtures for 100$, so yours should be much less. Plus they are extremely easy to find at any large home/building store, or even Walmart.

And you have the right idea with the types of plants to grow. Stick with anubias, and anything java. They will grow in low light, but will thrive in anything brighter. I was dumb and bought some baby leaf and fine leafed plants, not knowing they require insanely bright light and perfect water quality.

i'd place whatever fixture you use in the front for best penetration.
both fixtures would be overkill, imho.
10,000k isn't necessary but it is a very clean (looking), bright (looking), white (looking) light.
for cfl's check out walmart. the ge daylight 6500k cfl's are great. they'll probably carry 2 versions... t2 and t3. there's a couple ways to tell them apart. the t3's should be ~$5 for a 2 pack. the t2's should be ~$10 for a 2 pack. if you put them side by side you should notice one bulb (the more expensive ones [t2]) is thinner than the other. although they'll have similar stats and boast nearly identical lumens the t2 should be a far superior bulb. it will consume a little less energy and the tubes are thinner and shorter. this means more room for them as well as less re-strike (more of the light should get where you aim it).
