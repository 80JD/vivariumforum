Unless there is something very unusual about your set-up, you will be using may devices with low current-draw, so the issue is not so likely to be overloading the circuit (thus blowing a fuse or tripping a breaker) but just having enough receptacles to plug into. Even so, a couple of six-outlet power-strips will probably cover you needs.

Most devices will somewhere list their wattage and or amperage, and you can add up the total load to know if you're within a safe range (most household lighting circuits are rated at 15 amps). Heaters are likely to be the heaviest load, followed by lighting, but if you use modern fixtures, even lighting doesn't add up to much.

Submersible pumps, which move all that heavy water around to run a filter or waterfall, are surprisingly efficient. A pump that can move 380 gallons per hour (depending on height of lift, but plenty for a decent-sized paludarium) draws only about 19 watts according to the manufacturer.

Hope this helps.

Martin
