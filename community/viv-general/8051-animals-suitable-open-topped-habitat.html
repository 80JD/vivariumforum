Animals suitable to an open topped habitat
I am currently builing a rather large indoor pond that has a lot of land area to it as well. I am wondering what kinds of creatures may be able to live in it. I expect to have 12 inch plexiglass sides with no lid. The water area will have a couple hundred gallons and maybe 8 square feet of land area.

Right now I am thinking zebra danios and cherry shrimp in the water, freshwater terrestrial crabs on the land, and maybe just to complicate things pill bugs on one floating island and sugar ants on another. But I am not firmly decided on anything yet. What other intersting creatures could be left without a lid over their heads to stop escapes?

I am still building now so this is a good time to start changing my mind.

At the bottom of the pond I will have a few inches of lava rock. In the middle of that will be a tube running to a stainless steel tank hidden under the aquarium. The stainless tank will hold the pump and heaters. In a terrarium I had running until recently I had good success with the lava rock. It seems to make a good home for the nitrifying bacteria, it is very porous.

I think Geosesarma sp (vampire crab) would make a great pet in here. The problem with newt and salamanders (unless I am mistaken here) is that they like it to remain cool. Cool is easy in the winter but cooling it in the summer will be too much. I think it will be easier for me to heat it in the winter and just maintain a year round 28C. Actually, I am thinking that If I run enough wattage I will be able to heat half of my house from the heat leaking out of the pond but that is another hair-brained idea for later.

I strongly suspect that the soil will remain too wet for ants. An very likely issue you will have with the crabs is their devouring the other inhabitants. Same problem if you go with turtles.

Uropygids (aka vineragoons) could be very cool on the land portion. There are also some types of roaches that do not fly nor climb slick surfaces well.

Part of the question, too, is just how moist or dry your land portion will be.
