The plant on the left is from the Aeschynanthus family (common name: "Tai Pink Lipstick Plant/Vine"). Aeschynanthus radicans has fuzzy leaves and red flowers. Mine are smooth leaves pink flowers. Both are equally attractive. I bough it from Home Depo. I couldn't find the actual species for mine. Sorry. I did find a site with all the species though: Aeschynanthus. Hope that helps.

Center top is the a Cryptanthus sp. ("Earth Star") and center bottom is Microsorum diversifolium ("Kangaroo Paw Fern").

Right side is the Callisia Repens ("Bolivian Wandering Jew") and I just added another Cryptanthus sp. but biggerto the right side wall.
