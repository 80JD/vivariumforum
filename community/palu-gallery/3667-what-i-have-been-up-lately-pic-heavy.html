What I have been up to lately... Pic heavy
I know that I have been kind of MIA, I have had a lot going on, my daughter was in a production of Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, and we sold our house, and bought the one next door, lol.

I have also been getting back into aquatic tanks.

I know this isn't exactly a palu, but I wanted to share my new invert only tank.

It still has a lot of growing in to do, but considering it has been only 9 days, I think it is coming along great.





















Here is another full tank shot. I can't wait for the moss wall to grow in!



Moss wall after 6 days

The sand had more spine than I do
