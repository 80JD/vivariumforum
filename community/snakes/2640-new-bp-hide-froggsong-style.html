The feasibility points (to me) would be [1] if you could construct it such that it could be adjusted easily without being too intrusive to the reptile. I don't think so, cause they'll know the material/scent/etc). And, [2], if it wasn't crazy expensive/complicated to build... though you sound like you're always up for a challenge : )
