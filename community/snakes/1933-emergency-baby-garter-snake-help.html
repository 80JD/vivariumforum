Give it a large water dish it can get out of and feed the fish in the water bowl feed dead prey/tuna or trout on a plate or lid..

Whatever you do DO NOT use feeder Goldfish, they contain high levels of an enzyme called thiaminase which destroys vitamin B (thiamine). Feeding a diet high in feeder Goldfish will cause neurological disorders and death.

Because of the nature of worms and fish you'll need to feed it at least every other day until it gets large enough to start eating day old pinks.
