selective breeding by colour/markings is a great way to start out and can be extremely rewarding for any hobbiest,
but as i make a living from breeding large amonuts of both normal specimens and also rarer genetic strains then i have the need to 'buy in' fresh bloodlines and certain genetic charecteristics, if i want to breed albinos then i will obviously need at least one specimen that is at least het for albinism but even then (if bred with a normal snake)only a small percentage of the offspring will actually be albino (around 25%) while 50% will be Heterozygous and carry the gene for albinism and the other 25% will be normal snakes both in looks and genetics,
that is the reason i occasional spend huge sums of money on the 'perfect' snake, the 'bumblebee' royal python i paid £7000 for would have taken years to produce due the the extremely complicated genetics they posses, it was simply alot easier to buy one in and use it to develope my own stock,
