Look What the cat dragged in!
Got home from work tonight, and left my front door open so I could carry groceries in. On my second trip, spotted the neighbor's cat coming out of my bedroom! I chased it out the door, then went to check on my bedroom. Look what the cat left on my bed!







It's a twelve inch-long Ground Snake (Sonora semiannulata). It has a few tooth punctures from the cat, but seems to be ok. Who knows, though. We'll see if it's still alive tomorrow.
