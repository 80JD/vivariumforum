From someone who has kept birds for a LOT of years, don't do it. Birds are the dirtiest pets on the planet, bar none. They are basically feathered poop machines, it hardens like concrete and the poop can end up anywhere because they can be airborn. Even the "walls" or sides of the enclosure will eventually be quite difficult to clean. That being said, they are quite engaging creatures. I have kept budgies, several species of finch and a white-capped Pionus (smallish parrot). I even had a whole room of my house converted into an aviary at one point, with built-in baths, fountains, hanging feeders and nest boxes. So, if you're determined to have birds and go with finches, orange-cheeked waxbills are both beautiful and active and I would recommend them, but you can't beat your basic Society Finch (also called Bengalese) for friendliness and liveliness. They should be available at any pet store.

You are right on about the parasite issue. Birds can be carriers of giardia and other bugs. Even with something as big as the Majestic, cleanliness would be a 24/7 deal. It seems like if there is a clear wall (like glass or acrylic) it's a magnet for bird waste, LOL!
