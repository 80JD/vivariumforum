Sorry to get off topic, I just think it would help people be more aware of contests and such if it were more "visible".

I don't have much use for contests as they generally end up being contests between the most popular people or who can take the best pics, but if you really want to increase participation in this site it might be a good idea to start with the "Introductions forum". Look at how many people have introduced themselves here and had no response or those who I, as a new person, have been the first to respond to.
When a person's intro goes un-answered and/or the more knowledgeable people ignore a new comers intro it presents a less that welcoming image. Most people scope out a site for a while before joining and if they are ignored or treated as something less they move on.
Sorry if this ruffles any feathers but that is this old man’s observations base on personal experience as a site admin and a member of quite a number of sites.
