I do not house anoles on purpose but they are native here and I have them by the 100's outside in the greenhouses and even in the house. In the summer I never see many brown males unless they are fighting and then it's normally the loser. The females are brown sometimes but most of the time they are green with a lighter stripe down the center.

Winter is a totally different thing. They are all brown almost all the time unless they find a very warm spot and it stays that way. One other thing I have noticed. They detest being sprayed with water and this always makes them run from the water and turn brown.

So, if your anole is brown I would say it is either cool or stressed for whatever reason. Extending the dewlap is staking territory and displaying for females. I love the tank but I would move the anoles to another enclosure.

Actually no reasoning. After looking at the setup again I think you're correct. I would setup something else for the RBF's.

It doesn't seem to matter whether the water is warm or cold they rum. The water I use in the greenhouse is heated to about 90Fin the winter but they still take off.

Anoles LOVE to sun themselves. They like to sun on dark colors for obvious reasons. They come out if it's sunny in the winter at anything above freezing. I have seen them out when the water is frozen. They like anything they can catch. They also like to feed from the hummingbird feeders so they would most likely like a treat of something like you would feed a day gecko like some kind of fruit baby food.
