Hi Janine (Welcome to the forum, btw ), that isn't a silly question at all. FBTs ARE toxic. WC toads are much more toxic that CB toads, and even WC lose some of their toxicity after being in captivity for a long time. The toxins they emit shouldn't be harmful to fish as long as there is a large enough volume of water and plenty of filtration that is changed often (frequent partial water changes should also be made, especially if there is any evidence of the toxins building up in the water, like an oily sheen floating around). I have kept fish with my FBTs, and I also keep my FBTs with other frogs. My barking tree frogs used to sit on my Marvin's head, and would choose to sleep in the same hide with him over other hides that should have appealed more to a tree frog. I no longer have the barking tree frogs, one died (of old age), and the other two were lost during tank rebuilds (probably kitty snacks, poor guys I miss them so), but I never saw any ill effects from them being housed with the FBTs. I still have the golden tree frog in with them and she is healthy and happy. I don't currently have any fish in with them, because I don't have a large volume of water in their tank, and what I do have in there is kept shallow because they seem to prefer shallow water. When I did have fish with them, I never had any deaths (other than ones that got eaten, lol), and I didn't even feed those fish (they were placed in the tank for a cleaning crew and an occasional snack).
