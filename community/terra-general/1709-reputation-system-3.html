So I have written up a new post about the rep system. I'm going to post it here, and then post it in a new thread in Feedback, until we can get a new section set up for things like this.

Here it is:

What is the reputation system?

The reputation system is to be used to recognize the helpful input and contributions of your fellow bretheren. It involves leaving positive or negative feedback in the form of points for specific posts. By using the rep system regularly, you help other people know if they can trust what someone has posted.


How do I use the reputation system?

When you come across a post you find especially helpful/harmful you can leave reputation feedback for the poster by clicking on the small set of scales in the upper right hand corner of their post. This brings up a dialogue box which allows you to select "I approve" or "I disapprove" and a small text field to enter a comment. You MUST enter a comment in the field provided.


The reputation system is not to be used for baseless or personal reasons. It should not be used "just because" or "tit-for-tat". This includes but is not limited to "fluffing" or "twinking" someone by awarding them points because they are your online buddy, because you both disagree with the same poster or anything equally shallow. On the flip side negative points should not be left merely because you disagree with someone, have any sort of personal rivalries or for any other vindictive reasons. Misuse of this system may result in being "neutered" either temporarily or permanently depending on the severity of the situation. If this occcurs you may be placed in a different usergroup that lacks the ability to use the reputation system at all regardless of your current status.



What happens when I get positive or negative rep points?

As certain levels of points are reached, more little green boxes are earned. (Or red if you're going the wrong way). Also, the text that appears when you mouse-over someone's reputation boxes will change.

351 to 450 User is a splendid one to behold
451 to 650 User is a glorious beacon of light
651 to 750 User has a reputation beyond repute



Where do I find out what my reputation level is?

Everyone starts with 10 reputation points. You can view your reputation points and comments in the UserCP (look in the menu bar near the top of the site for this).

On your UserCP, you will have the same little boxes you have in a thread. There may be more than one box, which indicates the level of reputation you have recieved. If the box/es is red, you are in the negative, if the box'es is green, you are in the positive. If you have a grey box, your reputation is disabled for some reason.



How do I find out why someone left me rep?

Next to the reputation comments in your UserCP, you'll notice that there is a colored box:

Red square - negative reputation
Green square - positive reputation
Grey square - no points +/- (user was unable to use the rep system - because they are still a new member, have a negative amount of rep, or have reputation disabled)

All reputation "hits" should have a thread title next to them. Clicking this will take you to the specific post the reputation comment is referring to.



Can people see who left the reputation points?

No. In order to avoid unnecessary "paybacks" this information is not available.



Most people have one green box, but some have two or three. Why?

Everyone starts out with one green box. Every 100 points of rep earns another green box. And if the box is red instead of green, then the person has gone into negative reps.
