As long as they are worms and not some sort of larvae, they are fine. I haven't had any adverse effects with them in my vive. They tend to eat more rotting stuff than regular earthworms do, so they are good to have as part of the cleanup crew. If you have fish, they love to eat them. Just check one to be sure there aren't any tiny legs up near the front. I came across some when I was moss hunting that had legs but looked exactly like the little white worms otherwise.

I have used the worms sold as "red wigglers" for years in my vives. I'm not sure of the actual soil temp in my CWD tank, but I have dug these worms up in the yard back in SC in the middle of summer, so they should do just fine. Tropical springtails are a good addition, they'll keep the top inch or so of substrate clean. These little white worms do a good job too.
