As for the schefflera, thank you very much. It was suffering outside in the dry weather we've been having and has perked up quite nicely in the tank. It gets plenty of water and lots of light. Just a quick question, is it going to hurt it to be sitting in the water? I couldn't find anything when I put it in there to keep the pot out of the water (it is sitting on top of the filter box in about an inch of water). I do like plants that can go right in the water, but I'm not sure about this one. To be honest I haven't done the research, but I just don't have a brain for plant care and will likely forget anyway. I'm trying to do better, lol... I'm so tired of killing my plants accidentally right when I've become so attached to them.

As for the eggcrate topper, there is a plexiglass door that hangs from a piece of eggcrate that comes partway down from the top (to keep it all square). The screen lid that I was using on the tank sits on top of the eggcrate with the lights on top and the UV light hangs inside (the ballast on my hood burned out so I have to use a separate one... It stinks. But she gets better exposure that way anyway). If you try a topper this way, make sure you remember that the UV must be on the inside of the eggcrate because it is designed to block UV. It is remarkably strong and actually doesn't look that bad. I am quite amazed at how well it turned out. The only problem I am having is feeding. She hasn't been eating well for a few weeks, and now it is hard to just toss food inside and I can't get her to hand feed. I have resorted to force feeding... I don't like it but she is getting thin. Because there is no screen around the eggcrate, I can't just throw bugs in (they will quickly escape), and there is also no real land (just the "tree" and that coke crate for her to climb on). I'm out of funds right now to get anything to do it differently or "finish" it. We are trying to get a new house to move into and we'll have to borrow some money for the deposit already... (I hope we get it though, it's $475 cheaper per month, 500 sq ft bigger, and on around 5 acres of land [1 of which is the yard and for our use]. I could build her a mansion the first month, lol. This could totally be our big break.)

I don't recall the source, but they are designed to block the minimal amount of UV produced by regular fluorescent bulbs. It isn't so much the size of the holes but the depth of the squares (I can't think of a better way to say that...). The squares are sized so that the light reflects around the inside of the squares and the UV is blocked. I'll have to see if I can come across the source again. It was when I was trying to find out more about it so that I could locate some. So it either came from a manufacturer, seller, or perhaps even from Phillips or GE...
