Help planning a rainforest vivarium
Hi all, firstly I hope this is in the right section.
I am posting this thread in hope that someone with experience might be able to correct me in any areas of my build that will need altering, so I'll start with a bit of a description of what I have in mind.
Basically I will be building a rainforest vivarium over the next couple months (hope to be starting in a few weeks) to house a pair of angle headed dragons. I have a 900x600x600 reptile one glass enclosure to work with.
At this stage, I am looking to have a planted background (with small pots incorporated into it for the plants to grow in), a waterfall into a 'pond' area around 1/2 to 3/4 of the bottom. I am also wanting to incorporate a rain system aswell for extra humidity and to help water the plants. I have seen expanda foam backgrounds covered with fern root mulch that is stuck there with silicone, is this going to get wrecked with the dragons climbing on it? I would like to try and avoid repair jobs in the future lol.

As far as plants go at this stage I am thinking of using some sort of fern, bromelaids, umbrella plant and a ficus (pumila?) Also in terms of 'feeding' the plants, are you able to use some sort of fertilizer or something? Is this needed for them to flourish?

anyway I am trying to keep this short, so I think I have got everything else minus the plants situation worked out. If anyone has any helpful comments on what I could change or things to keep in mind when building, it would be very much appreciated because I want to get it right the first time .

cheers
