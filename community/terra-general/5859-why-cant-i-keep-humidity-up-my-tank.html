Um...Are you using one of those useless puck style guages? Those things are terrible. Mine was stuck at 150% humidity when it was in my tank.

If you are using a reliable meter, then it could be one of the following problems.

-Too much ventillation from the mesh top. Try getting glass cut to size so the mesh can be covered.
-Not enough REAL plants could be a cause. They definately help in maintaining high himidity.

I think if you posted a picture of your tank it would help a lot. Personally I think if you are using a humidifier you should not have a humidity problem. It is probably the measuring tool that is at fault.
