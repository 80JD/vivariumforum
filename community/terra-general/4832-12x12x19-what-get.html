1) yes and no... if i remember correctly crested need decent humidity but not astronomical. that being said imo a waterfall can be added to an awful lot of setups if proper care is taken to prepare for it.

2) substrate can be a very opinionated question. there are a lot of options and many will do. layers are only typically necessary to obtain an end result. for instance to make a rolling hill a drainage layer can be sloped or for better drainage in a wet environment.

3) if i remember correctly cresteds don't need an abundance of heat sooo your cfl may do the trick but the more light the better in many cases.

4) cresteds are common pets nowadays. you should have no problems finding one. i will say it's best to get any animals you can from a breeder for health and overall specimen quality purposes. for that reason online may be your best bet.

5) you can put silicone and coco on just about anything except acrylic, lexan and plexiglass. anything natural will break down over time and decompose. that being said it should hold up some time under a drip wall. i don't think i'd give it much more current to contend with myself.

you can find pretty good answers to some, if not all of these questions in the links i gave you and/or the stickies on this site. we will be glad to help you but you need to understand the fundamentals before you go forward with anything. lighting and substrate alone will be more of a debate (without material such as pics and vids to back up different points of view) than it's worth prior to having a good understanding. without having pics of a build going on you'll get a lot of circumstantial advice that may not apply to your overall plans. without knowing what to look for or how to go about it a build may be premature at this point. i understand you're excited and have a lot of questions but please go through what we have... pick it apart to no end... and get a decent understanding so you will have much better results with answers to your questions and in your overall outcome. if you take the time to look over everything twice i truly believe you'll appreciate the end result a lot more... cause they'll be that much better.
