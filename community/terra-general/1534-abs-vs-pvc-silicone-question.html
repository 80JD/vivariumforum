ABS vs PVC and Silicone Question
Hey all,
Just picking up some equipment to hopefully begin construction in the next couple of weeks and I seem to have hit a snag.
I'm trying to find PVC piping for the supports for my false bottom and in all of my searching all I can find are 10 foot lengths. I have noticed though that the ABS pipes come in much more manageable (and portable) lengths.
Is ABS a suitable substitute for PVC for this purpose?

Also, I've noticed a couple of times that people specify to get GE Silicone I instead of GE Silicone II. What is the difference?

GE II has a mold inhibitor that can leach into your water.
It seems to be debated... Many claim to have used type II with success in 3-5 year old tanks. I used type II
ALWAYS buy type 1 if available (it was hard to find in black).

If you don't don't have any option but to buy 10ft lengths of PVC then you can just use the connectors. My homedepot sells the PVC in 3 ft lengths too but you have to search around to find them.
