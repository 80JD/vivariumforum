I can't guarantee I'm correct, but it looks to me like you have a spadefoot toad there. If that's the case, than you have the wrong setup. They prefer to dig into the soil.
Look up Spadefoot Toad and see if that's what you have. Like I said I could be wrong, but it looks just like a spadefoot, and I know western spadefoots can be found in that area.
