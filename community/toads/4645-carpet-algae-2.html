Yes, pictures would be preferred. I've been thinking about this off an on and other algae types have come to mind. However, the next one that sounds close likes a lot of strong light. I have some that bothers the pond in the spring before the plants take over (or if I forget to put barley straw in). It too can be swirled off with a toothbrush, although that won't get all of it off because the "base" of the algae is slimey and grows thick.

Water wisteria is quite common in the aquaria hobby. I don't think you will have a hard time finding it in pet stores- and beck is right, you can find cheeper ways of getting some from the forums. I got mine by trading in some of my fancy F2 guppies for credit at a small local petstore. He gave me a huge bag full of the stuff... enough to cover the water surface of three of my tanks.
