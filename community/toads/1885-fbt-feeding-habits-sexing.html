I bred B. orientalis for a while and found there is no set count to feed. One feeding they might eat 8 crickets, the next they might only eat 2 of the same sized crickets.
Your best bet is to offer an adult FBT about 6 prey items and if they eat that in under 15 minutes offer another. If they don't eat the six remove the uneaten prey.
Alsoi be sure to vary the prey if you want them to have a ballanced nutritional diet.
I used Silkworms, Crickets, Meal Worms, Fly Larva, Earth Worms and occasionally Wax Worms.

Edit; Feed Adults two or three times a week and Juvies every day.

Sorry I missed some of your questions.
Sexing
Though it can sometimes be difficult to sex FBTs there are a number of things you can check and watch for.
Females are generally larger than males of the same age and the skin on their backs is generally smoother.
Males are usually smaller than same age females, the skin on their backs is generally rougher, during the mating season males develop nuptial pads on the first and second fingers and males are the ones that sing/croak/bark/etc.
*Note the nuptial pads on males are often present outside of mating season but may be difficult to see.

Color Change
They change color for many reasons. Stress, Temps, backround color and health can all affect their color.

Supplements
The prey insects should be gut loaded and dusted with multi vitamin as well as calcium supplements at each feeding.
