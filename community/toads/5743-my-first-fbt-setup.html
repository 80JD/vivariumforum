It looks great! I posted a comment on YT.

There are a couple of things you should change before putting the toads in:

You need to add a slope somehow, so they can easily get out of the water.

You need to put a rock or something in the middle that either breaks the water, or is just below the surface of the water. They LOVE to sit on rocks like that. Something flat on top would be best. BUT, once you put in a slope, that might not be necessary...

Make sure you check the water area to be sure there are no places they get can trapped under. They LOVE to get underneath rocks and stuff and make you panic, so just be 100% sure that anywhere they can squeeze in, they can squeeze back out.

I would add some broad leafed plants that they can hide under and climb on. Personally,I like pothos for FBTs, but it will outgrow that tank pretty quickly. It will grow anywhere though, so you might be abler to trail it into the water to have an aquatic plant.

That's about all I can think of atm. Care to take a couple of stills so those of us with connection issues can see it without having to load the video?

EDIT: oh, I almost forgot! You do have a lid for that, right? I recommend part glass/plexi and part screen. I'd do the screen over the water portion so that your moss doesn't dry too quickly.
