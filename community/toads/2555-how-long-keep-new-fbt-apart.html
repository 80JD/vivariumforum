What do you mean keeping them apart?? Is "Deuce" a new arrival from a pet store?? In that case I think the rule of thumb is 1 month quarantine (just to be safe) before introducing him to his new tank mate.

If they already "know" each other and you mean housing them in seperate containers while you clean/perform maintenance then as long as you need.



If it's the first scenario, make sure the tank is large enough where the newbie doesn't get picked on for encroching on Chopstick's turf. When I introduced my newer toads to my already established guys, I waited until the new house was complete before throwing them all into the new tank, that way there was no territorial issues, it was new to everybody. Until then, my 3 established guys and my newbies were housed in seperate old hermit crab critter carriers for the summer.

When I had my fellas seperated they would stare at each other's tanks and try to get to one another. FBTs aren't social animals, but they are communal. Meaning they don't have a social order (like pack animals, wolves, coyotes, hyenas, lions etc) and don't necessarily seek one another out, but they do prefer each other's company and tend to hang out in groups.
