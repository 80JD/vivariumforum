Yellow Garden Spiders
They're extremely common around here (and many places) but I've always thought these guys (well... girls) were cool.

Took these pictures in the backyard today. I had two others building along the side of the house, but they seem to have moved on.




More info :
Arthropod Museum, Dept. of Entomology, University of Arkansas

Excerpts :
Quote:
This species occurs in Nova Scotia and southern Ontario, the eastern half of the United States, and south to Guatemala. It also occurs in California and Oregon. It is absent from intervening arid areas.
...
The stabilimentum apparently affords protection, perhaps by camouflaging the spiders, startling predators, or acting as an aposematic warning of the presence of webs. It seems to be especially effective in preventing birds from flying through webs. However, it can also cost spiders the loss of prey, and hungry spiders, which most need to capture prey, are less likely to build stabilimenta
...
The female usually eats her web each day and constructs a new one, often in the same place
-darc

EDIT : It's also really hard to get an auto-focus camera to focus on the spider when there's grass in the background...
