Tarantula and Emperor Community Tank?
Hey so i am asking the scorpion and tarantula communities whether a few emperors and a tarantula would do well if they were housed together?

I am building a sort of mini-zoo in my garage (will have a huge build thread when i get started) and was still narrowing down the animals. THe cage for them that I am planning is about a 30, maybe 40 gallon. It is a custom made wood with an acrylic front. THe top will be a combination of wood and screen (prob about 50-50) and will have live plants, a misting system, and lighting.

I heard that emperors are fairly communal scorpions, but will they get along with a tarantula? I can split the tank if i need to, but i thought it would look cleaner, and much more badass if they were all in one tank..

Also, if it will work, or if i have to split the tank, i was wondering what kind of tarantula to get. The tank will have a fairly high humidty and temps (im guessing 70% and about 75 degrees....but its a guess, i havent set it up yet) but im open to opinions of other tarantulas that would do better in other climates. Emperors are supposed to be chill with varying temps so i would be catering to the spider's needs, not the scorpions'.

I do not want to spend a lot on the spider, def under $50 and would like to go as cheap as I can without getting a crappy animal. I would like it to be handleable, though i probably wont take it out often, maybe once a month. But I would sure like not to be bitten in that time hahah Also, if you guys know any cheap places to get/order them, please tell me. I know my local petshop has some, but i haven't gone back recently to ask their prices.

THe bigger the better, or at least the more badass haha

THanks for your ideas,

-Jeremy
