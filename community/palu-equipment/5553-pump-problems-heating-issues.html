the slowing might have something to do with negative pressure. if it pulls the gasses out of the water there'll be less suction. if the gasses build up over time it'll get worse till it doesn't want to pump.

that's why the fx5 shuts off once every 24 hours... to purge the gasses and let the siphon re-fill the canister.
