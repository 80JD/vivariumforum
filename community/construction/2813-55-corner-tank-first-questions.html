lowcountry, an ultrasonic fogger is not the same as a mister. There are types that you can just pipe the fog in with tubing, and there are types that are meant to sit directly in the water. Creatureman, I am assuming yours is the latter. You need to put it into a cage of some sort so that the frog cannot come in contact with it. They work by using an electric current to instantly vaporize the water. If the frog were to touch the place where the fogger vaporizes the water, he would probably be electrocuted. Putting it in a pond between waterfalls so that it stays full is a really good idea. You could try covering the top of the pool with a piece of eggcrate, covering the eggcrate with river stones, and letting the fog well up from between the rocks. I think it would look cool to have a place where the stream "dissapears" and fog rises out of the rocks. In fact, I think it would be so cool that I think I'll do it myself, lol...

Brown GE II is just as safe as the black (however safe that may be, lol). It should blend in a little better than black if parts of the covering fall of the BG, although black blends fairly well itself.

The wood should be fine, if it has held up under water for so long, being wet from fog or misting shouldn't be to much of a problem for it. It will likely mold to begin with, but that's normal. It will go away after a while. Boiling and baking will of course help keep the mold down. I have a piece of grape wood that gets saturated a couple of times a day (for the past...4 years?) and since I baked it, it has never gotten moldy again.
