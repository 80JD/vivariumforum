proseal is nasty stuff. don't get me wrong it works...really really well. its used a lot in aviation. my father used it to seal the fuel tanks on his airplane that he's building, also the airframers here at work use it on the planes at our squadron. the stuff we use here comes premeasured and is in a nice applicator that you can mix it and apply it easily and cleanly(thats important). the stuff my dad used didn't come premixed. it's a two part epoxy and its imperative that the mix is correct otherwise it won't work all that well. kind of difficult if you don't have a scale or something to accurately measure with. now...when i say it works, its the truth, i have pants that i got some of the stuff on about two years ago and its still there. nothing will take it off that i know of except maybe rhino spit, and i'm not about to try and get that. we had to scrape and sand our tools to get it off, or soak them immediately after application in Methyl Ethyl Ketone (MEK), which is also nasty stuff.

it would really depend on your application. if you need something sealed that youre not sure silicone would work, go for it. if you can find it premeasured and prepackaged tubes go for it, buts its hard to find, sort of expensive, and can turn a good project into a nightmare in a very short time.
