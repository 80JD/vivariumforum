won't turtles tear up the plants though? Even babies are pretty hard on thier environment, no?

I'd suggest floating plants. Duckweed is very popular but it will stick to the turtle's shell and dry up there when the turtles get out of the water to dry off. So maybe something floating but large enough to stay in the water and not get dragged out.

I agree with sinfl; dual T5's with an electronic ballast (instant start) will work.
