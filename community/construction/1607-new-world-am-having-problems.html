new to the world and am having problems

PPl just use black so you can tell if you've covered it well enough and makes no diff for sticking potential. the problem is if you take to long the silicone tends to skin over makeing it much harder to get the Coco fiber or anything else to stick properly, try only doing small sections of the background at a time as it only takes 5 min for the skin to take over and screw up your day, another reason ppl use black Vs clear is the clear will turn cloudy over time and makes spots you missed stick out like a sore thumb where black wont attract the eye as much. Fans or any other thing that would cause the air to move to rapidly will cause the silicone to skin over faster... hope this helps

I have used three different colors-and I prefer the brown and/or black. It does need to be spread on pretty thick. The coco bark or moss needs to be bone dry as well to stick to the silicone. After I spread out the fairly thick layer of silicone (I or II) I take cupfuls of the coco bark and pile them on the silicone. When I used moss I took fist-sized chunks and really smooshed them down with my hands. So far, everything is sticking very, very well!!! (Including of course the silcone to my hands, my hands to anythings else, etc....)!

Looks good, definitely needs more plants though. If you don't mind pruning regularly, dieffenbachia is good for GTF's and Anoles, mine always loved it (I'd stick it inthe back left corner there, looks like a good spot for one, and they like it if it's in the corner). It was my anoles favorite place in the whole tank. Your background is awesome. Just a suggestion, if I were you, I'd get a digital thermometer/hygrometer combo. They have one at walmart that is a little cheaper that measures the ambient temp and humidity, and that should be fine for the frog and anole. I think it was 6 bucks or so. I have a set of those dial ones in my dragon cage, and the humidity gage is off by like 20%. The temp gage isn't off as bad, but it isn't accurate either, and a few degrees can be a big deal.
