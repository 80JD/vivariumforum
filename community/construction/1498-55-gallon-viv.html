what I would suggest.
Now that you have it all setup nice and neat....
Why not deck out that plastic waterfall setup the same way we do the backgrounds with some moss or coco or something? It looks surprisingly good for what it is(i used to have one on a 20L REslider tank at work and it looked like Shite in there) you could use it to make a great custom background and waterfall and nobody would know what was really there.

I would also move that light to the land half of the tank. You'll get less algae growth in the water side, and more down the waterfall and stream(which is where it is most beneficial.)
