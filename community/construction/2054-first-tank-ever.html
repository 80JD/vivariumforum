It sounds bad the way I am gonna type this but no way to get around it. It's fake. I have the exact same piece in my Cichlid tank. Can be bought at Petsmart. If I remember correctly I paid 20.00 for it.

Nice tank. I haven't seen one of those in awhile. My roommate had one some years back. Used it for his Iguana. I love the fleck stone look. With that type of tank to keep it asthetically eye pleasing I would have to say definitely tree frogs with a larger than normal water area. Only becase the vents will cause some evaporation and will keep the humidity up. With that many vents you will be adding water quite often. If you want to cover the vents it would be a great cross draft from the lower end of the tank to the upper end on the other side.
