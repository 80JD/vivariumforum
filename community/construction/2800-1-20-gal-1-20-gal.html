heartagramtc, that is a wonderful idea. I can tell already you are going to be one of our designers.

lowcountry, I see a problem with what you did, unfortunately... It's an easy fix though. It looks like you cut the tank frame and used plexi or something to join the glass together to strengthen the seal; is that right? The problem I see is that you don't have any weight bearing support under the middle where the bottom panes of glass are joined. If the bottoms are tempered glass (most are) and the joint doesn't hold, they may shatter. You can avoid this by getting some of those foam or rubber stick on "legs" to reduce the stress on the joint, just make sure they are the right height. Put at least two on each side of the joint.
I definitely think you should do what heartagramtc suggested, it will also help strengthen the joint. I wouldn't do it with GS though, it may break the joint. It would be easy enough to make something exactly like that out of polystyrene foam and then silicone it in.
So, what are the dimensions of that now? Is it about 24"x24"? If so, four 12" inch tiles will fit it perfectly. Otherwise you could go with a 16"x16" tile and some 4" tiles.

Good luck! I can't wait to see what you do with it.

I am currently working on ideas to cover the seam up.. Than is a good idea about the low wall...

I do have some stabilization under the bottom, i used plexi to stabilize the joint itself from the bottom (the piece on the top was used due to a slight difference in floor heights). I also have some "legs" under it in some stress areas...


What I did to remove the glass is I first used a razorblade, and cut along the silicone in the corners. Then you need to remove both the top and bottom bracing around the piece you want removed. then its a matter ov making sure you take your time, and cut all of the silicone out and the glass will remove pretty easily.. I could have gone without the plexi on the sides, but I thought that the more I strenghtened the bond, the better..


I am going to measure it out today and get the exact dimensions, but it is about 24"x24". If it is, that will make my life a whole lot easier.... Right now I only have their basking rock and tree inside, but I plan to do a whole lot more, just need the $$$ to do it with....

I have a lot of polystyrene at work, so I might mess around with that and see what I come up with.. Thank you for the ideas, and keep them coming!
