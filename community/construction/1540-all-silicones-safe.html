Just to address it, I was able to find GE silicone I at walmart of all places... The kitchen and bathroom one has the mold inhibitor, but the door and window one does not (or at least makes ABSOLUTELY no mention of it anywhere on the packaging). They didn't have the black like I wanted, but they did have clear and white. I bought white so I can see it better. So instead of just looking at the home improvement stores, check and see if your local walmart or whatever carries it. They also had all three types of GS at almost 2 dollars cheaper a can.
