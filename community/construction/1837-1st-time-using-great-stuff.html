before you do anything. silicone the back of he viv you want silicones whether it be just the back or the sides a little too. then silicone in the eggcrate. then great stuff on the egg crate lightly since it expands try to do it like a centimeter away so it will expand into it evenly. if you have problems you can just cut out all the pieces to make the edges straight. then gs everything else. then comes the wood(cork bark, tree fern, driftwood. etc), silicone again like the back of the tank on everything and cocofiber if you are going this route kristy

Also, you can apply vasoline or something else that will make a waterproof barrier to the filter, and then apply the GS, let it cure, then pop out the filter (if you don't want to have the filter BEHIND something). I just made a mold with a bowl for some hides for my BP, and I did this, it worked just fine. Take a look at the https://www.vivariumforum.com/communi...kish-crab.html, in there she talks about how she did her filter in a similar way to what I am talking about. Using this method gives you more options, I think.
