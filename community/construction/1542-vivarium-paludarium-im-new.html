I think you should try using the "egg crate" stuff that so many people on here talk about. It is used for lighting, and is basically a white plastic grid. I haven't made a falso bottom this way yet, but I am going to when I rebuild my CWD's habitat. What you do is you get some of the egg crate, some PVC pipe (for legs), some mesh (to keep the substrate out of the water) and some silicone (either aquarium silicone, or GE type I [I've read that the type II will work, but it is toxic until fully cured]). Like I said, I haven't done this myself yet, but I have read several posts on how to do this. From what I understand, you cut the egg crate to the size you need (the size you want the land portion), and then cover it with the mesh. Then you attach the pvc pieces (cut to whatever height you want the false bottom) to either the bottom of the egg crate, or to the bottom of the tank (I think attaching them to the bottom of the tank will probably be easier, especially if something happens that you will need to remove the false bottom). You will need to attach another piece of the egg crate to the side of the top piece to divide the portion under the land and the water area (you don't want your fish or your dragon getting under there). The piece you attach to the side should also be covered in mesh, I suppose. If your tank is rectangular and you want to have one side land, one side water, you could probably even have that piece cut longer than you need so that it slopes at an angle to create a sort of beach. Now attach rocks or whatever to that piece to make it look nice. As for the land area, you can put a thin layer of gravel down (not absolutely necessary, but it would give the plant roots a place to take up more oxygen as they reach for the water), followed by another layer of mesh (perhaps not as fine as the previous layer), followed by your substrate. I personally recommend sphagnum peat moss (mines brown, where did you get yellow peat moss?). Plants seem to grow very well in this, I have always used it in my vives, and I am still using the same plants years later. You can get a huge bag of it for like 5 bucks from walmart or a garden center, it is recommended that you bake it for a little while to kill any bugs or fungus that may be in there. You can even reuse it if you redo the vive later on, either in plants, or in another vive (bake it first). If you use the peat moss, get it wet in a separate container first, it takes a while to properly absorb the water so that it isn't just floating in it. If you want to have something on top of the peat moss, you can use the coco bedding, or you can just plant grass right in it like I did (the grass is growing very well, and my dragon loves it!). In order to spread the seeds well, I mixed them with some leftover peat moss, and then just spread it on top. It grows in a week or so. I used kentucky blue grass, because we had some left over from planting our yard, but if your dragon is very active, you may want to try a hardier type. You could also plant moss on top of the peat moss, or any turf plant that isn't toxic. Setting it up like this should raise your substrate above the water level, that way it isn't leaching into the water like it has been. Your dragon probably doesn't like drinking it if it is dirty! Another thing that could be turning the water that color could be the dragons waste too, if you aren't cleaning the water out regularly. If you don't plan on making complete water changes, at least get a brine shrimp net (it's white) and net the water area once a day or so. Their urine is solid, so you can get that out along with the feces. If you don't keep the water clean enough, it's going to hurt your dragon, so please make sure it is drinkable; he is going to drink it and use the bathroom in it. As for fish, he may or may not eat them, but they do make the tank look nice. Another suggestion is to have a branch that extends from the land over the water, they seem to like that alot.

One more thing, if you don't want to see the false bottom and layers of substrate through the glass, spread a thin layer of black silicone over the side and when you are done, cut away the excess. Good Luck! I hope I was helpful.
