I have ran free fall waterfalls before, not only is it quite loud most of the time depending on the height of the drop but also it may displace more water due to splashing, so you need to have a pool or reservoir large enough and deep enough to handle this. these are just a couple problems i have seen working with free falls. How high are you thinking of going and whats at the bottom? (ie) rocks, wood etc. and what kind or return pump are you working with?

It is the Depth of the pool of water that is going to help you out here. A more shallow pool is going to splash much more than a deep one. if it's 13" drop I would guess-stimate atleast 6" depth of water to prevent too much splatter.

I was thinking about doing this with my current build, decided against it. I was going to have the fall spill off the top back of my tank, and was going to pour down behind a retaining wall to eliminate splatter. just seemed like it was going to consume too much space in my tank.. If you get one working post us up some pics, maybe I'll go for it in my next viv.
