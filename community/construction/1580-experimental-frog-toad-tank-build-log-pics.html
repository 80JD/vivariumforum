Experimental Frog/Toad Tank: Build Log with Pics.
Ok, I think I have enough done now to start my log.

I'm building this out of an old bookcase (it's made of that particle board stuff, pressboard, whatever you want to call it). The stores in my area did not have any epoxy paint available, so I decided to experiment by coating the inside areas with silicone. (OMG the fumes are sooooo much worse when you spread it so thin with a putty knife!) I have already been advised that this is the "wrong" way, and that I am going to regret it, blah blah blah. So if you have a comment along those lines, please keep it to yourself. This is not intended to be a permanent home for my frogs, I plan to move them to my CWD vive after I build his new home (which I plan to do the "right" way, thank you very much). If it does indeed hold in the moisture properly without expanding the pressboard, then I may decide to keep one of the species in the new vive. (There are 2 squirrel tree frogs, one banana frog, and a FBT in there. Please, nothing about, "you shouldn't mix species" either. They have been together for years and even sleep on one another. They don't like to be separated either.)

So anyway... Since I am building with scrap materials, I had to work around the dimensions of the boards. I also decided to incorporate the ten gallon they are currently in into the design (Yes, I know that is not NEARLY big enough for them, hence the new build and intent to move to the 60 gallon after building for the CWD). The ten gallon tank will hold the water feature, this way, the silicone coated wood is not actually holding any water, other than a little bit from the substrate draining from constant misting (Hopefully I won't have to mist as often since there won't be a large screened area for moisture to escape).

My arms got very tired holding this stuff up... both of these pictures were taken the next afternoon. First one is still laying down, the second is right side up:



Next I did a small section of the BG with a mixture of some separated coco fiber (the fibery part) ground bark shavings (coarsely ground) and larger bark shavings. I though it would lose some of the bumpy texture, but I was apparently wrong... I think I will have to figure out how to redo this part or something, I don't really like it that much.

I think you have already received enough grief over the wood, so I'll leave that be... I hope sealing it with the silicone works for you..

my only critique at this point, even knowing what your restrictions are working with the scraps.......
Lighting over the water.

People like to do this a lot, and it's a simple mistake.

Algae is photosynthetic, therefore the more light you put over water, the more algae it will grow. lots of algae will make your water quality great, but aesthetically nasty. especially with reptile bulbs because they tend to be high in the yellow spectrum (5500, 6500, and 7500 Kelvin) which is the spectrum algae thrives off of.
Animals "Bask" on the land side. so this is where they should be able to regulate their height according to the bulb(according to the heat)
Plants grow toward their light source(which should be up) in a bigger tank you would notice this more, they would grow at an angle toward the top right side of your tank.

otherwise I like the plan, very creative to work with what you have. Can't wait to see your progress.

They're nocturnal... Silly thing... The algae isn't going to be as big a deal anyway, because I am not putting in a filter, so I'll have to change the water every day or two (It won't be that much water either). I know it's an after the fact kind of thing but I'm sure others will find this a helpful tip:

To clean the straw and nozzle on a bottle of Great Stuff simply put some Acetone in it. I do this whenever I have some left in a bottle that I want to save for later. Do this as soon as possible. I like to set up a glass jar to set the can and nozzle in, and funnel Acetone directly into it and let gravity do it's job.
Again, soon as possible to make sure it's clean.

Since I make casts out of the Great Stuff often around halloween it's important I can reuse a partially emptied bottle.
Hope that helps!
