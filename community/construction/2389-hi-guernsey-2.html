I have added a bit more wood & more plants -
I am experimenting with the air conditioning, I have three fans fitted, one for internal circulation & one that draws from the outside air. The third fan is linked to the sump & can supply very humid & slightly warmer air than is in the tank.
I have added a air stone to the sump & a automatic top up to compensate for evaporation, I have linked the top-up valve to a R/O unit however I am using water from my planted fish tank to mist the tank.
At present, I am still hand misting about one litres a day but I have been fiddling with various options to automatically supply rain & mist.
One idea I have been playing with involves a pressurised cylinder & a solenoid valve, I am just waiting for a seconds timer to arrive.
