You've gotten farther than me so far!!! I had the case almost built, and then realized that I must have been on crack when I first calculated my measurements, so now I have to take it all apart and start over on that part!

It looks good so far, but I have a couple of questions...

First, what did you seal the interior wood with? It still looks like plain wood in your picture... Remember this is a very high humidity enclosure, so you have to be sure your wood will not warp or rot.

Second, what are you doing for lights? Your top is entirely enclosed, I see, and it is dangerous to have the lights INSIDE the enclosure, because if they are able to, they will try to hang upside down from the bottom of the lights, and you don't want to risk burns! I know you said two uvb and one uva lights, but what are you doing for ambient heat and basking heat?

Third, a screen front is a really bad idea for CWDs, because the screen will not hold in your humidity... The only way you will be able to keep a constant 80% is too keep the room that humid, if you use a screen front. If you can't afford glass ATM, then go with some plexi... You can get away with using the thin stuff for a while, but it will eventually warp from the heat. That way you can save for the glass.

Fourth, other than thinking about using a screen front, what are you planning for ventilation? You CAN go with an entirely closed tank, but you will have to at least move the air around so that it doesn't stagnate. I'm planning on using my current screen lid for the tank mine is in right now, and keeping part of it covered like I do now, and also having part of the top made of wood.

As for fogger placement, I think you would get better results from having the inlet into the tank up high, that way the fog falls to the bottom, instead of having to rise to the top. I could be wrong, but I just think that would work better.

One more thing, if you are planning to have a substrate that will support live plants, I would recommend using the marine epoxy at least for the bottom of the enclosure, if not the entire inside. If you don't want to do that, or can't find it, you can line the bottom with plexi, like I am doing, or you can coat the bottom with silicone (which is going to end up MORE expensive than lining with plexi).

Hope this doesn't come off wrong, I just want to make sure you have thought of everything BEFORE you get it finished!!!

I promise I wasn't criticizing you!!! Just making sure you had though of everything... I've been planning my large build for some time now, and I find I have to keep going back and redoing things because I didn't think of them before! I wanted to do sliding doors, but it isn't working out for me at all... So I bought some wood I could use to sort of make a three layer sandwich around the glass to frame out some doors. The reason I couldn't do sliding doors is because I could not find any sort of track anywhere... Besides, the way I am planning it now, I will be able to completely open the doors and fold them along the sides to get them completely out of the way for maintenance. I don't think UTH's would be a good idea with wood, they don't work properly because the wood absorbs the heat but doesn't release it as much, does that make sense? You CAN however, put an aquarium heater in to help with heating the enclosure and aide with humidity, just make sure it wont warm your water too much. I got one that is preset to 78 degrees and rated for 2 to 5 gallons for the tank I currently house my CWD in. I ended up taking it out though because his heat lights are now over the top of the water, and they warm it up enough on their own. The UV strip lights should be fine inside the enclosure, but I would do a test on how hot your particular bulbs get before you install them. Mine are barely warm to the touch, so I think those should be fine in the enclosure. If you want to be really sure, install them where you want them, and then put a wire cage around them so the dragon can't reach. He may hang upside down from the screen though, lol... I'm not sure about diagonal placement of your heat lights though... I'll have to think on that one for a while.
