Wow, you did a great job so far, even though no one answered your questions! I must have missed this one. I have gotten behind on the threads and haven't gotten caught up yet. What are you using to glue the styrofoam together? I love your "root", you got the shape perfect for it. You did a great job with those rocks too, you have a bit of an artistic side, don't you?

If you are planning to house darts, from what I understand they don't HAVE to have a pool, but it is a good idea to have a shallow one so they can get wetter if they want to. So, you could go with a FB (it is going to take up 3-6 inches, depending on the substrate depth needed for your plants), or you could go with the LECA. I think you might be better off with the LECA, based on what I think you want for a water feature. If you use the LECA, you can have a small fountain pump buried in the LECA, circulating your water up to the water feature and making sure it does not stagnate (this would be the best way to have the water simply recirculate through the substrate). Also, with the LECA, if you need a deeper area for soil for a plant, you can easily hide a planter down in the LECA to allow for deeper soil. You could also do without terrestrial plants and use epiphytic plants, then cultivate some Java moss for a substrate. It would grow quickly and make a gorgeous carpet, and you wouldn't need a separate substrate, it could just grow right on top of the LECA. Or, you could use terrestrial plants in buried planters and do the Java moss in the areas around the plants. I'm sorry this is all a bit late, but I hope it still helps.

Ok, a question... Can you still remove the BG, or is that GS sealing it to the glass? If you want to run a water feature down the BG you are going to need to run a tube behind it. If it is sealed, you can always cut across the foam under your "root" so that you can remove that part, carve a channel in the back for your tube, and then replace it.


I think a good place for the outlet would be the red mark (with the tube pointed at the back of the tank so it will flow down the root, rather than just over the side there), and a good place for the channel (carved into the back side) would be the pink mark. That would make the water run down your root, and I think that would look very cool. You'd have to figure out a good way to carve the channel for the tube to pass through the part that is sealed to the glass with the GS, but it shouldn't be too hard. You could always break the seal between the GS and the glass and remove the whole thing in one piece too, that way it would be easier. If you do it like that and have the water outlet where I marked, if it doesn't flow the way you want, you can carve a tiny notch in the GS in places where you want the water to trickle over the edge, and it will follow the easiest course down (the way you want it to).

Hope that helps!

EDIT: I changed the pic a little bit. The blue line is where I suggest you cut the foam to pull out the bottom section to carve the channel.
