Hey, sorry about the slow progress! I bought some Pothos, English ivy and two small pink polka-dot plants (bit girly but its some colour right?!) in the plant nursery last week. I’d been in looking for Pothos before but they had none so this time he remembered me as ‘the FROG guy’ and had one put away for me, nice guy! It was labelled as 26 euros (around 41 dollars, Ireland is ridiculously expensive now!!!) but he said since id been in before, and they didn’t have it, he’d give me it for €6 ($10)!!!! First thing I thought was wow, that’s a MASSIVE loss for him but when I got it home and went to re-pot it the roots were 98% rotten , but for 6 quid, I’m not complaining!
