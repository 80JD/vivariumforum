Hot Glue : The misadventures of a noob! PT1
Sooo my tank collapsed . seems there are a couple different kinds of hot glue and some cant take the humidity or much pressure.. i joined my false bottom egg crate to 2 sides and one front piece with some crap i found in my wife's craft bucket...3 weeks later the weight of the gravel pushing in on the sides cracked alll the joints and filled my water supply with dirt, choked my Fluval and burnt it out. a couple heavy stones i used as filler under the soil to reduce the amount needed to fill shifted and i came home to a swamp and a powerhead spraying sludge all over the front sheet of glass and soil blasting the plants leaf's right off. Death Toll = 3 plants 2 clumps of mold and 1 ego ..but the family of fruit flys made it out ok
