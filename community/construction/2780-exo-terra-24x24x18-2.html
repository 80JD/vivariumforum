Leca, Screen, Sphagnum Peat Moss.

if you want more precise info, we took pieces of Plexiglass that we cut to fit, then siliconed the sides in with aquarium silicone. as for the gaps in the background we first used Expanding Foam, then pressed it in to "pack" it in. then once that cured, we again applied some more silicone to the back wall and the sides of where the Expanding Foam was.

thanks for your comments!

so answering your questions.
1. we didn't HAVE to glue them in because they were so tight. but we just put a "seal" around the front of the PVC.

2. for the back pipe, we made "channels" so the pipe would sit in the channels when pressed up against the back wall. that way there is minimal space between the BG and glass.

3. so as for the drainage, my aunt drilled holes in the bottom of the PVC to help with the drainage, but other wise it just drains partially out the back. we blocked part of the back to prevent loss of the SPM.
yes that is a piece of bark, its from an oak tree. its a perfect shape for a planter too.
