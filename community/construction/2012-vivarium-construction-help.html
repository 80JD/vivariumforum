1. Do I need to put a screen over the eggcrate, and over the hydrotron substrate? Or will that increase the chances of spoilage?

The screen is needed to make sure the earth doesn't fall in the water part of your tank. If you are planing on planting, it is pretty much a must.

2. When I put soil in, how much should I put in? I'm thinking of a couple inches.

just enought to make the plants you have get a firm starting grip.

3. Creating a waterfall, where do I put the pump? Down under the false bottom? Or do I keep it outside in a bucket of water? I have a 3/4" PVC for siphoning the false bottom in case of spoilage and needing to change out something.

Lets see, outside reservoir is a very good way to do it if you have the time, space, money to do it. Actually, an outside reservoir+reservoir in the tank gives you a much bigger water quantity. And the more water you have, the easier it is to control your water quality. Outside reservoir is also very good for maintainence. See it as the same principle as a reef tank.

4. Is 10-11" enough head room for the amphibians/plants in the vivarium?

hard too answer this one....you will get ground or tree amphibians? For the plants, it will all be based on their growth.

5. Misting, am I fine using a 32oz water spray bottle?

With water feature, and 2 shoots a day, you will not deviate to much from the 80-90% humidity.

6. What happens to the vivarium if and when I have to move? I live in an apartment so leases expire. When I move out, what will be the easiest way to move without affecting the vivarium or amphibians?

Are we talking about a 60g full of water/earth and stones? or a 15 gallon? You have to be real, and think for yourself on this one. No matter what, it will be better for you to remove your animals from the terra, keep them in a small container for transport. The terra, you deal with it the way you can.

Ask away. As far as a good "starter" frog in black and yellow, take a look at Dendrobates leucomelas. Black and blue - Dendrobates azureus or Dendrobates tinctorius. Dendrobates auratus is available in black and blue and also in black and green. All are beginner frogs.

As far as a false bottom, I can give you a very basic explanation. You raise eggcrate off the bottom of the tank about 2 inches, usually with sections of pvc pipe or something similar. Then you lay down some window screen, then your layer of substrate. Basically what the FB does is allow excess water to drain from the substrate and down in to the area under the eggcrate. This keeps the substrate from getting too wet and muddy and prevents plant roots from getting to wet and rotting. That is it in a nutshell. There is more to it than that but that is the most basic explanation.
