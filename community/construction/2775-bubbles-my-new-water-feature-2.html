2 days should be sufficient... bottled or filtered water should be used. recording how long it takes to bubble will be very important to tell which gs will work. volume of water will have to be the same to do this let's say 1 gallon. the one that bubbles fastest should be assumed to be the worst and slowest = best (to use). temp should also be the same... say about 78 deg... most aquarium heaters are set at that temp... so anyone else can continue the same. buckets will only have to be clean and food safe. homer buckets will do as they have never held anything that can add to a failure. i don't think egg crate will help. i think total volume of gs will have to be the same... say a coffee cup full. we can line it with wax paper, fill it, trim it to level off the top, remove it and dump it in the water to test. curing time will also be important... same stuff 3 curing times. example: test 1 @ 2 hours cure time... test 2 @ 24 hours cure time... test 3 @ 72 hours cure time.

volume of gs will be directly related to volume of any chemicals producing bubbles. if the gs takes on any water this will make a great difference imo. i agree that if it does not saturate it will not matter. i also think that bubbles would not happen if water is not penetrating the surface. using the same amount shaped the same will assure not only the same surface area but the same volume in general therefore making your experiment more stable and your findings more accurate. if you do this on a time line or just observe volume of bubbles produced you can be sure of your findings either way. you can even do both and cross reference.

I definitely have some gs uncovered in the water of the tank. I used a few squirts on the back sides of the false bottom to keep it from shifting or moving. I did give it plenty of time to dry (or I believe I did, a full week)


Materials used, (dates were all good) Red Cans of Gs; Ge 1 Silicone; white egg crate from Home depot. Eco earth from Petco and clay pellets, with black aquarium gravel to hide the fb; and fiberglass screen.The pump is a Dueto 100 on its side set at 50%flow. For the discharge tube I used reinforced 1/2 inch tubing with plastic elbows.

If this foaming continues I will pull out the false bottom and raise the Gs above the water line. After it dries I will reseal the cut away edges with silicone and hide the gap with clay pellets and substrate.
