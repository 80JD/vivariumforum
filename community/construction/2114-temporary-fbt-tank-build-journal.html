Temporary FBT Tank (build journal)
Okay, so I decided it is time to redo my FBT tank that is in my classroom. I have been wanting to redo it for a while, but now I have an excuse...it seems that one of my FBTs has disappeared. I'm pretty sure it didn't escape on its own, so I want to tear down the tank to make sure it didn't get trapped somewhere and perish. I'm wondering if a student thought they could have a five-fingered discount and took it out of the tank. It wouldn't be easy to do, though, thus my confusion

Anyway, here is a journal of my temporary tank build.

I'm am moving Diego into a 6 gallon for now. I know its kinda small, but he'll be the only one in there. I forgot to take a pic of the eggcrate with just the screen on it, so the first pic is of the GS around the edges of it. I let the GS cure for about 36 hrs.

I put silicone around the bottom edge to seal the GS. Next, I siliconed and applied moss to each side, one side at a time. Once the sides were done, I did the same for the top edges. Another 24 hrs curing.

More to come...

While the curing was going on, I cut 4 pieces of pvc pipe into 3 1/2 inch lengths, and drilled six holes in each so water can flow through without getting stagnant.

Once my silicone was dry, I siliconed the PVC to the eggcrate. Waited 24 hrs again.

I skipped some pics here again (sorry), but once the PVC silicone had cured, I siliconed the bottom of the PVC to the bottom of the tank.

I put coco-fiber on the screen of the eggcrate, rinsed all the dirt off the roots of the plants, and arranged the plants to provide some shelter. Then I put the rocks in the bottom, put an Elite Mini submersible filter in and added the water.

I also put a picture of a rainforest area as a background to give the tank more depth.

So far it is maintaining a temp of 85 degrees. I haven't been able to check the humidity yet, as my gauge is in the other tank, but it fogs up the window a lot! I still have to put something in there to help Diego get out of the water and onto the land area, but I'll probably do that tomorrow.

What do y'all think?
