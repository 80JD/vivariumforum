Anyway folks, i'll pop a few posts up on the steps i've been through already and we'll work from there. Hope you enjoy it, i am!!!

So the first step was to put the false bottom together, I couldn’t find ‘egg crate’ here so I bought some plastic air vents and broke them apart and got something that I think will do the job! I bought an aquarium pump and a bit of wood I thought had character for my water feature. When I decided where to put it all I used a hacksaw to chop up the plastic and hot glued it together (hop that holds!).

Once that was all set I popped in some pea gravel and weed barrier where the planting section will be. Then i popped some into the water area too, I like it, I think its got character, I hope it looks well once the moss has grown in. Fingers crossed!!

Tonight im just after putting some expanding foam over the lid of the pump box and I put the wood for the water feature on top, so hopefully when I lift the wood, the lid will come up too so I can change the pump filter easily, that’s the plan anyway!! I left some pipe in the foam that I can pass the pump hose through later.

So thats up to date, i'd love feedback or advice guys!! Cheers for reading!

Hey folks! Thanks for the replies!

countryboy, i bought the vents in Woodies DIY (Ireland) but im guessing you can pick them up in any DIY store, the biggest i could find was 9" square so it takes a bit of hot gluing, they have wide flat edges so its easy to get a good join. Hope that helps. They cost around 6.50 euros but everything is expensive here so i'd say they're less in other countries!

Pandion59, thanks for the reply, i'll keep'em coming, lookin forward to seeing your viv!!

I put silicon (Non fungicidal / black) and coco fibre coat onto the background and water feature tonight so as soon as its dry I’ll be laughing! I love the way it looks now but hopefully it will look well when its dried in about a WEEK because its so thick!! I hot glued and then siliconed over the water pipe so its'll look like the water runs from under the cocofibre. The pics are below so tell me what ya think.

Thanks again folks, keep the feedback coming!

Hey folks! So the background and water feature are dry (i hope!) and I’ve popped them into the tank. I bought a hood for the tank and i popped a 60w sun-glo and 13w repti-glo for the plants (moon-glo comes later!). The exo terra hood says max 26w, but its the best place for the sun-glo, i hope it doesn’t explode!! I've wired up a US style screw-in light fitting (managed to finally find one!) with an Irish plug, im going to drill the hood and use that fitting for the 60w sun-glo and, fingers crossed, no explosions!!

Im getting 28c-29c (83-85f) at the top and 24-25c (75-77f) at the bottom of the viv right now, and its been sitting for a while. Bit high, what do you think folks? Pics below, any suggestions/feedback just shout!
