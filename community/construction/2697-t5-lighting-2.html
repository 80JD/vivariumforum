its a highly reflective material used in grow rooms and stuff, for covering walls. It looks like a mirror pretty much, but the kind im gettings is pixelated, not that it matters with floruescents but with a HID lamp, it would make all the difference. The reason its pixelated is to diffuse the centered light source of the HID. If it were a straight mirror, it would have a center point as a lightsource, giving the plant a sunburn. This causes it to be scattered and helps to not do that. I like it cause it looks cooler, and it wont matter in my case, it will just reflect better than anything purchasable now.
