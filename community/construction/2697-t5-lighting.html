I've changed things up a bit, and went with 2 foot bulbs, with 3 ballasts running 2 bulbs each. theres 6 bulbs, which will be on 3 different timmers, to give a nice dawn and dusk effect. I bought 3 neutral white, and 3 green LED's (green because that doesn't interupt photoperiods of plants). I am not sure which ones im going to use first because i want it to look nice viewable wize, but may just settle with the green's because it wont mess with anything. The woodwork is going to be my lighting system mount, and is basically plywood with the ballasts on top, and lighting on bottom. After i get mylar on tuesday i will screw all the wood together, then run my wiring through the plywood to the top. Then i'll cut the mylar to shape, and glue it to the wood, then screw in my T5 sockets so that i dont have to mess with the mylar around them. Then that will be ready, and wiring will be done. So till then, this is all i leave you with.
