Never trust wood cut by Home Depot...
OMG, I have been soooooo mad today... I have been trying to get the CWD vive BUILT, as in the enclosure and the cabinet to go underneath, not any of the BG stuff or anything... and I have come to realize, now that I have the cabinet part built, that you cannot trust Home Depot to cut your boards to the size you ask for, NOR can you trust them to even cut them straight!!!!! I got my cabinet built, and it is not level... I didn't measure the boards before I started building, and I can't take it apart because it is glued AND screwed, and on the left I have 24 inches on the front, and 24.5 on the right... On the back, I have 24.25 on the left, and 24.75 on the right!!!! GRRRRRRRRR!!!!! Stupid jerk... Not only that, but that board I was measuring across the back is ONE board, so it isn't even cut STRAIGHT!!! The darn thing isn't level in ANY direction...
I guess I am going to have to sand it level, but it is going to be SUCH a pain... How much of an issue would I face later if I don't sand it? The level says it is ALMOST level, but not quite. If I don't sand it, I am going to have to put my water area on the opposite side that I wanted it, so that is is "downhill", so to speak... I guess I am just going to have to sand it... Maybe I can do that with a sanding wheels that came with the drill bit set I bought... Otherwise, it is going to take FOREVER! ugh... I hate even thinking about it...

Anyway, a build log for this tank is coming soon... I have to take some pics of what I did today when I find my camera.

Get a wood planer to take the excess off. It'll be much, much faster than sanding. You should be able to get one at Home Depot or Lowe's. Like this: http://www.hardwarestore.com/media/p...4_front200.jpg

My dad (and his brother) own a company that is a distributor of doors, wood trim, etc, and a few windows here and there. This means we have unlimited acces to multiple tools, including the easiest way to cut seets of wood: the table saw. This eliminates us having to get anything cut by any idiot at home depot. But for what you're using it for, a circlular saw would be a great tool to have. I'd definately get one. Get an electric one, unless you want to pay a lot of money for a good cordless one.

Just make sure you practice on a scrap piece of wood with the planer before going at the cabinet. Even though it sounds like anything would be an improvement over the 1/2" off wood.
