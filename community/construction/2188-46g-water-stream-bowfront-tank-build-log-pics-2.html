Wow, that's just great. Keep going like this and you might get a sticky!!! I think newts or toads would be great in there. I wouldn't suggest mixing an anole with a FBT unless you have kept both of them before. I have kept a FBT and anole together with no problems, but I don't recommend mixing if you don't have the experience. That said, I don't think you have enough land area for an anole. They need lots of climbing space and ground space to hunt for food. Maybe someone else will have more suggestions for what to put in the tank.
