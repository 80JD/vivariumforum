GS floats VERY well, lol... I made an island in my frog tank, and I did not silicone it down because I wanted it to rise and sink some with the water level, and (lol) it floats a little more than I would like... It wouldn't be an issue if I had found some way to at least keep it in one place before I added my critters... My FBT's REGULARLY push it across the water area (thank goodness there isn't much room for it to go, other than where it is supposed to be). You should be able to make a perfect landing like those turtle docks with it, just make sure you rig it up to stay in place horizontally, but still have room to move up and down.

You shouldn't need to cover the polystyrene with GS, and I have read many logs where people were using it, and the generally cover it with concrete (habbacrete is supposed to be safe for pets without the long curing time or whatever) and then paint it with acrylic paints, I believe (just keep in mind that acrylic paint IS water based). You could also cover it with silicone and then milled peat moss/coco fiber/moss/sand/rocks/whatever (as long as it is safe for your herp).
