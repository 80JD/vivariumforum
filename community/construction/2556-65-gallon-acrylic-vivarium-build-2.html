Personally I found that a rain system looked better & worked better than a mister system.
I have approx 7 gals of water in my sump, I find I use about 3 gallons a week.
I have a water fall on 24/7 & I hand mist 1 or 2 times a day, the rain comes on in the evenings for 20 mins at tea time because we all like to watch it!
I top up the sump with mature water from my well fertilized planted fishtank this seems to work very well.
I have fitted several fans, one in the lid that draws from the room & two on the inside walls for internal air circulation.
I also have an external light source that shines a spot on the front glass this stops any condensation that the fans cant handle.
Designing the rain system was very easy simply drilling 0.5mm holes into 3 lengths of pvc pipe, just keep drilling until you match the pumps outlet & get the desired effect.
Lastly try not to get any water on the front glass when misting or raining as this is a pain to keep cleaning.
I hope some of that help you. Fox.
