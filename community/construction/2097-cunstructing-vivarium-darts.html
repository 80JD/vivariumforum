Well I hope you don't think that we percieve you as stupid? Just trying to make sure we can help in the best possible way. Start with the basics. What size tank? I just saw a post here about a substrate from a manufacture called Aqua Design Amano and it seems at this point this is the only way to do a tank without getting the "brown" colored water. Many will say that the colored water is very good for a PDF tank though. It helps reduce stess and also with the natural chemicals in the water helps the "eco-system" get established. Alos, it can help reduce the necessity to have alot of substrate changing due to the natural pollutants(frog poop). Mold is a normal issue when 1st establishing a set up and it should clear itself up after a week or two. You can speed up the process or atleast controll it by introducing springtails into the viv. They will usually dispose the waste products ie; mold. I am on my way out the door but hopefully some other will help with some input. Hope this helps.
