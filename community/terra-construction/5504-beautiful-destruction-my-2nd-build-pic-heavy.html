Beautiful Destruction (My 2nd Build) **Pic Heavy**
SOOO after my first Terrarium i knew i was hooked..even before i was done with the first i was thinking of what i could do better the next time...

Well here it is. I had a 20Gallon Long just sitting around..i cleaned it and got my brain moving on ideas..and i came up with something that ive never seen before.
The main theme is going to be a mini rainforest being cut down...u will get the idea when you see the pics...Anyway i dont have a hole lot done..so dont get to excited just yet...

Im going to be making a False bottom..i really like the idea of having a false bottom as apposed to just having gravel or clayballs...it gives me the ability to really create the landscape that i want...

What i might do with the cut down tree is cut a hole in the Trunk(for the tube) and then cut out a groove in the tree...and then have that fall into a little pond of some sort..OR i might just build a Waterfall in the back left corner...idk yet so we shall see.

Anyway here are the pics of what i have done...you can see the current inhabitant of my tank...its my cat "Ninja" lol O and dont mind all the bottles of Alcohol...
