The best way to dry coco peat without the risk of burning it is to take a box fan and 2 air conditioner filters and sandwich the coco peat in between the filters. Then bungee the filters to the box fan and prop it up at an angle and turn it on high. After about 2-3 hours the coco peat inside is completely dry.

When cutting Egg Crate material to make your background or false bottom, if you have a left over piece of egg crate that so happens to be long yet narrow like a standard ruler, you can use that for measuring out your detailed areas.

Sometimes things look good in paper, but when constructed we tend to see our errors, Its helped me to use egg crate to accurately measure what I need to cut. I also say that cable ties are your BEST FRIEND!

I can not express that enough, they are reliable and allow you to tie up egg crate pieces, and if you mess up its not hard to clip off the tie, and use another one. Another tip, when using the plastic ties, make sure you check them all before you cut the excess, sometimes other cable ties become loose when installing several of them to make pieces work together.

trimming eggcrate; the screwdriver method is by far the fastest way to get a rough cut. simply decide where you need trimmed and slam the shaft of a screwdriver down through all the tabs in one swift movement. once your piece is cut you can use the side of the screwdriver and slide it back and forth quickly with a little pressure to take off a fair bit of any rough edge too.
