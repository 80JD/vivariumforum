For now I'm just going to paint the inside with stone look spray paint. It's non-toxic and safe for foam, and I have used it in other tanks. I'm going to sand the skin off of the GS with drywall sandpaper, then give it a couple of coats. It should absorb into the foam a bit and make it look more natural than if it was just sitting on the skin. I have a grey one and a brown one, I'm thinking of using them together, some parts brown, some parts grey. I'll be painting the outside of the topper with black krylon fusion, that way it won't be so much of an eyesore. I want to paint the outside of the tank too, but I can't move it with that much water, wood, and dirt in it, and I don't want to completely empty it.

I think I'm going to have to make a new "roof" for the topper. I need to install the light that I bought INTO the topper, and I don't want to cut the screen top I have. The ones for that size are expensive... So I think I'm going to use a piece of eggcrate. How well will that hold up to the heat of the incandescents, you think? Because I'm going to use this heat lamp, I might not need as many incandescents, but I'll probably swap for CFLs for the plants. I MIGHT get rid of my light hood, I'm not sure yet. I might put it on the fish tank, use the lights that are on it for the frogs, and use the clamp lamps that are on the frogs for the CWD tank. That way I don't have to juggle placement when I install the heat light. I'm still not sure what I'm going to use to cover the "roof" to keep the moisture in. I'm thinking maybe plexi, with holes cut for the lights. That might not work though, the heat might be too much. I'll have to see. Anyone have suggestions for something cheap (or that I might have around the house, that would be even better) that would be less likely to warp or melt than the plexi?
