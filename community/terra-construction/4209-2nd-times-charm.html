The dracaena in the upper left is going to quickly outgrow the area it is in as well. They grow pretty fast. Here's a neat site I just stumbled across: Dracaena. It has info on the different varieties of dracaena.

As for adding a pump to the LECA, it would be a good addition, but there are lots of people that use LECA without a pump... The risk for anaerobic bacterial growth is definitely higher, but if you don't let it stay super wet all the time it shouldn't be too much of a problem.

I do like the way it looks. It's a very nice design. Are those real lichens? Cut straight from a tree and then glued on? I've been afraid to do that in the past, but the more I think about it... I LOVE the look of them. I've seen some really nice ones in the area too.
