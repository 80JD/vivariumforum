There are plenty of topics on how to make fake rocks, look through them before you make a topic. you can use great stuff and a powdered concrete patcher or a thin set mortar to make the fake rock, make sure you sand the great stuff or carve it before you put anything onto it. If you want to, you can put the mortar/concrete patch coating onto the GS then when it dries cure the ph and then you can paint it with non-toxic acrylic paint.

There is also a thread somewhere where someone made their own fake vines. I'm not sure which one it was, and I am about to leave for work, but if you can't find it with a search I can look for it later tonight or maybe tomorrow. You can either paint foam to make fake rock (make sure it is a plastic safe paint or the solvents will dissolve the foam), or you can cover them with silicone and coco fiber/peat moss/sand/pebbles/etc, or use the mortar/thin set/concrete method. I've even read something on another site where someone made a homemade "playdough" type stuff and baked it, then sealed it, to create fake rocks and hides (probably not going to be waterproof, even if it's sealed really well, or at least I don't think I would trust it).
