my 2nd attempt at a viv
Well, to begin with, i want to thank everyone on this forum for all the help and ideas prior to my build. I did extensive research prior to this build because i felt i rushed through my first and wasn't as happy with the outcome as i had hoped. So, with that being said, i hope you will give me some feedback on my construction of this attempt.
the start.jpg
my zoomed 18x18x24 with driftwood.
egg crate 2.jpg
I did the false bottom and framework for background with eggcrate. i also used plastic dixie cup for plant potters.
coco fiber covered1.jpg
I covered the egg crate with g/s foam and then carved it to my liking. Then i mixed spagnhum moss and coco fiber and attached to the background with silicone.
new viv and stand.jpg
I then plantted it after the fumes resided." brom, suculent, dracenena, ivy".
I made the interior fan that i picked up from one of the forums on here.(sorry, can't remember who originally posted it" but thank you.
I added the zoo med reptifogger."love,love,love it"
I am using the exo terra 18" hood with (1) 6500 13v cf daylight bulb, and (1) zoo med cf ultra sun bulb.
i build the stand from scatch. left over material from some of my side work.
view of waterfall and plants.jpg
Here you can see the waterfall that runs down the driftwood.

Well, that what i have so far. Let me know what you think.
