Well I got some glass for the front part on the new one I'm building. I just set it in with silicone earlier today. I'll be attaching silver window screen to both sides of that glass, also with silicone.



I made the opening in the front using a heated plastic knife. For the old one I did pretty much the same thing. I just cut out a hole in the lid and in the side I wanted to be the front. Then I siliconed in some silver window screen. The rest is just like a normal tank adding dirt and whatever else. The new one is going to be less sloppily done.
