Hey Tindomul1of9, by grout I am referring to the same grout you use on tile floors. You can buy it at any Home Depot or Lowes etc. Do a search on the forum for "grout", and you will get a lot of advice from people who know a lot more than I do. I plan on either putting down pond liner or silicone in order to make it completely water proof. You can use some non-toxic sealer for water proofing but I believe they will all break down over time. The canister I am using is the Penn Plax Cascade Canister Filter 500. It has adjustable valves to control water flow. I did a test run and in order to achieve the flow I am looking for I have to keep the valves wide open. If running your canister under your viv (in cabinet) like I am, I would recommend the model 700. It would give you more adjustability. That model is harder to prime than others I have used, but it is at a nice price level.
