I'm not sure what the humidity is in there. I have a large water bowl and an exo-terra waterfall in there, and I keep the substrate moist. I don't think the current one is very good a retaining humidity because all the openings are covered in screen. Hopefully the glass I'll be adding to the new one will help keep the humidity higher. Quote:
Originally Posted by Shane
Hello. Ive been trying for a long time to gget red eyed amored skinks(croc skinks there is so many names) but the only thing i have ever found to get these is a site which stopped selling them. can you tell where me where you got them
A lot of places sell them, but I think you should reconsider getting one. They're very shy and very picky. Also if you do decide to get one check for reviews on the place that is selling them. I bought three of them from lllreptiles and two of the three died really fast. I still have one of them but that's more luck than anything else. I looked up reviews after the fact and a lot of people had similar problems. Research ahead of time saves you the trouble. If you do decide to get one try buying from a place where they are either just freshly imported or else captive bred. The longer a wild caught one is in the hands of people who sell them for a living the longer they'll be left in a bone dry cage with crickets crawling all over them.
