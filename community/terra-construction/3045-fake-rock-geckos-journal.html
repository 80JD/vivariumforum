Fake rock for geckos journal
Here is the start to my tank for my geckos, the idea is to make it a desert vivarium. Seeing as there is so little info on desert vivaria I thought I would start something.

I built the basis of the rocks with polystyrene which I glued with a glue gun(didn't work just melted it) and a glue called "No More Nails"

Once the background was built in Polystyrene I then used tile grouting to beging forming the rocks, will post that later.
