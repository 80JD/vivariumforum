29 Gallon PDF Build!
After lots of research, I've started my build. So far, I have the 29 gallon tank, background done (it's almost dry! Yay!), all my plants are here and ready, substrate is assembled, and lighting has been decided.

Lighting:
I'll be using this light
(PLEASE tell me if that seems like a bad idea!)

Substrate:
I'm mixing cocofiber (coir), peat moss, charcoal, orchid bark, and Black Gold's Natural & Organic Potting Soil (screened earthworm castings, Canadian Sphagnum peat moss and perlite. I'll be baking or microwaving this before adding it to the mix so nothing unwanted grows).

This mix will sit atop 2.5-3" of LECA divided by fiberglass screen

Plants:
Climbing Ficus

Unknown Vine


Verigated heart fern


Lemon Button Fern


Palm


Pink Polka Dot Plant


Wandering Jew & Random Vine


Pilea 'red stem tears' & Macodes petola (Both from Josh's Frogs)


Cryptanthus 'Corrine"


Cryptanthus 'Pink Starlight'


Cryptanthus 'Ruby'


Cryptanthus 'Nova Star'


Neoregelia 'Olens'


Neoregelia 'Pheasant'



Phalaenopsis Orchids




My friend is getting me some riccia as ground cover and I also have some dried live oak leaves for the areas without moss.

Background:
I decided to use the Titebond method after reading Grim's posts on it. I am SOOOO glad I did! I LOVE how it turned out! The more it dries, the more natural it looks. I put it on about 1/4" thick max in some places so it is taking a few days to dry, but the cracks look so nice!

I started with a HUGE sheet of styrofoam insulation which I cut to the demnsion of the back wall of the tank. I wanted to be able to remove the background if at some later date I want to change it, and I didn't want to have to deal with curing a million layers of Great Stuff and spreading black silicon all over the place. I "carved" the foam piece with a styrofoam file to make it rougher so the GS and Titebond/peat could stick to it. I did use a little GS to attach grape vine pieces and add some depth. I will be using silicon to attach the background in key places and covering the exposed pink styrofoam with black aquarium background on the outside of the tank. After the carving, GS, and curing, I added a mix of Titebond III wood glue and peatmoss. The Titebond mix went on fast and looks great IMHO. I think the vines will really be able to grab onto it. The 3 largest pieces of grapevine will be homes for the 3 orchids.
