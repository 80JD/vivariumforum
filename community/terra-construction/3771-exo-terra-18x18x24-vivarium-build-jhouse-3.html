Go pick up a sterilite container (I like the ones with the locking lids) and if you don't have a drill or a soldering iron/wood burner, grab one of those too. Then put a row or two of small holes around the top edge of the container, near the lid, and some holes IN the lid, all for ventilation. The add your egg carton, food and water sources, and your crickets! I recommend heating them with a heat mat, rather than a bulb (if your room temps are too low, crickets do best when kept between 80-85 degrees, in my experience). Ventilation is key, as well as PLENTY of room to climb around where they can't get piled up on top of one another. My favorite thing to offer for them to crawl on is actually paper rolls, like come out of paper towels, toilet paper, and boxes of foil and plastic wrap (those are actually my favorite, they seem to prefer the tighter tubes, and it makes harvesting extremely easy). They are easy to stack in the tub, and you'll collect them faster than egg carton, unless you eat a ton of eggs.
