Having leaves to hide in will really help with them being so skittish. If they know all they have to do is move a little and still be able to watch you, but you can't see them, they'll be more comfortable. They live in bushes and stuff in nature, with lots of leaf cover, and they will come out and sit on the porch rails and stuff and watch people because they know they can hide so easily and still watch them. It's like how they say if you put lots of plants and hiding places in with dart frogs you'll see them more often. The sooner you put it in the better, or else you'll let them get comfy and then when you disturb the environment to put it in, they wont come out of hiding for a long time again (like when you first got them).
