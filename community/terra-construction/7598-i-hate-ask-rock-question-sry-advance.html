I believe quickcrete contains lime, which will lower the PH of water and other acidic things. That is why alot of people use it when making compost due to the acidity of the decomposing plants and to aid in the smell of decomposing food for those that do that part as well.

Now if your not already using it since this is an older post go ahead the extra curing time is for better strength for buildings and thicker concrete ( ft. instead of in. or less) typically in this kind of application 5 - 7 days should be enough.

certain plants do not like acidic soil they will grow much better in alkaline soil. so in that case you would want to add lime to the compost to raise the PH (I had it backwards in saying lower the PH when it actually raises it) to make sure the plant live a healthy life. depending on the plants of course.
