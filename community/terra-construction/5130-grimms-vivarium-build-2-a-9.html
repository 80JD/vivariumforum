Grimm, I feel bad that I didn't warn you to expect to have to refill the cracks... I am a BIT surprised that it shrank so much with the titebond though... Perhaps it is more the properties of the peat moss and/or coco fiber that makes it shrink than the properties of the glue? I would be willing to bet that it is because the peat moss and coco fiber swell when they absorb water, and then as the water evaporates, they shrink back down. I didn't get very much shrinkage with mine, but I did get a bit. I think it is because I added sand to the mix (did you?). I believe this helps stabilize the mix as it dries. You have to be careful not to add too much though, as it makes it much more rough (can be smoothed with sandpaper though, don't forget that!).
