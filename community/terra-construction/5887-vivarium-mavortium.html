Vivarium for A.mavortium.
Hi all.

Yesterday i started buildning on a vivarium for tigersalamanders that i have been planing a while now. Not much done for the moment because i have to wait untill my epotex aqvarium sealing arives next week, however i thought to start a thread with some pictures and short explanations what i have done so far.

Inside dimension: 120x70x55cm lxwxh. For water/drainage i have a small leaning on the bottom so water can drain down to a bucket under the vivarium through some holes. And this is what i need sealing for, to make plyfan waterprof. On top i have some kind of balcony plastic floor, as i will cover with insects net later for substrate/drainage.

I have both screwed up and glued all wood with Casco SuperSet to make it extra stable. It is a wery good glue/silicone that i have used to other constructions. For inside i shall use aquarium silicone.





Regards.
