well, i wouldn't test it with running water first, lol.

i would wet it and let it dry several times over that time period. a spray bottle, pump sprayer, etc. would be great for this purpose to make sure it cures throughout pretty good. leaving paint off for that time period would be a good idea too, imo. that should let it cure before it can't expel moisture due to it being covered.

i think a few weeks would be a good thing. before testing, i don't think is necessary... maybe a week in most cases. you'll only want to run your test long enough to know where water wants to escape... maybe 20 seconds or so. if you soak the whole piece the day before and the mortar doesn't want to dissolve you should be good enough for a quick test... after you've let it dry over night.
