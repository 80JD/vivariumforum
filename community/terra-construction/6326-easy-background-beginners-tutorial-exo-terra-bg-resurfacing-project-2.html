the way lots of people do it:

- if you dont want to see the GS from the outside, put a layer of black silicone on the glass first

- shake the foam up good and spray whatever awesome shapes you want (make sure the tank is laying down- that stuff is heavy when wet) add in pieces of wood and plants pots if desired

- beware the foam expands a lot-leave room

- let it dry and harden

- bake the coco fiber dry dry dry

- then cover with black silicone

***do this in sections at a time so the silicone doesnt skin before you put the coco fiber on****

- squish fiber/moss into the silicone- careful its a messy job lol

- let it cure for a long time- a week of twoish, depending on how much silicone you used yatta yatta


hope this helps!
