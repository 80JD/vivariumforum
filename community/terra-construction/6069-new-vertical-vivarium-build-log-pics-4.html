Mine cost $50 to build. Really, thats not bad considering I have 4 bulbs and a more expensive balast. Also, it is built to the application. It also taught me a skill that I can use in my home later if need be. For a 20 gallon, you could get away with 3 15" bulbs and a less expensive balast. so I would say maybe around $40? My setup has a 2.0 UVB, 2 5000k, and 1 10,000k bulbs. you could get away with 2 5000k and 1 10,000k bulb. I dont know if frogs need the UVB light or not, so which bulbs you use depends on the needs of the frog(s).
