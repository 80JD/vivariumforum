if i were to put flexwatt inside an enclosure i'd take lots of precaution.

early morning, before coffee example...

fill the clips with silicone and then put them over your connectors pressing the silicone in place to overflow a bit.
use your regular 3/4" black tape over the exposed ends like the instructions say. then run more all the way around the seem. then repeat with 2" black tape all the way around.
then take foil tape and tape your black tape down to the flexwatt on the top and bottom so the black tape doesn't curl up.

at that point if you wanted to waterproof the bottom with silicone or just silicone it in place go ahead.
one more piece of foil tape around the perimeter to hold it in place on the glass wouldn't be a bad idea.
then if you'd like you can waterproof the whole thing with silicone.

cobra mat is flexwatt with a little rubber and a name brand stamp that skyrockets the price tag. wiring up a flexwatt takes about a minute and since you're already wiring it there's no qualms with adding essentials in line like a controller or dimmer. my flexwatt with dimmers cost me less than a cobra mat half the size and i have full control.

with all the extra we do to our tanks to make them more comfy/better looking and the fact that most of the extras we'd use on a flexwatt we'd most likely have laying around or can be found for cheap/used on other projects... i see no reason not to.

EDIT: for example i bought a lamp dimmer cord for my heat rope under pj's enclosure. i was in a hurry to get it so i picked it up locally for $19.99 plus tax. the heat rope was (if i remember correctly) ~$35. for the price of the dimmer alone i could have installed flexwatt with a dimmer and been done with it and saved the $35 i paid for the heat rope.

pssst... btw, the reason i chose the heat rope over the heat mat in the first place is it was about half the price for the same heat/area at my lps.

also, i find the adhesive on heat mats to be of poor quality to the point of being a major fail point and a sales gimmick. not only that i feel it's a common reason why glass shatters under the heat provided by heat mats. the heat provided is neither even nor consistent after a very short while when the corners and even the middle start to peel off the glass because the heat breaks down the adhesive. also, have you tried re-using a heat mat? i have used all the tricks i know of from using dish soap and a spatula to remove it so-as not to ruin/tear off the adhesive, storing them attached to wax paper, using dish soap and warm water to rejuvenate the adhesive for re-use, etc., etc. ... all with poor results. with flexwatt you apply the adhesive... silicone (that we likely already have) and foil tape (that we should already be using so we don't crack our glass) so there's no reason to try to salvage it for re-use. rip it off when you take the mat off and re-apply when it's time to...
