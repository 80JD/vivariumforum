There are successful other ways to making quality backgrounds ( if GS or handi is not at your reach) Example of these Youtube videos besides Handi Foam or Great Stuff Pond method.

YouTube - Exo Terra "Do Your Reptiles Feel At Home" Contest 2011 - FIVE vivavriums setup for Dart Frogs
YouTube - My fake / phony/ faux rock waterfall build for my dart frog vivarium PART 2

the sculpting styrofoam method ( has to be the dense White or green kind you get a michaels hobby store ) you need a sculpting tool ( specifically to cut Styrofoam) It should have red handle ( long and thin- needle like) then its easier to carve and manipulate into looking like rock / or stone. then cover with /grout stonework & or 2 step compound cement (cure it for a couple for 6 days). Nontoxic Acrylic paints and a final sealant.


but in the end. its more expensive anyway. so I agree with Jesseb32. Handi Foam or Great Stuff pond and stone is really the best. its cheaper on Amazon than ebay. but dont go cheap ( Save your money and do it right!!!)
