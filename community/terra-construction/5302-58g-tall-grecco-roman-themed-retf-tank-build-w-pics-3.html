From a little quick reading it seems to me as though moonlight is something that many plants will actually seek to avoid (leaves folding up at night is the prime example given)- the night's rest seems to be essential to their well-being.

Not too sure what effects you'll see from your moon lights at this point- I mean, I keep lots of plants that are lit by ambient artificial light in the room well into the night, and I'm not sure to what degree this affects their growth.

I wonder if Pavel, Harry or one of the other more knowledgeable plant people might know.
