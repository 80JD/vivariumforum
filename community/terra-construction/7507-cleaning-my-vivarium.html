if your substrate is properly formed and you have good microfauna, it should last practically forever...

you should not need to clean poop...the only thing really necessary is to trim plants that are growing too much.

also what mix did you use for your substrate, and what type of viv? is it a tropical viv, temperate viv, desert viv....that all affects what type of soil mixture you should be using
