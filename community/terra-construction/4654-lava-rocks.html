yep, lava rocks should be fine. they do come packaged for different purposes. there's for gardening and for grilling and maybe more. they should be packaged according to size depending what they were intended for. i notice that the ones for grills sometimes smell almost of fossil fuels, like maybe diesel. be careful not to put any in your viv that smell in such a way.

all that aside lava rocks used to be highly regarded for fish tank and pond filtration because they're pretty much completely inert and very porous. this makes for a lot of surface area for beneficial bacteria to grow in moving water.

good point deacon... i would keep an eye or be careful not to put it somewhere they'd rummage constantly for fear of it scraping them up... it is awful rough. if you wanted to make a waterfall, background, etc. i don't see why not though.
