Could you post a pic of your current set up? It sounds like you want a pond/waterfall and 3 sides (it's a hex tank, right?) to have a wood background. If you don't have the background going into the water feature, there are several options that don't require sealing. Cork bark is the easiest and readily available at pet stores like Petco and Petsmart, but it can get pricey when covering a big tank. It takes very well to high humidity and is easy to cut to the desired size. Tree Fern Bark slabs are nice looking and hold up well for a while, but I've heard they disintegrate after a few years. Great Stuff Foaming Insulation can be applied directly to the inside of the tank and custom shaved into the desired shape as well as allowing you to add pots for plants and some branches directly into your wall/background. you would need to cover it in either grout, titebond/peatmoss, silicon/cocofiber, or spraypaint. You can also do the clay method, but you would need constant humidity in the tank. Look up some of GRIMM's posts. He shows about 3-4 different background methods. Hope this helps. Good luck!

terrarium: noun

1) a box that houses only animals (may be furnished accordingly, but nothing else live)
2) a box that houses only plants (may be furnished accordingly, but nothing else live)
3) a box/cage/etc. who's intended and practical purpose is to house plants, animals or both at some point but currently is empty... and is unable to fill completely with water due to construction of said box.

vivarium: noun

houses animals and plants

paludarium: noun

vivarium with substantial water to land ratio... commonly 50 water/50 land or better/close. one common sense tip to decide whether it's a palu or not... can fish live comfortably in it? (often times palus do have a water-bound life form)
