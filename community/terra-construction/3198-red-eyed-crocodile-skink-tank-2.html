It's easy to make a topper with just about anything. Wood, plexi (if you aren't using heat bulbs, otherwise it will warp), glass... You can even make one out of an old aquarium and just attach it on top. I'm building the one for my CWD out of wood (since my landlord wont let me build her big tank, he's afraid it will leak). If you go with glass and add enough height with it, you can make the top out of glass too and set your lights on top (with any UVB lighting you may require mounted inside the tank), with the door on the front. Imagine it as another tank turned upside down on top, with the front side able to open (you could make that piece out of lexan or something so you can drill holes to put in vents, you could use a window screen kit and make a small ventilation area at the top, or you could just install pc fans). If you have more questions or want me to sketch something out for you, just ask.

There are several species referred to as "mountain horned dragon" or "mountain horned lizard". When I was reading about them a while ago, most things I read said that they lived nearly as long or as long as a CWD, which can be from 10 to 20 years (usually topping out at 15). While captive breeding is rare amongst these species, you can probably find a captive hatched specimen, which shouldn't have parasites unless it was not properly cared for.
