Don't carve a hole for the branch, cut a slit in the GS and put the branch in. With someone else holding it (or some other way to keep the branch steady), spread the slit so that you can spray more GS inside, then let it close. The foam will expand, filling (and likely overflowing) the slit you cut, holding the branch in as well as (maybe better than) it would have if you had put it in to start with. I have several thinner branches just driven into the GS, and they hold very well (plus I can remove them occasionally for tank maintenance, though I avoid it).

When you glue rocks to the eggcrate, don't glue down the entire rock, glue it just enough to keep it in place, that way the water can flow around the glue.
