I often use the Big Gap Filler for larger projects on layers I don't need to carve into. It makes bigger bubbles and doesn't carve as well. It's the Windows and Doors (blue can) that should be avoided.

I've never heard of ghost wood spontaneously sprouting moss... Of course, I've not done any research on it either. You can get moss to sprout on pretty much any wood if spores are present (and they always are, pretty much everywhere, as they float around on air currents waiting to be deposited on the perfect spot).

Clown tree frogs are awesome. I sure hope you find some!
