You can buy a sheet of foam from home depot that is inexpensive as well, and you wont have to worry about the size restrictions imposed by the size of the cooler. You can also go to just about any store in the mall and ask for their packing foam (or wal-mart, or any other store for that matter, lol). I've brought home a few good pieces from the Macy's where I work that I'll be using for the beardie enclosure. They have to pay for their trash pickup, so if you take stuff that means they can fit more in the bin. They are usually happy to let you have stuff like that.
