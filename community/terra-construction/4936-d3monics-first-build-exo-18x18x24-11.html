Because of the amount of moss you have in the tank, I recommend either buying distilled water, collecting rainwater, or distilling your own water. The rainwater would be the best. It isn't so much about the deposits on the glass or the potential to clog the heads, but whether or not the TDS of the water will harm the moss. I never realized that regular water was such a problem for the moss, but after reading that book, I totally understand why my moss suffers so badly after a while.

There are no dissolved minerals in rain water. Moss is adapted to use rain water, not ground water (so no minerals is better than some or alot). According to what I've been reading (as well as my own experience), using anything other than rain water or distilled water can hurt the moss. I have some moss that I thought I killed long ago that I put in a dish outside. It is beginning to grow again, and I'm very happy about that.

12 might be ok. I'm certainly no expert either. I'm just sharing what I'm learning. I'll be able to explain it better tomorrow when I'm not so hopped up on pain meds for my back.
