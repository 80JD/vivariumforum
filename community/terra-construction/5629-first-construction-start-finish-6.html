Vere nice build. I'm in the planning stages and you've provided many excellent ideas and for that I thank you.

I have a suggestion to save us a few bucks.

You can get big slabs of that green foam from any cemetary for free.

All florists use it to create funerary arrangements. After the funeral, the flower arrangements are taken to the cemetary with the deceased for a final disply and after the burial, they are taken to the cemetary's trash area to await pickup.

The workers always let you take what you want because it means less for them to toss on the truck. Even if you don't need the foam now, if you're passing a cemetary, see if you can pick up a chunk or two.
