When he waters his plants...Excess water will drain down into his false bottom...The false bottom can only hold so much water until its start to overflow and go up into the substrate. So he will syphon out the water when it gets too high (using the slit he left unpainted on the right side of the tank to view the water level). Also I believe he put that white fixture in to make sure it filtered any debris that might get stuck in the syphon hose.

For the fogger I used a Kaz coolmist humidifyer. If you search on here you can find how to mod one for use as a fogger. Its super easy and costs around $40 all together to make. I just drilled a hole down and then in so In a L shape and stuck the fog line into the top so it would roll out the wall. If your not affraid to you can just drill the tank for drainage as long as its not tempered. I plan on eventually drilling a hole in the tank but at this point I dont want to risk cracking it.
