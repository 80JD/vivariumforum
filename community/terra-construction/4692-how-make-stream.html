One thing to consider when building a stream is to make your pump accessible without having to tear everything down to access it. This sound kind of cliche, but if you tear out half of your viv once or twice to fix your pump... you'll learn.

The way I built my stream (in a previous viv) was to have the flow run from back left to front right (back left was elevated). The pump lived under the substrate (false bottom) but had plenty of tubing for the stream so it could be pulled all the way to the pool I made at the front right (with an attached cable). I attached a cable to both sides of the pump so I could pull it towards the pool in the front right to access it, and then after maintenance I could pull it back to the back left so that water circulated correctly from back left (entering the pump, and going up) through the stream to the front right (the exit from the stream, which will then get sucked to the back left towards the pump).

My streambed was made from cheap clear plastic (used for painting) contoured by the substrate (aspen, with rock/gravel bottom, suspended above water bottom) and held in place by "large" rocks on each side of the plastic. Then, I placed some 1"x1" tiles in the stream bed to cover, protect, and beautify the plastic bottom.

It looked pretty good, and it worked pretty well, but my pump was a piece of crap.
