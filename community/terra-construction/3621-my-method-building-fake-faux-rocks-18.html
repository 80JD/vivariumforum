I've finally finished my terrarium!!! Here's a photo of the finished item (minus a few plants I'm still waiting on). I made this myself 100% from start to finish, including the actual terrarium, backdrop, pond (obscured by the fog), and waterfall. This is the first time I've ever done anything like this, but I think it's turned out ok, and my frogs (red-eyed treefrogs) seem to like it too! The top includes 3 canopies - one with 4 day light bulbs, and two with night lights, so we're finally able to view the frogs at night when they're active! Temperature is running around 77F, and humidity at around 70%.
