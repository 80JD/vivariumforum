well a few tips for you...


-GE II Silicone has been known to have issues with spindly leg syndrome. Many people use it, myself included, but be warned that in a fully sealed frog environment problems have been noted. Most "serious" froggers use GE 1 (way more indepth info can be found at dendroboards) This is not a major issue at all, but something to be aware of esp if you used a lot of it and plan to have PDFs.

-Mixing species is generally for the very advanced user....mixing animals is NOT like mixing fish. Tree frogs and anoles do habitate the same environment, but care must be made to provide each with appropriate areas of housing (otherwise they might all fight over the "best" spot in the tank)

-You don't need to heat your water imho. You DO need to keep the entire tank at an appropriate temperature.

-I can GUARANTEE you that keeping a river/waterfall will not suffice for humidity. I still wonder how you would plan on watering your plants with this idea anyway...?

-When using the coco / foam background method, I find it works best if the coco is BONE DRY. Any moisture prevents it from sticking in my experiences, but I also use silicone not wood glue to bond them. I have not yet tried the wood glue method.

-It is too late now, but i would have ***HIGHLY*** recommended you to use egg-crate backing and make your foam piece removable. If you ever need to gut the tank, it would make rebuilds way easier (and I find its easier to work outside your tank and then "drop in" the background after)
