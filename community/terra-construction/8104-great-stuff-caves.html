Great stuff caves
Just recently started using great stuff. Was using styrofoam sheets, but it is so muh easier to use. I hate the mess and time it takes to carve out styrofoam and stack it into layers. I used the styrofoam to make the basic shapes of my caves and used the great stuff to fill out everything else. I'll grout it and paint when its dry. Post a pic when it's finished.
