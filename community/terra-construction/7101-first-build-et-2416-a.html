I'm thinking the little guy will be quite happy, as well.

As for the rock panels, I actually just used black grout. So far, I have 4 layers on the styrofoam. Now that I have them mounted, I need to trim back the GS and I will add maybe two more black layers on top of the GS, up to the wood, and then I will add a few coats of a light brown grout. Not sure if I should add any sand to the final layer before I seal it up.... I read (somewhere) that I can take a wire-brush to the final layer and scour the brown layer off to expose the black surface underneath. This seemed like a reasonable idea, so that is the way I am going with it. Now I am trying to figure out what I am going to do on the left-side. I am thinking about making some caves and I will probably run a couple of red LED's to add a little bit of illumination.
