If you use a green bulb, it shouldn't disturb your leo as long as it is dim, and on all night. If you are turning it on and off, it's going to bother him (obviously you know that already). I'd try a green "party" bulb from wally-world or somewhere similar and see how it affects him. Those bulbs are only 25W, so they are fairly dim. OR, you could use some green LEDs (this would probably be even better).

crete transfers heat quite well. it just takes some time that's all. the crete itself has to get to temp first. it's always best to check your temps for a few days when making changes or setting something up anyway.
try citrus s.o.s. pads... they worked well for my 50 gal and i'd wager you'd be shocked how bad it was when i started.
i would never pay $100 for a thermostat unless my viv was too large to purchase or it were to run quite a bit of tanks. i'm using the repti-therm 500 and feel it's sufficient if not perfect for my application.
you can get a dimmer... either one you'd wire in yourself or a lamp dimmer cord. the dimmer cords i've been able to find for ~$7 online. when i got my last one i was in a hurry so i got it locally at a lighting shop for ~$20. if you have any wiring knowledge you can get a dimmer for only a couple bucks too.
a cheap dimmer and some flexwatt should run you less than a heat pad you'd find at a pet store and be fully controllable.
