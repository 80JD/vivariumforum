Made a fair amount of progress. The tank finished cycling and I added a few plants, some snails, a red claw crab, a small clown pleco, chinese algae eaters and some small schooling fish. The clown found a nook in the stump and hides there all the time, I've only seen him twice since I got him.
I finally got my fans installed and a cut a plexiglass cover for the tank. The fans I got have blue leds in them (thanks radio shack!), so I hope it will give just enough light for a bit of night viewing. I havent tried it yet...


I'm gonna go to the nursery and collect mosses and liverworts for the background tomorrow. The weather forcast says thunder storms tonight, so I think Im gonna build a makeshift rain collector. I dont want any of our heavy water getting on the mosses and killing them. I had the experience that putting liverworts on fertile soil will eventually kill it, so hopefully the lack of substrate in most of the background will help them live longer. Same with the mosses.
