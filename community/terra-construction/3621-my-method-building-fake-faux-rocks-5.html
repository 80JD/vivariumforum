Well, I have another thread on here describing my Habacrete nightmare, which is too bad, because I really like the stuff. It's the source that causes the problem. It took a long time, and a lot of arguing, then the guy was threatening me. The product is really cool, similar to the regular quickset. I think you can sculpt this a little more if its thick. I also like the tan color. You'll notice my other vive has the grey rock appearance, which is cool too. You'll love the Habacrete more because you do not have to soak it in vinegar and water for weeks! Which can be a pain if your working on a larger scale like this now. It is pricy, but worth the extra cost in the end.
