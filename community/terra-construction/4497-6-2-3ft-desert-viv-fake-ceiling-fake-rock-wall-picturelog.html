6*2*3ft desert viv with fake ceiling and fake rock wall: picturelog
What better way of introducing myself than with a complete build log of my first vivarium?

This build is for a Bearded dragon, Daku, who's been living it it for a few months now.
The tank itself is 140*50*75cm (equals about 55" x 19.5" x 29.5"), built out of waterproof plywood, 6mm glass doors and a fake ceiling.

Total cost for this project was around €800 (950USD), but that includes all electronics and tools (saws, drillbits, a dremel multi tool, ...)


The thermostats I got for free from a guy I know who has tons of these at his company (which he owns)



So off to a start: the wood arrived


first side attached


...pre drilled...


2nd side attached


and the back wall in


part 2

Went to work with styrofoam, expanding foam and slate rocks


stationary cave with removable top:


overview with 2 tubes which will be used to pass the thermostat probes into the tank



layout:
- left side is cave
- middle will be filled with slate tiles
- right side is a sandbox
- platform will be plain grout



a few close ups




part 3: finishing touches and grouting





















started the fake ceiling



since the entire UVB attachment was to tall to my likings (10cm), i cut off the feet and attached them to the underside with the light, and put the rest on top of the fake ceiling, out of sight. This reduced the height of the light to 4cm, removing it from sight.






fake ceiling includes:
- 3x 50W halogen spot on dimmer, timer and thermostat
- 1x 60W ceramic heater on thermostat
- 1x 120cm reptisun 10.0 UVB TL on timer and on/off switch
- 3x moonlight led (set by lucky reptile) on timer
- lots of cables (they have been organised by now)




Then I added the 2 front panels (top and bottom) with ventilation in the bottom one, and added the real ceiling which hase the same ventilation:


The colour in that image was a try out, which failed miserably (got pretty gay pink instead of dark reddish terracotta...)

so back to grey it was:


After this I covered the entire thing in yacht varnish. this not only made the entire thing glossy (it said matte finish!!!) but it also reacted with the black colourant in the grout, giving it a strange look. luckely enough I kinda like it



I also put in a real plant, a basking log, 25lbs of white playsand, the removable cover for his cave and his feeding dish.





switches & thermostats (upper one controls daytime halogen spots, lower one controls backup temperature with 60W heater)





one of the thermostat probes coming out of the tubes built into the back wall:


nighttime lighting:



the finished product



inhabitant the first day: (20th September 2009)


same inhabitant 2 months later: (19th November 2009)


he's gone from 25.5cm and 43 grams on 12th Sept 2009 to 43.5cm and 255 grams on 19th November 2009!!
