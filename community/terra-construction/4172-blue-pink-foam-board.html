or you can start at the beginning... i referred to that thread in another thread in which if you read from here: Back ground grout question !! and click on the links as you go you should get a much better understanding. one thing i will say is that if you plan on getting it wet and are unsure of how to work with concrete do not attempt regular mortar. i personally know it will be ok with water for a long time. the thing is it needs reinforcement and or a pretty thick coating to hold up to water for any length of time. that makes your details wash out or your viv pretty heavy by the time it's sufficient to deal with constant submersion. if you were to do a large closet or a small room then for the price it would more than be worth figuring it out. otherwise it might be a problem for someone not familiar with it.
