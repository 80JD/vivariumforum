K now that I'm home here goes....

The more water you have in a viv, the easier it is to maintain. This is because less water changes are needed since there is just more volume to become "dirty". The water will take more time to become stained with substrate/wood tannins. More water is gooder! Therefore no need to section off the false bottom.

As for the false bottom leca question. I havent built one just yet, but I have my next falsebottom viv all planned out in my head so I can at least explain to you what I would do in your situation....1st, under your large flat eggcrate piece, you do not need any leca. I would have 2 pieces of eggcrate slopped down inside the top cutout, then add egdes around the perimeter of the large top piece. Then wrap the created eggcrate "box" in some sort of window screen or mesh. The screen will prevent your leca balls, and substrate from entering the water section and clogging your pump.

So now that you have created this "mesh covered box" you are left with about a 1" gap in between your eggcrate box and glass. Now all you have to do is add the leca balls up to the top of the eggcrate box, and then add substrate ontop of everything else. All the eggcrate will be hidden by the leca so it will give you a clean, tidy, and natural look.
