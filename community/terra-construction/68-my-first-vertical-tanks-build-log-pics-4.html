Questions are what this place is for. If I didn't want to answer them, I would come here
I used various methods for securing the plants to the foam. I used string and needle to basically sew the plant up against the background. I would sew the string into the foam and tighten it so the plant was held against it. I also used staples and paperclips to pin the plant against the foam. I never punctured the plants ofcourse, only puncture the foam.
I also much prefer something called Jungle Mix. Its a mix of bark chips, sphag, and soil. Very good stuff. I found it at my local petshop.

Hello,

The top of the viv is a glass top, but it is not removable. Only the front opening can be used as an opening.
I keep amphibians and UV light is not necessary for these if you feed them with Calcium + D3 and multivitamin supplements, which you can find in powdered forms online.
As for heating, the lights are hot enough to bring daily temps up into the upper 70's and lower to mid 80's depending on the season. If you find your room is too cold, then I would go for a heater, a problem I have not encountered. If I had reptiles, this would be a different story.
