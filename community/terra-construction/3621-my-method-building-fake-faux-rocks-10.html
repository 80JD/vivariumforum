since you've already discovered "shocking news", rofl, i see nothing wrong with taking precautions and using a grounding rod. i would however try to eliminate one by one the source of the current leak.

you know as well as i do (i',m almost 100% positive) that those cords can be the cause of such a situation. i would not put it past and might personally suspect my electrical box (fuse box/junction box/etc.) if you cannot isolate a specific piece of equipment or eliminate the issue by reducing your wire clutter.

i would take each piece of equipment that comes into contact with anything that could carry current into your viv water and test it in a bucket full of water. i'd do it with and without the cords and in another room on another breaker if necessary also.

if you find that you only have the issue with your extension cords plugged in replace them ofcourse. if you find it's a specific piece of equipment (many heaters are notorious for leaking current eventually ofcourse) then obviously replace that. if you find you can only replicate the problem in that room then it may be time to rewire, add or replace a faulty breaker or move your setup to another breaker/room.

i am sorry if any of this seems obvious to you. i'm hoping some of this sparks a thought and may help you in the long run.
