18x18x24 Exo Terra Build Log
I decided its time to customize my 18x18x24 Exo Terra, I hate the cookie cutter look. Ive searched all over, and picked different things as i went. I am building a waterfall background, and a false bottom to make the tank about 50/50 land and water. I will update this thread as I go. No pictures of the false bottom yet as I am redoing it to better suit my needs. Ill also update this thread later to include the materials and tools used.

Background: Not finished, just carved and treated with 1 coat of Permastone.



I did pick up a 10g tank to house my anole while I renovate his tank.



More pics and info to come.
