someplace always soaked... java moss
under constant humidity and moist soil... riccia
right on what you have... 2 unidentified mosses i collected locally. you'd have to set the lumps in there with their soil and let them spread for a while but they're taking well to my setup so far. it's just cocofiber and croutons on the bottom capped with sphagnum moss. i do have a mister and fan in there and keep it at 100% humidity, but that moss dries out in the guys yard every week and comes back...

i do think a little sand in there mixed in might help establish a ground cover but i don't think it's necessary.

unfortunately i have very little of what i do have as of yet. what i got locally was from the tip of a very well manicured elderly neighbors yard. i'm not taking more till that spot clears back in because it's already noticeable.

i have not tried tillandsias to comment on them yet, but they look good on ebay though.
