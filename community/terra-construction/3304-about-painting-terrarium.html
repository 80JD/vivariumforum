With spray paint, you need to check and see if it is rated for plastics and if it will paint on foam. Alot of paint contains chemicals that will melt foam. All of those warning signs are for the product in the state you buy it in (liquid), they don't tell you anything about whether it is toxic once cured. I know that Krylon Fusion is supposed to be safe, alot of people have been using it.

Acrylic paints are non-toxic, even in their liquid form. I don't think I've ever seen a bottle that didn't say non-toxic (although I could be wrong, and you should certainly check the package). The problem with acrylic paints is that they are generally water soluble. You'll have to seal it with something that is non-toxic if you don't want it to come off ( which it will do a bit at a time when your geckos lick it).
