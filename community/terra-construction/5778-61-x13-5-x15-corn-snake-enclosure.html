61"x13.5"x15" Corn Snake Enclosure
Ok, so I've had this glass for a LONG time, and now I'm finally doing something with it!

It used to be an old curio case, I got it for free off of craigslist. It came with the glass I'm using (which is tempered), and three glass shelves (which are not). the top and bottom (which will be the sides) are made of what I thought was pressboard, but after surviving months in the rain with no ill effect, I have no idea what they are made of, lol... Suffice it to say I think I would be comfortable using whatever it is to build an aquarium! lol...

I picked up some epoxy to glue the glass into the channels cut into the wood pieces so I don't have to figure out some way to bolt it together (lost the hardware for the original pieces that held it together), and I'm going to put it together today and perhaps begin foaming for the BG and hides.

This will be for my corn snake, as the title suggests. It's a palace for him, and I can't wait to get him into it. He loves slithering around his 20G long, and I don't think it's anywhere near big enough for him. This, on the other hand, will be the pinnacle of perfection for him to slither his little coldblooded heart out in.

I've already taken a few pics, mostly of the glass and how it goes together. I'll get them posted this evening when I update the thread with all I've gotten done on it today. I'm supposed to be doing chores, so I'll have to sprinkle in a few throughout the day, but I'm confident I can get quite a bit done.

Stay tuned for updates!

Because I despise silicone? lol... The cure time is a big part of it. It's not as much of an issue in this build, but I did like th ease of the epoxy, even though it smells like ass... lol

I went with the epoxy to attach the wood to the glass because I didn't have room in the track for glass and silicone. The epoxy was really thin, so all I had to do was put some in there and then put the glass on top of it. The quick set up was good too, I needed to be able to hold it in place myself because I didn't have any way to clamp it or strap it.

It really sucks that I forgot about the braces though, I had enough epoxy to do them as well, I just forgot about them so I used it in the track instead.

You know, I probably should have thought of this before, but I don't know what I'm going to do for ventilation... I guess I could cut vents in the ends (don't really want to do that), or install a fan...

Anyway, here's a new pic of the day's progress. I have to wait a while before I spray anymore foam. So I might not work on it anymore today.
