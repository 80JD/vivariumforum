if you want the heat not to be an issue i'd stay away from power compacts. ho bulbs can get hot too though.

what's wrong with the nova extreme fixture? i'd do it if i were you... i've been trying to talk the wife into letting me have the 36" nova extreme for a week now. you can get bulbs from one of the places we've already listed in the 6700k spectrum if that's really what you want. i know they're odd size bulbs but your tank pretty much is an odd size for bulbs in general. you'll get better light coverage and more planting options with the longer bulbs so i think fitting the bulbs as close to the size of your tank would be your best bet.
