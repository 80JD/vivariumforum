yep... can't see jack through it at all. pretty sure i got the tint at wally world too. if not it was autozone. either way you can find it anywhere that has an automotive section, really. not too expensive either. you can do a bunch of 10 or 20 gal. tanks for ~$11-$15. no need to fuss with taping off or cleaning up paint either... that's a plus. just gotta keep a good amount of soapy solution on there till all the bubbles are out and you're good to go.

just need a razor trimmer (breakaways, exacto, just a blade, etc.), a spray bottle and a credit card or squeegee. make the soapy solution according to the directions, put them in your sprayer... size up and pre-cut a piece a bit bigger than you need... cut a slit in the corner and peel back the tint from the clear plastic sheathing making sure it doesn't fold and it stays wet... spray down your tint and glass with soapy solution till they're dripping... slap it on and smooth it out longways from middle out... anything that starts drying before the bubbles are out pull back and re-spray... once it's on trim as necessary with your blade.

it's easy as pie once you're doing it right. when i was younger tinting was one of those mystical "how does that work" things. once you're keeping it wet enough you won't believe how easy it actually is. that tank took me all of maybe 10 minutes and it was filling up with water... and i'm no master tinting machine either.

you don't need high tint either. that was cheap stuff. it turned completely purple in the berreta in less than a year. since it's not exposed to uv on the tank it's been on there over 3 years and not a hint of turning yet.
