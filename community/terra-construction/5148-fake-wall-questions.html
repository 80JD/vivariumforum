I wouldn't agree entirely with boabab's method (I went from thick to thin, layer-wise), but tile grout is cheap as anything (you won't need much, as a little goes a long way), and plain cement may be even cheaper. I used several concrete tints added in different ratios for the different layers, and got a good effect that way- the tints aren't expensive either. I mixed in an acrylic copolymer for added strength and water resistance- I don't know if it helped or not because it's my first one. The whole thing cured really well, and it takes a lot to crack it, even though the whole thing's not much more than 1/16" thick. I've never kept crabs, but I doubt they'd be able to make a dent in it.

I basically went this route (DB member HX's): http://www.dendroboard.com/forum/par...e-rocks-3.html

any of those products will crack if not applied with the right precautions. grout is made to fill in tight spaces, crete is made to be poured thick, levelers and such are made to adhere to crete itself so we start at a disadvantage. prevention is pretty straight forward though. avoid extreme heat and low humidity for the first couple days and you should be good, if not add a little spritz with a spray bottle here and there, etc..

adding a binder such as flevopol, acrylic paint, etc. should help to avoid such an occurence also.
