The toads won't really mind brighter light, IME (I miss having some SO much, but all the pet stores around here are carrying European FBTs and I just don't like the look of them much). What kind of fixture do you currently have? Do you want to go with spiral CFLs or fluorescent tubes? Either way, 6500 or 6700 (better for my plants, so get those if you can find them!) work very well, and the color of the light is nice to look at.

Does the tank have a hood? If so, you can get dual socket s and install them into the hood and use CFLs.
