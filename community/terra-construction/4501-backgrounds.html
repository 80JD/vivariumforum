My tank is 6ft tall The back is just as you described, I never used a drop of silicone on it i believe less chemicals in the tank is better. There is a trick to not using silicone though. First get a piece of styrofoam then carve some shape in it use spray foam to attach pieces of wood and pots. let dry. now get coir ready. spray foam small areas at a time, then here is the trick, do not let the foam swell as you spray it on rub it around and get the air bubbles out of it. It will thicken and turn a darker yellow(use gloves) now press the dirt on firmly, it will stick way better and form a hard shell. If you try to put dirt on after it swell it will not stick good and you will see foam through the coir when it dries. use way more coir than you need. as it dries you need to keep pressing the little swells of foam down that try to pop up. I garentee this method works better than silicone as long as you DON'T let the foam spray expand. Mine stands up to a cwd clawing all over it and part of it is completly under water If you did it right the foam spray will be as hard as rock, and the less air you have in it the more water proof it is.
