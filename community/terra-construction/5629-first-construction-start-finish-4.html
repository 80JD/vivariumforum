I'm Back
Hey Everyone I am sorry that it has been so long since my last post. I had to go back to school and was unable to bring the tank with me. However, I am now back at home again for the summer and have settled back into a summer internship. As such its back to work on the tank.

In my last post almost a year ago I had just received the Habicrete and since then I have applied several layers to the foam board and it is starting to look pretty good.

How I did it:
1. The habicrete calls for a mixture ratio of 3 (Habicrete) to 1 (water) however, I have found that adding more water makes the job much easier. The key is to keep mixing very frequently and adding a splash of water every three or so minutes to keep the mixture runny.
2. I used two paint brushes one large one and one small one. The large one was for covering big area and the small one for the cracks. I also had a pick tool which I used to clean up the cracks when they were filled in.
3. A third dry paint brush was used to brush the concrete once it had dried to clean up bumps and help smooth out the texture.
4. To help smooth out the cracks I used the small brush and a very watery mix of habicrete.
5. It will take several coats to cover all the foam completely.

More Concrete More Pictures
Today I did some more touching up of the concrete trying to make sure all the cracks and holes are covered. I have found that it is almost impossible to get the concrete into the fine cracks that I created in the foam. As such I have had to give up on many of the fine details, however, I am still pleased with how it looks.

The Short of It:
1. Don't carve deep thin cracks you won't be able to concrete them, instead have shallow gradual cracks.
2. Don't be a perfectionist, at some point you will have to stop concreting and then scratching it off because it wasn't perfect and move on. Don't worry it will still look good even if it isn't perfect.
