Hey man, awesome idea! Have you thought of a acrylic enclosure instead of wood? Then here would be no worries on rotting, and possible leaks maybe. In my opinion, the more water, the better water quality you will have with less problems. Remember I am a reefer, and the more water the better.. my reef system is 450g... I am a novice when it comes to herps, but we're talking water now. It deosn't take much water depth if you have a small pump. My pump was overkill, and is turned down quite a bit, cause I get some splashing that eventually makes a lot of ground pretty wet in spots,,which in turn will start killing some plants. I had to add more soil, and drop the water level about a half an inch, cause it was effecting my bonsia tree's health.
