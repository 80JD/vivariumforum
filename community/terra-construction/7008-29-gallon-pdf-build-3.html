Looks pretty good. Nice selection of plants. You may need to get supplemental lighting if you want to keep the brom and earth stars colour. Also those 2 neo brom above the earth stars will need to be moved if they are planted in the substrate. They will rot if the base stays moist. Since you bought some springtails, I would suggest putting a bunch of leaf litter in the back section. Just dig out a little substrate, and fill it up with leaves. I regret not adding a lot more leaf litter. My leaves are only 2 layers thick and my springtails need to be replenished on a regular basis.

Josh's frogs sent me a culture that had 2 live springtails in it and resent me one with 10 springtails. I got a culture from Darren @ the Portland reptile expo and there were more springtails than substrate! I poured all of that culture into the 29 and it had 5 days to seed into it before I put the frogs in it. I took out some of the soil from the 29 and put it in the gecko tank and there are wood beetles and springtails in it. I also took some of the soil from the 29 and mixed it with the cultures from Josh's frogs in a small rubbermaid to keep a side culture going just in case. Both tanks and the rubbermaid are crawling with springtails. Cocofibre works so much better than charcoal.
