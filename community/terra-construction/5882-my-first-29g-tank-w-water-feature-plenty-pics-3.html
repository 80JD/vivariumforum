for the g/s removal... vac and if you have it blow it out with a compressor. at that point a rinse should be sufficient. you'll be covering most of it and hopefully giving the tank a chance to settle, plants root, etc. anyway. plus, if it's cured it should be pretty safe.

colored sand usually is a bad idea. there are sands that aquarium keepers use pretty often that would provide a little color... although i'm not sure how many options you'd have for colors. black, tan and white are the most prominent in the hobby.

i've yet to see an acrylic paint that doesn't state it's non toxic right on the front label, so you should be good there.

the buckets certainly seem like a good idea since leca floats but i'd think some of the material you're trying to get rid of would also. the holes should help a lot.

i was wondering how you're going to hide that slope... i'm guessing your slate? if i may make a suggestion... stacked (glued) pieces would look a lot better than a vertical piece imo.

carving g/s is usually a better option than sanding. sanding it makes a nasty mess.
