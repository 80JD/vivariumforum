uva comes from a good full spectrum bulb
uvb is the uv herps may need and the one sold as uv bulbs
uvc is sold as a uv sterilizer and kills, blinds... whatever it shines on eventually

to get uvb inside and still keep humidity up you can build a topper to keep in humidity or you can close it in and put the uvb inside... but use caution where you put it... too far is useless... too close can be deadly

and yes everyone who needs higher humidity does seal their tank better... it's how you go about it.... if you need 80-90% and you got 30%... you have to seal your air exchange... there are other things you can do too but none will give you consistency.
