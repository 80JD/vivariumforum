Hello all. Im mike, and I am fairly new to this hobby. This thread has inspired my girlfriend and I to build a pdf viv. I did a lot of research and found some great information. I recently went to Guatemala and bought a myan mask. Since I realized that a lot of pdf's come from central and south america, I decided to incorporate a similar mask. I'm no artist by any means, but I am really happy with the way things turned out. The only thing left to do is caulk in the back ground. O... there is also 2 water lines that come from the water pump to feed 3 waterfalls on the background. 2 waterfalls on the right and one on the left. so yes there is a false bottem with egg crate. Instead of a foam hot knife I decided to use a dremel tool with a router bit. That works great on the foam but makes a HUGE mess...lol.(plus its not a fire hazard) The shop vac quickly became a good friend. Cant thank you all enough on all the endless knowledge. I would love to return the favor, please feel free to ask questions. Now I just wish I had another tank so I can do it again. I will post more pics soon.
