Is the moss exo-terra moss? If so, I personally will NOT use it. I have a block of it and I used some to incubate my J. splendida eggs when she layed a few hours after bringing her home and it was all I had. It hatched out some NASTY little critters. Mites, worms, earwigs. Some of the stuff ate into my eggs and killed them. I've never used any since. It molds really easy, and is in general just nasty. The go out in the woods and gather random moss and press it into the blocks without sterilizing it and market it as "bioactive". The problem is that they don't know what actually might be in it. The mites alone can be harmful, depending on what happens to be in it.

Regardless, if it smells bad, don't use it. If you don't want to waste it, use it in gardening outside. If you've still got the receipt and it's not been long since you bought it, return it. Tell them it stinks when you expand it and you don't want to use it.
