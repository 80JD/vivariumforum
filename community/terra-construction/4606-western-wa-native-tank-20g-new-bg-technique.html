Thank you! I'm QUITE proud of it. This is my best work. I thought the ten gallon was good, but this one looks like a piece of our yard.

The part of the background that I took from the ten gallon has moss sewn to open cell foam, but that doesn't count for the prize, lol... I guess I should elaborate which part of the BG is made with the new technique. It's the part that is on the back of the tank. The part on the side is the BG from the ten gallon, cut in half and stacked on top of each other.

Yes, that is a real mushroom. I cut it from a tree stump just in front of our house. None of the others had that perfect shape, but there are quite a few more I could cut off, if anyone wants one (just PM me if you're interested). Unfortunately, it must be quite tasty for the mold bloom currently running rampant in the tank. The small branch that comes forward to the glass is covered in white mold, as is the tree mushroom.

EVERYTHING in this tank is native, other than the non-living supplies I used to put it all together. Most of that was collected the day before I put it together, though I did go find a bit more when I didn't have enough moss for the entire back. I still didn't cover the entire back, I left part in the middle down near the substrate open. The back of that tank is painted black, so it adds depth to the tank and makes it look like there's a cave in the back.

You jackass... Humph... You don't get the points... Well... I'll think about it, lol.

I was thinking I had actually said how I did it, but I was hoping I hadn't... Oh well. It was still fun while it lasted. :P

It worked amazingly well. I didn't press the moss into it, I just sprayed the GS underneath, so it expanded up into the bottom layers of moss. As the GS expanded and I wanted to limit how far it came out into the tank, I pressed and molded it gently. I've done that before with GS after it skinned to keep from having to break the skin by carving.

This isn't something I've seen done, so as far as I go, I made it up. Has anyone else seen this done before? I'm just curious. I bet you could do this in alot of ways... You might even be able to spray GS underneath sand or peat/coco fiber and see how that works. Of course, you'd have alot less control over shaping the foam if you did that... Hmmmmm... I might have to experiment.
