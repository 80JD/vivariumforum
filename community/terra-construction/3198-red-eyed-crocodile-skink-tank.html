Red Eyed Crocodile Skink Tank
So, I began construction on my 34g aquarium finally.

I had my girlfriend's mom cut the glass pieces for me that will enclose a small pond area and filter area. From the picture of Aquarium specs, it may be hard to see, but I basically just have too rectangle enclosures connected by a small canal. My main goal with this aquarium is to make it easy to clean, so I didn't want to do a free flowing false bottom (of water).

The aquarium is 36L x 14W x 16H for a Red Eyed Crocodile Skink. Unfortunately, her mate died soon after I purchased both of them, but they have a 75% mortality rate from all the research I have done (I do not reccommend these animals to anyone).

I already managed to silicone the glass pieces together, as shown in the pictures, with GE 1 Silicone w/o Mold Inhibitor. I was sort of messy with one of the pieces, but it's my first time. When my dad gets home, he will be helping me silicone the glass to the actually aquarium.

While that is drying, I will be using a lazer saw to cut eggcrate to cover the area above the glass areas, supported by PVC pipes. All the glass is 3 inches in height, so the PVC support will be 3 inches tall. I will be using a 7.5 inch tall 10i filter, that will be surrounded by slate rock of some sort to create a rock barrier from the filter water pool. Yes, I am still debating on how I'm going to create that area, but tile broken into pieces seems like the best bet.

The eggcrate will be covered by fiberglass Window screen, and there will be Eco Earth substrate and moss above that. I'm going to be using all artifical plants, because I'm lazy, but I may end up buying some small ferns.


And I'm going to reseal the sides of the aquarium that will be present in water, because I'm pretty sure this aquarium was not made to hold water.

Any suggestions for later?
