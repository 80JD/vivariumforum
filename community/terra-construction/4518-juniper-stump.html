if it's sappy or scented strongly that's natures defenses at work. if it's not either then generally it's fine. pine and cedar are known to be the biggest culprits. we use cedar mulch in our gardens because it's a great deterrent for insects.

i collect a lot of my wood from local watering holes. creeks, streams, swamps and rivers are a great place to go collecting. cork bark, mopani, driftwood, manzanita, ghost wood, cypress and tree fern wood are all acceptable for a short list.
