Quote:
Originally Posted by Dinosaurz
Great work! I have a 20 gal long and have three questions...
1. What kind of pump/filter did you use?
2. How is the pump/filter set up to take water out and bring it back to the falls? (is there a small hole in the creek?)
3. The end of the creek, you can see right into it, so you can see fish if you'd put them in. How'd you secure it to the glass so water doesn't leech out?
1-I used a pump that is bigger then the tiny waterfall pumps, and it is overkill... on the new tank, we are going smaller for sure. My pump is turned down on the tall tank, I cant remember exactly how big it is right now. For a waterfall, you don't need much. A big pump will cause splashing all over your vive- which may seem cool at first, but it will eventually soak everything.

2/3- On the front of the glass area, I did use some habacrete to seal the creek to the glass, but, only on the top half... the bottom half is rocks..the same rocks that are covering the front of the false bottom egg crate. This way it does drain out the front still. Look back in the very fist build to see how I do make drains using a drinking straw, it really helps and your creek will not over flow either...
