58G tall Grecco-Roman themed RETF tank build (w/pics)
Hey guys! So since the weather got nicer I started my 58g viv build. I have a few pictures for now and will continue to put more up as the build progresses. I am a Classical Civilizations minor, especially Greek philosophy so I am theming my tank to look like an ancient Greek city-state taken over by the jungle. I got the tank from Glasscages.com (thanks again guys!) and it wasn't made waterproof so first, i re-siliconed the sides using black DAP. I did 2 coats of this and it looks like this: . i extended up the sides so u cant see the FB. I also made an aquaduct/ wall to hide the FB in the front. it looks like this:.U can see in this pic how i did the FB with 3in PVC pipes and zip ties. I drilled 2 holes into the pvc, put them thru the FB and tied them tight. I did this after i covered the FB in window screen, to keep the hydroton from falling thru. I'll show a better pic of it tonight. I plan on covering the collums with tile grout. I recently put in some malaysian driftwood into the background with GS, i'll post those pics tonight. DONT UNDER ESTIMATE the expansion power of GS! they weren't kidding when they said it expands 5x! Its a slow start but you know what they say: Rome wasnt build in a day!

day 2
So I GS'ed the wood into the background and made the "column wall" to hide the FB from the front. Sorry about the lighting im working on my back patio at night (after work). Here's the background: . see the little greek decor? i hollowed that out and am going to run some wires through it. I also hollowed out some of the background near the wood to put Neoreglias in. I GS'ed tubing through the bends in the wood for drainage, since they are prone to root rot in wet conditions. In retrospect i should have included some PVC pipes up the sides to run the wires through, but i will come up with something creative to hide them with. Here's a better view of my FB . I covered it with window screen mesh.
