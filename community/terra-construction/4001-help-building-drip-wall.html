More info on what the "everything" you have to build the wall is would be helpful, and you overall design idea. But, I've done this in tanks with a typical GS/silicone/coco fiber wall with tubing running up behind the background from a pump in the hydroton layer below the substrate. Options are nearly endless so its all up to you on how exactly you do it.

Looks very cool. I love your water feature! I can't tell where your land area is going to be though? Are you going to cover all the eggcrate with substrate? If so, I'd be careful about the water dripping from the wall into the substrate. I'm re-doing a couple of tanks right now where I had some water overflowing into the substrate. Lets just say that too often I ended up with mud and a clogged screen layer. Tooo damp of substrate = some unhappy plants and can lead to rotting soil. I'm keeping my original design intact, just adding a much needed barruer of cork bark to keep the water flowing against the wall instead of through the substrate. Good luck, it looks AWESOME so far!
