Make sure you let the silicone cure before putting water in the tank. Hopefully the DAP won't be a problem fr you. There have been alot of reports that it doesn't cure properly... That's why we always recommend the GE type I or II silicone. As long as you let it cure fully before adding water, it should be ok though.

The mesh for the pond doesn't HAVE to go all the way to the glass. If you have it go down so that there is enough of it to anchor it down with gravel, that will be enough. That way you can leave that part unpainted so you can see into the water from the outside.

For the pump box, I think your idea sounds good. Let me be sure I'm getting it right. The box will be a cube that fits into the open spot and is easily removable to maintain the pump, right? I think that's a great idea. That way you can put some filtering material inside the box to filter before it gets to the pump so the pump doesn't clog. It will be easy to change the media because the entire box comes out.
