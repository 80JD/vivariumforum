If you want to plant it in a substrate, plant it in some of the coco husk mixed with some sand. It needs something really well draining if planted terrestrially. If you want to mount it, find the stolon if there is one left and mount it to the BG with a paper clip or something similar. I mount all of the one I buy that are still growing on stolons from the mother plant and generally pot the mother plant because it was already potted and has alot of roots.
