Now I have to move to the other side of the tree! This will be the center section of the background. I'm going to end up making a small crack/waterfall through the center of this later.

I do the same thing for this side. Hold up the wood, and try to outline the edge as best as I can. None of this will come out perfect, so just take your time and do the best that you can. It can all take several steps with fitting and trimming.

This side has a pretty big gap that you'll see where my hand is. so I need to make the back thicker in that area. The more time you take to fit the foam, the less concrete you will waste later. Just take the time to stare at it and measure over and over, thats all I am doing.

Now comes the fun of the last piece of the puzzle. The right side. By the way, this has taken two or three hours to get this far... it takes time!

Now this piece is curved a lot more, and I will need to use chunks of foam to fill in some rock. (don't throw away your scraps of styro when you start cutting! save them)

Looking at the side profile of this bark, you'll see another big gap. After the usual outlining, I took the Sharpie marker and outlined the bark on the glass-on the BACK of the tank. I then held up the last piece of foam in place on the back side of the tank.... then started freehand drawing the last outline on the foam lightly.... each time holding it on the glass and looking at the initial outline I drew on the glass... we just want to be close as possible.
