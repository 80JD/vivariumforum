This is my Ambystoma opacum, Edited 7/16/06
This is the only pic I have of my marbled salamander. I only have one, and its very very reclusive. It hides in its exoterra cave, and I only see it rear its head just to the edge of light and that when I know its time to feed it an earthworm. It goes after the earthworms like crazy. Becuase of these traits, hiw name is Gollum. I was pulling on the worm to lure/pull him out of the cave to get a good pic.
