Substrates:

Mixture 1
-2 cups eco earth
-1 cup peat moss
-1/2 cup charcoal
-1/2 cup orchid bark
-1/8 cup clay
-Can also add shredded and long fiber sphagnum moss, sterilized sand, hydroton clay balls.

Mixture 2: High Draining (posted by Boabab95)
2 part peat
1 part orchid bark
2 parts sphagnum moss
1 part charcoal
1 part sand
2 parts chopped leaves


Matt Mirabello’s soil recipe: Panamanian soil analysis
kaolinite (EPK) 1100g 56%
Bentonite clay (absorbent clay kitty litter) 550g 28%
Fe Oxide red 70g 4%
Fe Oxide Yellow 70g 4%
Al oxide 60g 3% (not needed as it may be harmful for inhabitants)
sugar 15g 1%
cornstarch 15g 1%
gelatin 10g 1%
soy protein 10g 1%
CaCO3 20g 1%

Great soil discussion threads- http://www.dendroboard.com/forum/par...-analysis.html
http://www.dendroboard.com/forum/gen...te-thread.html

Substrate Micro Fauna/Dart Diet:

-Fruit flies (melanogaster/funebris)
-Tropical Springtails
-“Blue” Springtails
-Woodlice (Spanish Giants, White Dwarfs) (good source of calcium in shell)
-Bean Beetles/Weevils (Cultures have very low smell)

Culturing springtails - Springtails and http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iw8n2x7COL8
Culturing Woodlice - Woodlice
Culturing Fruit flies - Drosophila
Culturing Bean Beetles (plus everything else you would want to know) - http://www.beanbeetles.org/handbook/
Adding fresh mushrooms and flaked fish food under leaf littler will act as an additional food source for micro fauna


Leaf Litter:

-Northern Magnolia (fast decomposition, and good for springtail populations)
-Southern Magnolia (slow decomposition, good for top asthetic layer)
-Oak
-Photinia
-Rhododendron
-Maple (fast decomposition, good for maintaining isopod populations)

Preparation methods include: Rinsing, boiling, microwave for 2-3 minutes in between wet paper towel, and bleach solutions.

Wow sounding really good Grimm!! And thanks for the links!!

I use a similar soil recipe...
-2 cups eco earth
-2 cup peat moss
-1 cup orchid bark
-1/2 cup sand
-2 parts leaf litter... Leaf litter is a mix of Oak and Maple.
-1 cup of tree fern fiber (when I have some small pieces left over)
I sterilize all leaves by boiling for 5 minutes.
Over all I never have added charcoal and clay (just because I never have any on hand).
