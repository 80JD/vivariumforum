Personally I would highly recommend crested geckos. I had two until yesterday when I bought two more. They are very easy to care for the will require only 3 minutes of your time daily and depending on how you setup the inclosure 10 - 20 minutes for cleaning. They don't mind being held but they are jumpy so you have to becarful not to hold them up to high from the floor. I could go on and on about these beautiful reptiles. if you want to know more or have any question feel free to write. good luck
