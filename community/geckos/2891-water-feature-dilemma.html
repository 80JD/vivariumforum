The only thing that would concern me about your water feature would be humidity levels. From what I've read, CGs need about 50% humidity, which is supposedly best accomplished by misting twice a day so that they can drink the droplets of water and also so that things dry out within a few hours between mistings. It's possible that the waterfall will maintain too high a humidity.

Can you try it in another enclosure first? Build it, acclimate it, & test humidity levels prior to moving your CGs into the new build to ensure that the humidity doesn't go much above 50%.

An open screen top might help aerate the enclosure enough to reduce the humidity, but I'm not sure.

At any rate, if the humidity gets too high, IMHO you're risking skin problems and perhaps other problems as well.

everything i see suggests between 50 and 90 %. the big deal seems to be keeping the substrate dry most of the day and still misting 2x daily. i think you will need a divider for your land and water area, a mostly screen top and a small computer fan that can reach the substrate with air flow. misting will increase humidity to around 90% give or take wherever applied temporarily. to get the substrate dry 2x and remain dry for most of the day will take below 60 easily if not below 50 especially if not using a fan. imo... the smallest fans should be used and should not be blown on plants if possible. you cannot do a f/b to run water under this one imo... you need a divider!

A fan will also help cool the enclosure. I think that if you want to use water to cool the enclosure, you should make it so that a sheet of water pours down one wall. This will keep the glass cool, which will cool the enclosure. It will also raise the humidity considerably, but a full screen top should help with that. If you do that, you should probably have a fan pointing OUT of the enclosure, as well as one pointing in. This will blow warm, moist air out of the tank and pull fresh air into it. You can install the fans into the screen, with the one blowing out on the side where you have the water wall, and the one blowing in on the opposite side. When it is hot, it will be important that you do not use a light that will produce heat, so go with a fluorescent light (either tube or CFL).

22.5 degrees down and at one wall and one out of other side will provide enough across sustrate without drying everything else off. do this on parallel walls and water fall on adjacent so cooling affect of water can still be utilized without raising humidity in soil too much.

it doesn't have to be exact... just lay fan flat on wall and lift lower edge away from glass til it looks like half of 45 degrees... this will provide air under plants as it will circulate around the border. you can use a cooling controller if necessary.
