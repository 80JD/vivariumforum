What about Leopard Geckos?? They're pretty cheap, like $20 each or so, and are the perfect beginner-gecko.

My girlfriend just got two, one from Petco (male) and one from PetSmart (female) and they're doing quite well. They're very docile, not as fast as other geckos so if they get away from you it's not as hard to catch them, and conduct themselves like a dog or cat will. In fact, when they're ready to jump on a cricket they wag their tails back and forth, very cute!! Within 2 weeks they recognized my girlfriend's voice and respond to it.

If you get them young, and handle them quite a lot, they will bond to you just as a cat or dog will. They also come in a variety of colors/patterns. Most petstores carry them, I would suggest at least going and having a look before you buy something else.

Also, ask to hold anything before you buy it. Then you can really see the temperament of that particular lizard/animal. I know when we were picking out her first LG there was one in the PetCo tank that hissed whan it was touched. The rest (including the one she got) were very docile!!
