scorpions are really not much different than a bee sting in most cases... allergies ore more relevant to your reaction in most cases, although there are a few exceptions, but most exceptions cannot easily be obtained as pets unless you live where they thrive and collect them yourself... so usually someone who has one that can do damage is very aware of it due to living with and dealing with them locally... but i never really wanted to get stung by mine anyway
You would be amazed with how many scorpions are dangerous... may not take your life, but you won't having a good time.

And some of the most dangerous species in the world are readily available in the hobby... at quite reasonable prices.

Do some research on Centroides, Hottenetotta, Androctonus, Leirus, Rhopalurus, Lychas and Tityus sp., just to name a few... all of these are prevalent in the hobby, each of these amount to much more than a 'bee sting'. Many are also more than capable of taking your life, as well.
