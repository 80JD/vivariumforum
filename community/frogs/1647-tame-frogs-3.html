Although I have not tried to actual 'tame' my frogs, I have noticed that mine are way more comfortable around me than anyone else. In the past I have had WTF and FBT and they would also come out or make their 'chirping' noises more often when I was around, but would hide or become quiet of other people entered.

Currently I have an Caco-Ornate Horned Frog (Caco-Paco Man Toad) who is pretty lazy and content when I am around, but when other people get too close to him he does this head-bobbing action.

So far, in my case anyway, it appears to be familiarity. As for training Frogs, I would think that with the proper enviroment and practices you could get them to do certain things, but I wouldn't go as far as say they would be trainable as a dog.
