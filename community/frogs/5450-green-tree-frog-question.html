I've heard that Goldfish can be the absolute worst to feed a creature because it lacks anything of real nutrients. I had to feed my shark squid (frozen) or shrimp. Although I'm no expert, I would go with other forms of food than feeder fish.

If you are going to go purely for looks and not for food for the fish - the fancy guppies (the ones with the pretty tails) work well. The males have long pretty tails and the females don't. Also, neons would look sweet too. If you get a live plant then they also MIGHT feed on the plant.

Anyway, that is what I think, but there are people on here far more knowledgeable than myself. Hope that helps.
