Hi Paval,

An arachnologist friend who has spent his life working with spiders in the wild and captivity has written me to say that he is not aware of any problems associated with high calcium diets. Shedding troubles are generally related to humidity levels or (possibly) age.

As a footnote, I receive several of the prof. herp journals and am always surprised to see how many reports there are of various tarantulas and lg wold spiders consuming reptiles and amphibians. Just anecdotal, but it seems that many species take calcium rich food items in the wild. Wild caught inverts are often found to be far richer in calcium than trade species as well.

I'll let you know if anything new info comes in. Best, Frank
