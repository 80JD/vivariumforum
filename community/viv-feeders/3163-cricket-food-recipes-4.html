My new cricket food seems to be working well. Very few deaths. Still hard to tell though because there are alot of other factors holding my crickets back from their full potential, lol... I think I shall order a thousand or so and see if we can get this project going, hehehe. If and when I do that, I will be able to give mroe definite results on my new recipe than I can with 50 or less at a time. Once I see how it is actually doing, I will post the recipe.

One thing I will say though, if you go with drying your own greens to add to the mix (I definitely recomend it), use a dehydrator or the microwave, rather than the oven. The oven traps in too much of the moisture as it heats the leaves and it doesn't dry them the same way (and takes FAR longer). Carrots seem to be easiest if you grate them with the large side of a cheese grater and then dry them. If you are using greens with thick stems through the leaves, remove them, grate them, and dry them like the carrots and add that too. If you use a food dehydrator and don't have the liners for wetter foods, some window screen cut to size is perfect. If drying in the microwave, don't forget a couple of layers of dry paper towel underneath.
