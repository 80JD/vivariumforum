In an aged tank with lots of algae and good lights, you could keep tons of them. Just make sure there are plenty of plants, snails like to lay their eggs in them. Plus the dying leaves help feed the algae which feeds the snails, which feed your critters. Add fish food as needed to keep the algae growing.
