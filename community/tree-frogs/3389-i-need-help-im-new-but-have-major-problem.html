Hi I'm Lady_M sorry about the poor intro, but I have an emergency.
I work at a pet store, and I had a manager who was being transfered order a bunch of frogs knowing it was going to cause problems, well he ended up ordering a red eye tree frog and not a lot of people understand that it's delicate. So, I told my manager it's probably better if we had more than one??? I heard they like company but not too much???? Firsts off lose the spot light . A cool white florescent will be fine temps should stay around 75 deg and Humidity around 80 % constant. The temp is a little lower than normal because high temps and high humidity can lead to bacterial or fungal on the sore. Some pics of the sore would help a lot. as for the exact way to treat the sore on a Red eye I am unable to give you a 100% answer. I do know that many WC dart frogs develop nose rub, also an open sore and is treated with Silver Sulfadiazine or even neosporin. Both are topical creams.
I will try and find some more info for you..

Baytril has a reputation for being irritating... Using a long course of it on the skin or orally can cause problems.

If it was me, I would try some bactine or neosporine on the wound. While medicating with a salve I would remove the substrate, as anything "sticky" like that will attract dirt. Use damp paper towels in lieu of coco fiber or dirt.

I am not a vet, mind you, but I am a vet tech in training and I have dealt with gashes on horses so big they would make you pass out...

I would cover about 50% of the enclosure, plain old aluminum foil will work, and go from there. If you are still having humdity issues, cover a bit more, leaving some room for airflow.

Misting a couple of times a day can help, too. If the edges of that wound dry out too much.... Not good. I think an ointment is needed in this case.

Try and gently flush the wound with plain water, let it dry a little, and then apply the ointment.

Good luck! I am glad you have him, you seem to have a level head and are willing to do what the poor baby needs! Just please don't beat yourself up if it ends up being too much for him, you are doing excellent.
