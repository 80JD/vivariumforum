for the GTF you will only need the reptiglo 2 bulbs mine do fine with them.
if you cover the bottom with coco husk soil and keep it moist, the heat mat will warm it up and this heats the whole viv, as long as you have it in a warm room.
you will also be well off sticking an 18" repti glo 2.0 fluorescent behind the hood with a reflector clipped on.
my green tree frogs spend most of the day sat on a leaf right next to this.
don't bother faffing about with water, just put a small tupperware container about half a litre sunk into the soil. they never really go into the water. give them a few climbing plants and make sure you spray the tank with about 250ml of water every day, i do mine straight after the lights go out once per day for 4 days then i do it twice as often for the next 4 days. but only spray about 150 ml of water in the morning spray.
i work 4 on 4 off shifts so this works for me.
good luck on saturday give the taffs a thrashing at croke!
