Hi Grimm man i am sorry i hope ted is going to be ok!
That looks like it could be a prolapse either rectal, intestinal or colon.
It will eventually kill him if it is not fixed. It will get dried out or damaged with it hanging out in that manner.Is he getting calcium regularly? If not, he is most likely calcium deficient. He needs to have calcium with D3 dustings several times per week. Also, the feeders he gets should be fed & gutloaded prior to being fed to him.This happened probably due to him having to strain too hard when going to the bathroom. The GI tract operates off of calcium ions & when calcium levels fall too low, the smooth muscles cannot functino properly. What are the temps in the tank for him? It needs to be around 70-75 & no higher than 85. What is the humidity in the tank? It should run around 30-50% on average.
You may want to move to a sterile container with nothing it can catch it on and no crickets whatsoever.
Try bathing him in a dilute sugar solution, get some tupperware add about a centimeter worth of water and then a teaspoonful of sugar. This will at least keep it moist and if it is a prolapse this could help it reabsorb by itself. Do this for an hour or two at most and check him every 5-10 mins.
