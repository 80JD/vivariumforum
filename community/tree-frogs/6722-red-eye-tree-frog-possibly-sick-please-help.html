Sounds like you may have "force fed" your frog... I wouldn't do that until extraordinary circumstances are presented. You can do much physical damage; not to mention stress on your frog. Reptiles and Amphibians are cold blooded animals. They do not need to eat as often as we do.
Many animals sleep a little during the day and a little during the night and vice versa. I wouldn't be all that concerned with sleep patterns. Your frog will figure out what he is supposed to do.
Only having the tank for two weeks, the frog is still trying to acclimate to it's new set up. I can imagine you probably "harass" the frog frequently. They are nocturnal animals (sleep during the day). I can imagine you "poke and prob" him during the day constantly.
Your frog needs time to itself to adjust.
Keep the humidity at optimal range, keep the temperature at optimal range. Offer food every day of appropriate size(he may eat, he may not) Try not to "mess" with him. Ensure there are plenty of hiding places for him.
We really can only control those above listed things. Sometimes they just don't make it.
