New Vivarium 90% done
This is my first vivararium so please go easy on me, I built this for my children (maybe a lil bit for myself also) I am planning on housing tree frogs in it. I also plan on adding a nice taller branchy piece of driftwood in this tank. I think the Mopani wood REALLY makes planted tanks and vivariums pop due to its very earthy browns and tans. The 1 piece of wood in the tank is Mopani a very dense wood found in africa from the Mopane tree. My substrate is a 50/50 mix of jungle floor substrate and ground coconut husk soil. All the plants in my tank are live and I hope everything I put into this tank thrives. I am also considering taking a stab at a DIY 3d background but I am not 100% sure yet. I look forward to any RESPECTFUL critiques both good and not so good, I also look forward to any advice on decoration to add to the tank to make it more eye appealing and habitable.

I like it.
I have only put together one vivarium myself so I am no expert but there are a couple of things I might do differently if it were my own Vivarium.
1. I think that some taller plants would would look better. In particular on the right and left corners and not in the back. It would make the illusion that you are looking into some hidden place behind a couple of gate like borders, where-as a larger plant in the back with smaller ones up front tends to look more like a common display. Either way, I feel that it needs more use of the upper areas of the space that the tank provides.
2. I just don't like seeing electrical cords. i don't know what you can do about it. Maybe run them tightly down the back corner and cover them with a small piece of something painted the same color as the wall? I realize I am nitpicking now, but whenever I see a cord it just ruins a bit of the magic for me.
3. I don't think I would make a 3d background for this tank. I like seeing through the tank. Conventional wisdom says to put the tank in a place with the large curved face facing the audience and the rest into a corner. If you leave conventional thinking behind and the other sides open glass, there is no reason that those sides couldn't also be viewing angles. Just thinking out loud is all.

All in all a very nice and tidy looking tank, much better looking than my own.
