Red eye worry
hi peeps i got 3 red eye tree frogs at the end of jan and they were about 3 cm. 2 of them have now doubled in size but one has hardly grown...

i always feed a variety of sized crickets/hoppers so that there is something for everyone. he is very thin but seems to be healthy.

the one that has not grown i have moved to a 8inch x 3inch x 4inch exo terra plastic tub, so i can monitor his eating, i will count the crickets in then count in the morning. he must of been getting some food because i came home to a poop. on the floor i have put a damp paper towel so crickets cant hide and only decore is a smalll piece of thin plant at the top of one side and a branch corner to corner. i have drilled holes in the sides for airiation, and half covered the top with plastic to help with the humidity.

does any one know if females grow faster than males? i keep calling him a him but could be a girl...

feel bad taking him out his home but hoping it for the best, is this the right thing to have done?

Knighty, I would have done the exact same thing. Monitor how many crickets he eats. Then you'll know for sure if he's ok or not.

If you want him to put on little weight feed him cut earthworms. The cut pieces still wiggle so he'll notice them.

My RETFs do not like light at all, so I have to make sure they are awakened and ready to eat before I put the crickets in and I usually do it with a flashlight because it scares them when I turn on a normal light.

Maybe his bigger siblings are giving him too much competition.
