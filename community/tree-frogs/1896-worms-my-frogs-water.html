Worms In my Frogs Water
Okay so i am watching the pacific tree frogs hop around an eat fruit flys and crickets i notice these clear white worms in my frogs "pond" water.

I am fairly new to this whole naturalistic self sustaining terrarium thing - but i am trying. I have a 20 gal tall type set up - i have about 1qt pond down at one end - it has some river rocks that i got out of my yard and soaked and washed off really well and then some plants (bambooo and some tropical plant) to help filter the water... its really not deep or that much water so i am having trouble fiding a way to filter it and i just clean it out every couple weeks. the frogs tend to poop on the moss and plants and not in the water so it doesnt get that dirty - but they do go in the water alot, they hide down under a rock for a while and then hang out floating next to a rock... they seemto like it in there. So i was mildly disturbed to find these worms.

There seem to be two diff types one is REALLY thin almost microscopic if it were any thinner and clear, kind of whitish and about .5 -1.5 cm long.
the other ones are a little thicker and faster moving and about .5cm long. you can actualy tell where the head is on the fatter ones. The thin ones i thought were just some weird stringy dirt in the water at first.

both worms stay on the glass or rocks and dont swim at all, they will stay on a rock and eat stuff off the surface... or at least thats what i think they are doing.

and i just went back to look and there are little swimming white specs too... again almost microscopic... i know "wild" frogs (which mine were prior to them hopping onto my window and me catching them) swim in streams which have all kinds of fun water creatures - i just dont want them to be exposed to parasites and harmful stuff or concentrated populations of these things. reguardless even if they are not harmful i'm not to happy about worms in the water.

frustrating! i tried really hard to get this to be mostly self sufficient, naturalistic and clean and really would rather NOT go the water dish route.

sorry this is so long. any help would be appreciated and i just need toknow if i should evacuate the frogs permenantly.

Good. At least you didn't have to tear everything down. Me, I stay away from bleach because I find it hard to get rid of entirely. Suggestion: maybe use some aquarium water dechlorinator before adding your frogs back in there. For the next time (hopefully never) just dry out the water area completely and it should kill off the planar worms. Warm water also drowns worms. There's also a third way using dry ice.
