If you are planning on breeding them you will need a few things.

1. A misting/rain system set on timers throughout the day and 1-3 hours at night.
2. Large leaves overhanging a body of water
3. Multiple males to incourage competition

I havent tried breeding just yet but I plan to once I find a few more frogs. Ive herd they are pretty picky!

Yah its more nightime hours. I have done crazy amounts of research on mating. Ideally you want a large community of frogs in the same enclosure. Most commonly competition is a good thing but, it there has to be more more than one female in the tank to establish a pecking order. From what i have read online is 3 females and 9 males is a rather successful combo. Also atleast 6 inch deep water is required with overhanging broad leafs. the leaf must be big enough for both male and female to rest comfortably. One of the more interesting finds is that RETF breed on monsoon calendars. So a moon light cycle and increased raining is important. all this in consideration makes breeding a difficult task to top it off females dont put out like nickel and dime street walkers.
