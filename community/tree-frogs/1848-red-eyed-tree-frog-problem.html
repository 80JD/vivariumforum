I'd say it's pretty normal behavior for the frog to lunge at prey and overshoot a little. Without getting into a long-winded explanation I'd suggest taking pics of the frogs face if you get worried about it being injured and post on here. Several people on here and myself could let you know if it was serious enough to warrant putting on an antibacterial cream (like polysporin -without painkiller) to help the frog heal without it getting infected.
