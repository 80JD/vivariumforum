As for my experience, it seems that only bigger space that is in need and search of stabilizing of all the parameters of course. Of course, it is easier to watch and take care about a terrarium with one specie…But it is such a painful wish to make something as a tropical garden …
As for beginner or not…my opinion that we will never know if we do not try…but we must KNOW what we want to try to do. About what we CAN and SHOULD….Yes, it sounds logical, but how we can know what we should and what we shouldn’t. It must be based on an experience. Whose experience? I would not try to do something that was already done by lot’s of people and had a bad result, but terrarium hobby is not so old, there are not so many people who prefer to keep frogs and not so much information. So, I would and will and already try to have my own experience and then I would be able to answer the question about mixing frogs that I keep….but again, I would not be able to answer the question about other species….
For the moment I can assure that it is more difficult to keep a terrarium with different frogs, but it is very interesting. And it is very possible that myself I will say NO MIXING ANYMORE …who knows, I do not deny that.
