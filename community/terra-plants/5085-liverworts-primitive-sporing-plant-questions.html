I have species of both genera in terrariums. In general Liverworts like bright light and moist conditions. Riccia is a Liverwort. I also grw the common Marchantia polymorpha. It's often found growing in nursery plant pots. It grows very fast given good conditions. Lycopodium/Huperzia will also grow well in terrariums. Many are not happy if disturbed such as many native species. The tropical species do better. I have had great success with 2 small species, pinnifolia and salvinioides. Both are small and have done perfectly in a terrarium for 2 years now.

Most of those guys look pretty delicate. That means they haven't been growing in the most optimal conditions for moss, which is to be expected in this case. If you spread them out so that none of them are touching and get a fogger going to keep them constantly hydrated, I think they'll start getting more lush and spreading out fairly quickly.
