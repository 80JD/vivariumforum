I think you meant liverwort instead of lichen. I don't know any species of lichen that will live long in a terrarium. Wish that were not true. The key to liverwort is light and plenty of moisture. Daily watering is good. The amount of light it can take is a proportional to the amount of water it has. Lots of water, lots of light. It will grow quickly in these conditions. I grow it in a tray and it spreads fast.
