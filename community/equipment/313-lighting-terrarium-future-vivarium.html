Terrestial and aquatic plants are going to benefit most from bulbs in the 6500to 10,000k range. 6500 should give a more 'warm' light while 10,000 will be the bright white look. Anywhere in between is fine, just go with what looks good to you.

I think usually like 75% humidity and up for the frogs, just some plants may not like higher if the air is stagnant and they can't dry out periodically. Temp depends on the species, but I think 80 would be getting a little high, maybe more 70 to 74.

The temp can be maintained in different ways; I use an aquarium heater in the false bottom that heats the water in the waterfall to 74, though it helps the viv is in a room with 300+ gallons of 78 water. Sometimes the heat from fluorescent lights is enough, but you need to be carefull it doesn't dry the air too much. For any dedicated heat source I would recomend a temperature controler, not just plug in and forget like a heat lamp for a lizard.
